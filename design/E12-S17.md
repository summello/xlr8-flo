## Requirements (inlined)

- **SRC-013** — An award must record selected vendor, awarded lines, amount, rationale, evaluator results, exceptions, and approval history.
- **APR-003** — workflows support awards. **APR-020** — the panel is where a task is decided.

## Design

A variant of the shared decision panel (as E11-S15 does for vendors), with the evidence for
`document_type = "award"` between the key facts and the Decision Trail: the basis; the totals by
vendor; scope and funds per requisition with the amount to be released and returned (server
figures); not-lowest lines with their justifications; exceptions; and evaluators with their
declarations and weighted scores. Then `Open the full award`, linking to `/sourcing/rfqs/{evidence.rfq_id}/award` (the route E12-S16 adds). The panel builds that link from the evidence; the adapter's `summaries()` link (`sourcing/approval.py` ~59, RFQ detail) is **not** changed. **The footer is not touched** (E11-S10).

**Server change 1, the award evidence (Decisions 2).** `AwardAdapter.snapshot()` (`modules/sourcing/approval.py` ~38) stores
only `lines` and `rationale`, so `InstanceRead.evidence` is null for awards. Add `"evidence": json.dumps(AwardService(conn,
scope).evidence(document_id), sort_keys=True, default=str)` to its material, exactly as `modules/vendors/approval.py` ~92 does;
the engine already decodes it (`approvals/engine.py` ~882). `AwardService.evidence(award_id)` (new, `sourcing/awards.py`)
returns decimal strings only: `rfq_id` (the inbox `document_id` is the award id; the panel needs the RFQ id for every read below); `basis`; `currency`; `totals_by_vendor: [{vendor_id, vendor_name, budget_total, total}]` (`budget_total` is the primary figure; `total` is the full RFQ-currency amount, `amount_rfq` summed; names as the comparison
returns them, `sourcing/comparison.py` ~188); `requisitions: [{requisition_id, requisition, scope, award, over_scope_by, release, return_project}]` straight from the `checks` that `figures()` builds, `release` and `main_project` being the fields
E12-S16 adds to `RequisitionCheck` (`return_project` = `main_project`); `not_lowest: [{line_no, vendor_name, justification}]`;
`tie_overrides: [{line_no, vendor_name, tie_override_reason}]`; `exceptions` and `rationale` as stored; `evaluator_results` exactly as frozen at submission (`awards.py` ~742-753), the weighted scores the panel shows. Nothing is computed in
the browser. The evidence joins the material digest (as the vendor adapter's does), and `approvals/material.py` ~88 compares
`approved_material_digest` with a fresh snapshot, so the evidence holds **only figures that do not move when the award is
approved**: `funds` and `over_funds_by` are left out, because approval releases the remainder and changes them. The panel shows
those two from the live `GET /api/v1/sourcing/rfqs/{rfq_id}/award` `checks` (`rfq.view`, the award's read permission), matched
by `requisition_id`. If that award read fails (403/404, an approver without `rfq.view`), the funds columns show `Funds are not available to you` and the rest of the evidence still renders; the browser never derives funds itself.

**Server change 2, declarations for approvers (Decisions 1).** `EvaluationService.view` returns `evaluators` (with each
declaration) only `if can_edit` (`sourcing/evaluation.py` ~388). Widen it to `if can_edit or can_approve`; `get_evaluation`
(`api/sourcing.py` ~574) already passes both. Read-only: no write route changes. The panel reads declarations from `GET /api/v1/sourcing/rfqs/{rfq_id}/evaluation` and the weighted scores from `evidence.evaluator_results` (frozen, never the live recompute); a 403/404 there (an approver without `rfq.view`) shows
`Evaluator details are not available to you` in that fold and the rest of the evidence still renders.

## Decisions

**27 Oct 2026, operator (final)**, from the Codex pre-flight of 27 Oct (3 findings):

1. Approvers holding `rfq.approve` **may** see evaluators' conflict-of-interest declarations, read-only, in the Award Evidence block. The server read widens from `rfq.edit` to `rfq.edit` OR `rfq.approve` (`evaluation.py` ~388), with an authorization test per role.
2. The award snapshot carries the evidence payload (vendor names, per-requisition released/returned amounts and return project); that server change is in this story's scope.
3. E12-S16 is a dependency (`Open the full award` links to its route).

**28 Oct 2026, planner**, from the Codex pre-flight of 28 Oct (5 findings, all gaps, no rule decided): evidence gains `rfq_id` and per-requisition `requisition_id`; the full-award link is built from `evidence.rfq_id`, `summaries()` unchanged; an award-read failure degrades the funds columns like the evaluator fold (existing `rfq.view` gate unchanged); the fixture gains sourcing stubs.

## Decided

**28 Oct 2026, operator** (both blockers from the Codex re-run pre-flight, `DecisionPanel.tsx` Files gap fixed above):

1. **Vendor total** (amended 28 Oct by the operator, after the Codex pre-flight showed both figures are in RFQ currency, `awards.py` ~193-204). Each vendor shows `budget_total`, labelled `Budget`, as the primary figure. The full `total`, labelled `Total incl. tax & freight`, shows beside it **only when the two amounts differ**, which happens when `tax.consumes_budget` or `freight.consumes_budget` is off. Both figures carry the RFQ currency code (`evidence.currency`) and follow the money display rules (tabular figures, right-aligned). The browser compares the two decimal strings and computes nothing else.
2. **Scores.** The panel shows the **frozen** submission scores from `evidence.evaluator_results`, so the approver sees what the submitter saw. Declarations stay on the live read. A live recompute is never shown as evidence.

Pre-flight of 28 Oct 08:33 finding 2 (1440 and 375 are not tokens) is dismissed: they are axe test viewports, not layout values, and every story's UI acceptance uses them.
