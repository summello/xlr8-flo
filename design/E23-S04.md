## Reconciliation: nemotron's scorecard history does not match its configuration

The backlog asked why nemotron's history shows L-size and engine-kind work when `agents.yaml` allows neither. Answer, from `agents/scorecard.json` at `799797d`:

- All 10 nemotron records are early-October M1 stories (`E05-S01..S03, S10, S14`, `E06-S01..S03, S05, S06`). They were written **before** the 10 Oct 2026 config introduced `allowed_kinds`, `avoid_tags` and `max_author_size: M`. The history is not a violation of the config; the config postdates it.
- By the current rules only **5 of the 10** are stories nemotron would be assigned today (`E05-S01`, `E05-S02`, `E06-S01`, `E06-S05`, `E06-S06`: crud, size S or M, untagged). Four are size L (`E05-S03`, `E06-S02`, `E06-S03`, `E05-S10`), two are kind engine, two carry the `auth` tag (`E05-S10`, `E05-S14`).
- **Every one was reviewed by Opus alone** (`reviewers: []`, substitution reason "per-story review is Opus alone"). Nemotron's "100% clean, 1.00 rounds" was earned under the strictest review the fleet has run, on early foundation work.
- **`project-memory.md` is wrong in two places.** The 10 Oct entry says its "9/9 clean merges were all M or smaller", and the 11 Oct entry says "10, 100%, 1.00, 0 (all size M or smaller, which is why the cap stays)". Four of the ten are L.
- **Survivorship.** The one attempt under the current lane, `E06-S07` (ui, L, 10 Oct), exited with nothing committed and was re-authored by Codex, so it is in Codex's record, not nemotron's. Counting it, nemotron is 10 clean of 11 attempts. The scorecard holds no nemotron record after `E06-S06`.

**Consequence the planner must accept knowingly:** the reorder rests on 10 pre-policy records, 5 of which match today's lane, and zero records under the lane as configured. The case for it is cost and capacity (nemotron is free and has no worker cap; Sonnet draws a shared Claude window), not a demonstrated quality edge in this lane. State it that way in the memory entry.

## Design decisions (confirm on review)

1. **Nemotron has no concurrency cap.** `can_author` limits Sonnet by `max_workers_per_account` and the metered agents by budget; nemotron has neither. With it first, every untagged crud/ui/api/chore/docs/test story of size S or M goes to nemotron at once, up to `max_stories_in_flight: 6`, and Claude accounts can idle (the 21 Oct problem in reverse), with OpenRouter's rate limits as the only brake. Recommended: ship the reorder as scoped and watch with E23-S01's durations; do not add a new cap field in this story.
2. **Revisit trigger, written into the memory entry:** if any of nemotron's first three stories after the reorder needs a second round, or is re-authored by another agent, the planner restores the old order.
