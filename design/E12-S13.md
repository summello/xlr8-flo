## Requirements (inlined)

- **SRC-002** — An RFQ must contain items/services, quantities, specifications, delivery requirements, response deadline, terms, attachments, and invited vendors.
  *Ruled 25 Oct 2026 (operator):* publish refuses an RFQ whose `specifications`, `delivery_location`,
  `delivery_terms` or `terms` is null or blank (whitespace only). **Attachments stay optional.**
  **Backend change, in this story:** `RfqLifecycle._check_publishable` (`sourcing/lifecycle.py:112`)
  adds one `ProblemFieldError` per missing field, `field` = the column name, messages
  `Add the specifications.`, `Add the delivery location.`, `Add the delivery terms.`,
  `Add the terms.`, collected with the existing problems into the same 422 `cannot_publish` (every
  problem at once). Line specifications are not required. Update every test helper that publishes

  of `publish(`, and the e2e/seed paths that publish through the API) so they fill all four.

- **VEN-012** — ineligible vendors are not invited unless an authorized override.

## Screens

**`/sourcing/rfqs/:id/edit` (draft):** cards `Items and Specifications`, `Delivery, Deadline and
Terms` (deadline as a date field plus a 24-hour time field, entered in the RFQ's `deadline_tz` and shown
beside them as a one-option select, as `sourcing.md` §7.6 draws and `ExtendDeadlineDialog.tsx:10`
already does; reuse its conversion. Choosing another zone is not in this story), `Compliance
Requirements` (from a template; editable; `Waive` with a reason for the lead), `Attachments`
(visibility per file, `Internal` by default), and `Invited Vendors`. The invitee picker uses
`Eligibility variant="picker"` (E11-S11) for the RFQ's unit; a not-eligible vendor shows its reasons
and, **only for `rfq.approve` holders**, `Override` with a required reason. A sixth vendor is refused
in words (`Up to 5 vendors per RFQ. Remove one to invite <vendor>.`) even with an override.

**Picker API, built here (operator, 25 Oct 2026).** The vendor list (`GET /vendors`) needs
organization-wide `vendor.view` (`api/vendors.py:83`), which a unit-scoped RFQ editor may lack while
`POST /rfqs/{id}/invitees` lets them invite. Add
`GET /api/v1/sourcing/rfqs/{id}/invitee-candidates?q=&cursor=&limit=` on `sourcing_router`,
authorized by `rfq.edit` on the RFQ's unit (`rfq_target`, `api/sourcing.py:75`; foreign or unknown
RFQ → 404), draft only (409 `not_draft` otherwise). The sourcing service calls
`VendorService.search(q=, cursor=, limit=)` (`vendors/service.py:270`, server-filtered keyset pages,
limit 1..100, default 50) and `VendorService.eligibility(ids, unit, today())` for the page. Response
`InviteeCandidatePage {rows: [{vendor_id, trading_name, eligibility: Eligibility, invited: bool}],
next_cursor}`, nothing else from the vendor record (no contacts, tax, banking or documents). The
picker uses only this endpoint; it never calls `/vendors`. Invite stays `POST /rfqs/{id}/invitees`,
whose own eligibility and cap checks stay the authority.

`Publish RFQ` opens a confirmation listing what each invitee receives (by contact) and which files
stay internal; a 422 lists every problem as a focused summary. The summary renders every `errors[]` entry the
server returns by its message, generically, so a later problem (E12-S07's `weights_total`) needs no
change here.

**`/sourcing/rfqs/:id/amend` (published):** the same cards, but only what `RfqAmend`
(`modules/sourcing/schemas.py:318`) accepts is editable: the `RfqContentPatch` fields (specifications,
delivery location and terms, terms, deadline, line specifications) and line **quantities**.
The deadline may stay or move later, never earlier (`lifecycle.py:298`, 422 `deadline_earlier`, shown on
the field). A quantity may rise; lowering one on a `sourced` requisition is refused with 409
`requisition_sourced` (`lifecycle.py:348`). The server does not say which line, so the message shows in
the error summary and at the top of the lines card, input kept; the form does not pre-check it. `Requirements` and `Attachments` render read-only on amend, and so do
adding and removing vendors (`service.py:808`, `:893`, `:941`); an invitee's **contacts** stay
editable, saved per vendor through `PATCH /rfqs/{id}/invitees/{vendor_id}` (`service.py:1027`, allowed
while published), outside the amend submit and not part of `What Changed`. Plus `What Changed`, a required
`Reason`, and `Who Will Be Told` by contact (the current invitees). There is no server preview of the
diff (it is computed inside the amend transaction, `lifecycle.py:290`), so `What Changed` is computed
in the browser from the loaded `RfqRead` against the form: one row per changed field, the new value
in words and the old one struck as `was`. After the amend, the detail shows v2 from
`GET /rfqs/{id}/versions`, which is the record.

Submit and Save are never disabled; errors are field-level with a summary; input is kept.

## Acceptance
