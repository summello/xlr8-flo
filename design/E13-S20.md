## Requirements (inlined)

- **PO-003** — POs must follow a configurable approval workflow before issue.
- **APR-020** — the panel is where a task is decided.

## Design

A decision-panel variant for `document_type = "po"` (as E11-S15 and E12-S17): the source award,
the budget effect when issued (one row per project: project number and name, then `Money`), vendor
eligibility **now** (the `Eligibility` component; a vendor suspended after award shows it, and
Approve stays enabled, DECISIONS 52), the award justification lines, and what will be sent. Then
`Open the full purchase order`. The footer is not touched.

**Server contract (pre-flight 28 Oct)** — `purchasing/service.py::PurchaseOrderService.evidence`
today returns `budget_effect` rows of `project_id`/`currency`/`amount` and a reduced
`vendor_eligibility_now`. This story changes it, and only it:
- each `budget_effect` row gains `project_number` and `project_name` (read through
  `flo.modules.projects.service.ProjectService.get`, once per distinct project on the PO, never
  `projects.models`; a `ponytail:` comment names the ceiling — a PO spans few projects);
- `vendor_eligibility_now` becomes the full `vendors.schemas.Eligibility` (`model_dump(mode="json")`:
  `eligible`, `reasons[]` and `warnings[]` of `{code, text, document_type, date}`), so the web
  passes it to `<Eligibility variant="field">` unchanged;
- regenerate the client if the approval-evidence schema is typed.

**What will be sent:** recipients only (`evidence.recipients`). The vendor-visible items are E13-S11's
and join when it lands; render nothing for them here and record that in `notes.followup`.

**Wiring:** in `DecisionPanel.tsx`, mount `<PurchaseOrderEvidence>` when
`row.document_type === "po" && instance?.evidence` (beside the vendor and award branches), and make the
link label say `purchase order` for `po`.
