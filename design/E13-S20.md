## Requirements (inlined)

## Design

A decision-panel variant for `document_type = "po"` (as E11-S15 and E12-S17): the source award,
the budget effect when issued (server sentence per project), vendor eligibility **now** (the
`Eligibility` component; a vendor suspended after award shows it, and Approve stays enabled,
DECISIONS 52), the award justification lines, and what will be sent. Then `Open the full PO`.
The footer is not touched.
