## Design

`/purchase-orders/:id/issue`: a confirmation that lists the recipient, version and template, **what
will be sent and what stays here** (from `GET …/vendor-copy`), and the commitment (server sentence).
`Issue` sends one request with an Idempotency-Key generated **once per confirmation open**, so a
double click or retry returns the same result. The **moment** is as MASTER §7.16: the document lifts
on `--shadow-drag`, the seal marks it, and a trace runs to the vendor row as the email is queued. On
a transmission failure the trace snaps back in danger and `Resend` is already focused. It plays once
per session (a session flag). With `prefers-reduced-motion`, it shows the finished picture with a
note. After it ends, the page shows the result in words and a small table (recipient, version,
result).
