# E12-S04 — Bid capture: keyed by the buyer, in the vendor's currency, never altered

**Epic** E12 · **M3** · **crud/M** · after **E12-S03** · tags: `money` (Opus review)

> **Conventions:** `docs/m1-conventions.md`, `docs/m2-conventions.md`, `docs/author-traps.md`.
> **Decisions:** canvas DECISIONS 145 and 155 (the vendor's sent time, evidenced, decides lateness;
> `Keyed By Us` never changes); no vendor portal in v1. `design-system/pages/sourcing.md` §2 "Bids are
> keyed by the buyer", "The original bid is never altered", §4.5. Lateness enforcement is E12-S05.
> Back end only; UI is E12-S14.
> **Writes:** every `PATCH`/`PUT`/`DELETE` on an editable resource follows `docs/m2-conventions.md` §O (`If-Match` with the integer version, `ETag` on reads, 400 when missing, 409 `stale_version` on mismatch). `Idempotency-Key` protects **POST** commands only (the middleware ignores other methods); where a packet says "(Idempotency-Key)" on a non-POST route, read §O instead.

## Requirements (inlined)

- **SRC-006** — Vendor responses must capture line prices, currency, tax, freight, lead time, validity, exceptions, attachments, and submission timestamp.
- **SRC-010** — Currency conversion used for comparison must show rate, source, and effective date and must not alter the vendor's original bid.

## Data

`bid` (RLS): `id`, `org_id`, `rfq_id`, `vendor_id` (must be an invitee), `rfq_version int` (the
version answered), `currency char(3)` (the vendor's; validated by `org.fx.validate_currency`),
`sent_at timestamptz NOT NULL` (entered by the buyer), `evidence_attachment_id uuid NOT NULL` (the
vendor's email or file, an attachment on the `rfq`), `keyed_by`, `keyed_at timestamptz NOT NULL
DEFAULT now()`, `freight NUMERIC(18,4) ≥ 0`, `lead_time_days int ≥ 0`, `valid_until date`,
`delivery_terms text`, `warranty text`, `exceptions text`, `status` CHECK in `draft` and
`submitted`. One `submitted` bid per (rfq, vendor) (partial unique index). A corrected bid is a
**new** bid that sets `replaces bid_id`; the first is kept and shown as replaced.

`bid_line`: `bid_id`, `rfq_line_id`, `quoted bool` (`Not Quoted` = false), `unit_price NUMERIC(18,4)
≥ 0` (required iff quoted), `tax NUMERIC(18,4) ≥ 0` (amount for the line), and `line_total` =
`unit_price × rfq_line.quantity` computed by the server and quantized through `kernel/money.quantize`
in the bid currency. `bid_requirement`: `bid_id`, `requirement_id`, `met bool`, `note`.

**Append-only after submit:** a submitted bid and its lines are immutable (the append-only trigger
set). A draft bid may be edited by its keyer.

**Never converted here.** No column in these tables holds another currency (SRC-010). The comparison
(E12-S06) converts.

## API (`/api/v1/sourcing/rfqs/{id}/bids`)

| Method and path | Permission | Behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| `POST /` (Idempotency-Key) | `rfq.edit` | Creates a draft for an invitee. 409 if the RFQ is not `published` or `closed`. |
| `PATCH /{bid_id}` (Idempotency-Key) | `rfq.edit` | Draft only. |
| `POST /{bid_id}/submit` (Idempotency-Key) | `rfq.edit` | Validates every RFQ line has a row, quoted lines have a price, and every requirement is answered (422 per field). **Lateness check is E12-S05's hook:** call `sealing.check_late(bid)` here, a no-op until E12-S05 lands. |
| `POST /{bid_id}/replace` (Idempotency-Key) | `rfq.edit` | A new draft copied from a submitted bid. |
| `GET /` | as E12-S05 decides | E12-S05 owns who may read figures; until then, only the bid's keyer and holders of `rfq.approve`. |

Audited: `bid.create`, `bid.submit`, `bid.replace`, without figures in the audit `after` (figures
stay in the bid tables, which are sealed until the deadline).
