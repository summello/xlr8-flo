## Requirements (inlined)

- **SRC-011** — Evaluators must be able to score qualitative and quantitative criteria with comments and conflicts-of-interest declarations where configured.

## Screen `/sourcing/rfqs/:id/evaluation`

1. **Declaration first:** until the evaluator declares, the page shows only the declaration form ("Do you have a conflict of interest with any invited vendor?" yes/no; detail required on yes). No bid content is requested or rendered before it.
2. A conflict → a recused state ("You are recused from this RFQ. The RFQ owner has been told.").
3. Before the deadline → `Scoring Opens at the Deadline`, with the server's time in the user's locale.
4. Then a fold per vendor: a score (0..10, the range shown beside the field) and a comment per criterion, weights read-only; `Save for Later` and `Submit Scores` (all or nothing; field errors and a summary on failure).
5. After submitting, the evaluator sees their own scores read-only, and everyone's once every non-recused evaluator has submitted (server-driven).
