# E23-S10 — Completion-triggered supervisor wakeup instead of a fixed 300 s poll

**Epic** E23 · **MT** · **chore/S** · tags: none · backlog item T11 (optional, lowest priority) · after **E23-S01** · **BLOCKED** (status `blocked` in `agents/roadmap.yaml`)

> MT stories ship by a pull request to `main`, as E21-S01 did (PR #18).
> **Benefit is unmeasured.** The backlog says do not ship this on faith. Read "Gate" below first.

## Requirement (inlined)

- **SUP-005** — Administrator and operator documentation must be updated in the same PR as the feature it describes (UX-010).

## Why this story is blocked

1. **The supervisor is not in this repository.** The tick loop, the `interval` handling and the locking that prevents duplicate launches are in `~/.claude/fleet/fleet.py` (see E23-S07 for the full finding and the operator decision it needs). `agents/fleet.toml` holds only `interval = 300` and the step commands. A "completion-triggered wakeup" is a change to the loop, not to config.
2. **There is no number to beat.** Dead time between stages is the claim. No record carries a stage duration until E23-S01 lands and has run for a few stories. Without it the saving cannot be shown, and the risk (a faster trigger launching a step twice) is real.

## What can be said now

- In-repo, `author.sh`, `review.sh`, `submit.sh` and `finish.sh` already write a log ending in `exit N` and a `.pid` file; the supervisor and `flo facts` read those. A completion signal could be one more file, for example `.scratch/wake`, touched by those scripts on exit. Whether the supervisor can watch it is a supervisor question.
- Shortening `interval` is a one-line change in `fleet.toml` and needs no loop change, but the supervisor's per-tick work (reading `flo facts`, running limit checks) may not tolerate a short interval. Unknown from here.
- Whatever is built must keep the concurrency caps (`max_stories_in_flight`, `max_parallel_checks`, `max_workers_per_account`) and the per-story locking intact. E23-S06's `mkdir` story lock is the in-repo guard against a double launch of the author step.

## Spec for when it is unblocked

- Add the completion signal in the four scripts; the supervisor consumes it or `interval` is reduced, per the operator's decision.
- Acceptance: no duplicate launch under a burst of completions (a planted burst of ten completion events launches each step once); caps held; before/after median stage-to-stage gap reported from E23-S01 data.
- Plant: remove the per-story lock and show the burst test launches twice.
