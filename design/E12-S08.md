# E12-S08 — Award: split by line and quantity, justification, scope and funds guard, release of the unused reservation

**Epic** E12 · **M3** · **ledger/L** · after **E12-S07** · tags: `money`, `concurrency` (Opus review)

> **Conventions:** `docs/m1-conventions.md`, `docs/m2-conventions.md`, `docs/author-traps.md`.
> **Decisions:** canvas DECISIONS **204** (no published award workflow → 409, supersedes 147),
> **205** (the unused reservation is released once at award approval and returned to the main
> project in the same transaction), **206** (requisition `awarded` and `open_for_purchase` together
> when every line is fully awarded), 150 (non-lowest justification is an org setting, on by default),
> 115 (ties: the earlier submission wins; a buyer override needs a reason), 6j (vendor currency at the
> award's stored rate). D-M2-19/26 (the requisition's **sub-project** holds the reservation),
> D-M2-21, D-M2-25 (expired codes block via `assert_current_upstream`). E09-S16 `reduce_to`.
> Back end only; UI is E12-S16 (draft) and E12-S17 (decision panel).

## Requirements (inlined)

- **SRC-012** — Awards may be split by line or quantity across vendors.
- **SRC-013** — An award must record selected vendor, awarded lines, amount, rationale, evaluator results, exceptions, and approval history.
- **SRC-014** — A non-lowest-price award must require an explicit justification when configured.
- **SRC-016** — Award totals must not exceed approved requisition scope or available reserved funds without an approved amendment.
- **REQ-014** — Split sourcing and partial awards must be supported without exceeding approved quantity or amount.
- **FIN-011** — a release happens exactly once.

## Data

`award` (RLS): `id`, `org_id`, `number` (`org.numbering` doc type `"award"`), `rfq_id`, `basis`
CHECK in `lowest_cost_supplier`, `best_average_cost` and `manual`, `status` CHECK in exactly `draft`,
`approval_pending`, `approved`, `superseded` and `cancelled` (MASTER §2.3), `rationale text`,
`rates jsonb` (frozen from the comparison at award creation: per vendor currency, the rate, source and
effective date), `evaluator_results jsonb` (from E12-S07 at submit), `exceptions jsonb`,
`created_by`, `submitted_at`, `approved_at`. One non-terminal award per RFQ (partial unique index).

`award_line`: `award_id`, `rfq_line_id`, `vendor_id`, `bid_line_id`, `quantity NUMERIC(18,4) > 0`,
`unit_price` and `currency` (the vendor's, copied from the bid line), `amount_vendor` (=
`unit_price × quantity` plus its tax and freight share, quantized in the vendor currency),
`amount_rfq` (converted at the frozen rate, quantized in the RFQ currency),
`not_lowest bool`, `justification text NULL`, `tie_override_reason text NULL`.

## Rules

- **Split (SRC-012, REQ-014):** a line may go to several vendors by quantity. For each RFQ line,
  Σ award_line.quantity ≤ rfq_line.quantity (422 per line). A vendor must have quoted that line.
- **Non-lowest (SRC-014):** `not_lowest` is set by the server against E12-S06's per-line lowest.
  When the org setting `sourcing.require_non_lowest_justification` (new, bool, default **true**) is
  on, every `not_lowest` line needs a justification (1..500) at submit, or 422 per line.
- **Budget amount (decision 211):** an award line's *budget amount* is its price, plus its tax when
  the org setting `tax.consumes_budget` is on for the RFQ's unit, plus its freight share when
  `freight.consumes_budget` is on (both new settings in `modules/org/settings.py`, bool, default
  **true**, resolved through the nearest-unit override). The scope and funds checks and the release
  below use budget amounts; the comparison (E12-S06) still shows full landed cost.
- **Scope and funds (SRC-016),** checked at submit **and** again at approval, under the locks:
  for each requisition on the award, `award amount for its lines` (Σ budget amounts, in the RFQ currency) must not exceed
  either **(a) scope**, the approved amount for those lines pro rata by quantity (E12-S06's formula),
  or **(b) funds**, the live reservation remaining on its sub-project. Over either → 409
  `over_scope` with `checks` = `{requisition, scope, funds, award, over_by}`. The way out is to
  change the selection or amend the requisition (pages/sourcing.md §2). Nothing is disabled.
- **Submit** (`rfq.approve` on the unit; the RFQ must be `under_evaluation`):
  1. `require_workflow_for(conn, scope, "award", award_id, "awards")`, or 409 `workflow_not_set_up`. **Never auto-approve** (decision 204).
  2. `assert_current_upstream` for every requisition (a stale version, a pending amendment or expired codes → 409).
  3. The checks above.
  4. Status `approval_pending`, and start the approval.
  Register the `award` `DocumentAdapter` (`approve_permission = "rfq.approve"`, target the RFQ's unit).
- **On approval** (`on_outcome`, **inside the decision transaction**; lock order: every
  `project_balance` involved in id order, then the requisitions in id order, then the award):
  1. Re-run the scope and funds check (409 rolls back the decision).
  2. `requisitions.service.award_lines(conn, scope, [(requisition_line_id, qty)], actor)`: `awarded_qty += qty` (the CHECK `awarded_qty ≤ quantity` is the backstop).
  3. **Release and return (decision 205),** per requisition: `unused = scope of the awarded lines − award amount` (≥ 0). If `unused > 0`, call a new `requisitions.service.release_unawarded(conn, scope, requisition_id, unused, award_id, actor)`. It calls `funding.reduce_to(new_amount = remaining − unused)` and then transfers the released amount **sub-project → main project** with `post_legs` (idempotency key `award:{award_id}:return:{requisition_id}`). Exactly once: a retried decision posts nothing new (FIN-011).
  4. Award `approved`; RFQ `awarded`.
  5. **Requisition status (decision 206):** if every non-cancelled line now has `awarded_qty = quantity − cancelled_qty`, move it `sourced → awarded → open_for_purchase` in this transaction (two audited transitions) via `requisitions.service.mark_awarded`.
- **On rejection:** the award returns to `draft` (the award vocabulary has no `rejected`; the approval history keeps the rejection). Nothing is posted.
- Register the `award` link-graph node (with `access`).

## API (`/api/v1/sourcing/rfqs/{id}/award`)

`POST /` (draft from a basis: `{basis}` copies E12-S06's selection; `manual` starts empty),
`PUT /lines`, `PATCH /` (rationale), `GET /` (with live checks and figures), `POST /submit`, and
`POST /withdraw` (approval_pending → draft). All take Idempotency-Key and are audited.

## Acceptance

- [ ] Migrations up, down and data preservation.
- [ ] Split: one line to two vendors by quantity; over the RFQ quantity → 422.
- [ ] Justification: required per not-lowest line when the setting is on; not required when off.
- [ ] No published award workflow → 409 on submit; nothing written.
- [ ] Over scope and over funds each → 409 with the figures.
- [ ] **Approval posts:** a hand-computed example where the award is 900 against a scope of 1,000. The ledger shows one release of 100 on the sub-project and one transfer of 100 sub → main; the sub-project reservation remaining is reduced by 100; **drift is empty** (`budget/reconcile`). Approve twice (a retried decision) → no second release or transfer.
- [ ] **Concurrency (real Postgres):** two awards for the same RFQ lines approved at once → exactly one succeeds (the other fails the awarded-quantity check under the lock), and the ledger has one release.
- [ ] A requisition fully awarded → `open_for_purchase` (via `awarded`); a partly awarded one stays `sourced`.
- [ ] Rejection → draft, and no ledger entry.
- [ ] TEN-010 and authorization per role (a hidden Submit is refused by the server).
- [ ] `flo check` green.

## Plants

1. Release before locking the balance: the concurrency test sees two releases.
2. Skip the transfer back: the "returned to the main project" assertion fails.
3. Auto-approve when no workflow is published: the 409 test fails.
