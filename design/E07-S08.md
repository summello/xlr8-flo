# E07-S08 — Cross-hierarchy transfer up-then-down through both ancestries, one transaction

**Epic** E07 · **M1** · **ledger/L** · after **E07-S07**, **E06-S02**, **E06-S04** · tags: `money`, `concurrency`

> **Conventions:** read `docs/m1-conventions.md` (authorization outcomes, scope targets, wire formats). It applies unless this packet says otherwise.

## Requirements

- **BUD-003** — A cross-hierarchy transfer must flow upward through the giver’s ancestors and downward through the recipient’s ancestors.
- **BUD-004** — Entries generated by one transfer must commit atomically and share one transfer identifier.
- **WF-003** — The document transition and its financial entries must commit atomically.

## Design decisions (confirm on review)

- **D-M1-13 Funding modes decide where money lives, and therefore what a transfer posts.** (Funding modes are defined in E06-S04; this story consumes the effective `funding_mode` setting at the transfer's lowest common ancestor.)
  - **Roll-down:** funds are *held at nodes*. A parent holds a pool; a child's allocation is a transfer *from* its parent. A cross-hierarchy transfer therefore moves money hop by hop along the tree path: **up** from the giver through each of its ancestors to the lowest common ancestor (LCA), then **down** through each of the recipient's ancestors to the recipient (BUD-003). Every hop is one balanced pair of `transfer` legs (`allocated`, `−X` out of the lower node, `+X` into the upper node, or the reverse on the way down). Intermediate nodes and the LCA therefore receive and pass on the same amount: their net `allocated` change is zero, but the ledger shows the full path.
  - **Roll-up:** a parent's budget is the **aggregate of its descendants computed at read time** (PROJ-008); funds are held at the nodes that were allocated directly. Posting ancestor legs would count the same money twice, so a cross-hierarchy transfer posts **only the two leaf legs** (giver `−X`, recipient `+X`); every ancestor's aggregate moves automatically.
  - Same-parent siblings are a single hop in either mode (E07-S07's path).
- The same-level restriction from E07-S07 is lifted: any two eligible projects in one org may transfer (same currency, both `Draft` or `Active`, D-M1-11).

## Contract

Extends `POST /api/v1/budget/transfers` from E07-S07 (same request body and response). The service picks the path from hierarchy position and funding mode. The response `entries` lists every leg in posting order. Hierarchy helpers come from E06-S02 (`projects.service.path_between(a, b) -> (up: [ids from a to LCA exclusive], lca, down: [ids from LCA exclusive to b])`) imported through `service` only.

## Behaviour

1. Compute the path and mode first, then lock **every** `project_balance` row on the path `FOR UPDATE` in ascending `project_id` order in one statement, before any validation (AGENTS.md 3.1).
2. Validate: the giver's `available - X >= 0` unless `allow_negative_budget` (BUD-008). In roll-down mode each hop's lower node on the way up must hold at least X of `available`; because every intermediate receives X before passing it on, validation runs on the **running balances in leg order** (simulate the legs on the locked values, then post).
3. Post every leg through `post_entry` with one shared `transfer_group_id`; assert the group sums to exactly zero before commit and abort with `TransferInvariantViolation` if not.
4. Idempotency, audit, evidence and permissions as in E07-S07. `budget.transfer` is required on the **giver and the recipient** only, not on intermediate ancestors.
5. A single transfer is never partially applied: any failure on any leg rolls back the whole group.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Roll-down, cousins under different parents (path G → P1 → LCA → P2 → R): posts the hop pairs, every intermediate node's net `allocated` change is zero, giver `−X`, recipient `+X`, group sums to zero
- [ ] Roll-down, five levels deep on both sides: correct hop count (4 up-hops, 4 down-hops), one transaction
- [ ] Roll-up, same cousins: exactly two legs, and the aggregate of each ancestor (computed with E06-S04's subtree aggregate) moves by `−X` / `+X` with no duplication
- [ ] Roll-down transfer larger than the giver's available is rejected with nothing written
- [ ] A failure injected on the last leg leaves zero rows and unchanged balances for every node on the path
- [ ] 50 concurrent mixed transfers over a small tree (seeded RNG, both modes) complete without deadlock; for every project `project_balance` equals `SUM(amount) GROUP BY bucket`; the sum of all `allocated` across the tree is invariant
- [ ] Siblings under one parent post exactly two legs in both modes
- [ ] Giver or recipient outside the caller's granted scope → 403; foreign tenant → 404
- [ ] Replayed `Idempotency-Key` → same group id, no new rows

## Amendments (preflight audit — authoritative, override anything above)

- **Funding mode source (replaces "at the LCA"):** the mode is the effective `funding_mode` setting at the **root ancestor's** BU/OU of the tree (one mode per tree). This story adds the minimal reader `funding_policy.mode_for(project_id)` (using `org.service.effective_setting` and the root from `projects.service`); E06-S04 later extends that module.
- **Eligibility:** both projects must be in the **same root tree**. Two projects with no common ancestor are a 409 `TransferNotEligible` (label `different_trees`); there is no cross-root path in M1.
- **Acceptance moved:** the criterion that uses "E06-S04's subtree aggregate" is verified in E06-S04's tests. Here, assert roll-up behavior by checking that exactly two leaf legs post and that the sum of `allocated` across the tree is unchanged.

- **Shared leg-posting helper (from the E06-S04 review).** `transfers.transfer` (E07-S07) and the roll-down child allocation in `budget/service.py` (E06-S04) duplicate about 50 lines of leg posting, evidence insertion and replay checking. Extract ONE helper in `modules/budget/transfers.py` that posts a list of legs under a transfer group with the `<key>:out` / `<key>:in` (and numbered hop keys here) idempotency convention, handles `ledger_evidence` and the replay mismatch check, and make all three callers (sibling transfer, roll-down allocation, this story's multi-hop path) use it. Behaviour of the first two must not change: their existing tests stay green unmodified. Plant-verify by breaking the replay check in the helper and watching tests of all three callers fail.
- **Revision id** (if a migration is needed, none expected): follow the corrected convention in `docs/m1-conventions.md`.

- **Eligibility precedence (decision, resolves the E07-S07 contradiction).** Evaluate in this order and nothing else: (1) the E07-S07 sibling rule first: equal depth and the same `parent_id`, **including two root projects (both parents NULL)**, takes the unchanged E07-S07 single-hop path with its unchanged eligibility (giver any status, receiving `draft` or `active`, same currency); every existing sibling-transfer test stays green unmodified, including `test_status_guard` and the tests that transfer between two separate roots. (2) Otherwise, if both projects are in the **same root tree**, this story's multi-hop path applies: the giving project may be in any status (residual funds may leave a project, as E07-S07 and `assert_posting_allowed` already allow), the receiving project must be `draft` or `active`, and every **intermediate node that receives a leg** (roll-down hops) must be `draft` or `active` too, reported before posting as 409 `TransferNotEligible` naming the node and its status. Same currency throughout. (3) Otherwise (not siblings, no common root) -> 409 `TransferNotEligible`, label `different_trees`. The sentence in the Design decisions "both `Draft` or `Active`" is superseded by (1) and (2). No existing test is to be modified.
