# E09-S06 — Approval generates the sub-project, and reserves for standalone requests, in one transaction

**Epic** E09 · **M2** · **ledger/L** · after **E09-S05**, **E07-S04**, **E10-S05** · tags: `money`

> **Conventions:** `docs/m1-conventions.md`, `docs/m2-conventions.md` (H: `on_outcome` runs inside the decision's transaction; L: funding rounds) and `docs/author-traps.md`. Decisions: **D-M2-15** (a project-linked requisition already reserved at submit), **D-M2-5** (standalone), D-M2-7, D-M2-12. Read `design/E10-S05.md` "Contract" for `OutcomeContext` (`actor_id`, `decision_id`, `assign_project_id`, `permitted`, `reject_mode`) and `design/E09-S05.md` for the adapter and the release helper this story builds on.

## Requirements

- **REQ-002** — Approval of a project-linked requisition must create a requisition-generated sub-project and reserve its approved amount against the parent. *(D-M2-15: the reservation was made against the parent at submit; approval confirms it and generates the sub-project.)*
- **REQ-003** — A requisition must not be created against a sub-project that was itself generated by a requisition. *(This story sets the marker that E09-S01's guard reads.)*
- **REQ-004** — Approval of a standalone requisition must create a project unless an authorized approver assigns it to an existing project.
- **PROJ-003** — Project consumption must be triggered by approved requisition reservations and/or PO commitments.
- **PROJ-004** — Sub-projects must be creatable manually or automatically from a requisition.
- **ACC-001** — Two concurrent requisitions cannot reserve more than the same project's available budget.
- **WF-003** — The document transition and its financial entries must commit atomically.
- **D-M2-5** — A standalone requisition's approval creates a new top-level project and allocates the approved amount to it as new budget, then reserves it; the final approver must hold `budget.allocate` on its BU/OU. An approver may instead assign an existing project, which then funds it like a project-linked requisition.

## Design decisions (this packet)

- **The generated sub-project is born `active`.** The requisition's approval is its approval, so it skips `draft` (PROJ-002: it activates when no further approval applies). Fields: parent = the requisition's project, BU/OU = the parent's, `name` = the requisition title, `description` = "Created from REQ-00431 on approval.", department and ledger account = the requisition's, currency = the parent's, owner = the requester, `planned_end` = `needed_by`, `generated_by_requisition_id` = the requisition. **It holds no allocation and no reservation**: the reservation stays on the parent (REQ-002 "against the parent"), and M3's commitments are the parent's to convert. Record the M3 question in `notes.followup`: does a PO commit against the parent or the sub-project?
- **Standalone with a new project:** a top-level project in the requisition's BU/OU and the base currency, born `active`, **not** marked generated (it may take further requisitions). Then `budget.service.allocate` for the estimate (Direct mode, since it has no parent), with `evidence_ref = "requisition:REQ-00431"` so an `budget.evidence_required` BU is satisfied, then `reserve` round 1. Requisition-generated sub-project: none (the new project *is* the requisition's project). `requisition.project_id` is set and `standalone` stays `true`.
- **Standalone assigned to an existing project** (`context.assign_project_id`): treated exactly like a project-linked approval, against that project: lock its funding row, validate (active, not generated, depth < 5, same currency, funds), reserve round 1, then generate the sub-project under it. **Who may assign (confirm on review):** the final approver must hold `requisition.approve` on the assigned project's scope, checked with `context.permitted("requisition.approve", project_target)`; otherwise 403. This reads REQ-004's "authorized approver" as "an approver for the project it lands in". It is recorded for the operator in `notes.followup` and is not guessed beyond that.
- **A project-linked requisition with `assign_project_id` set** → 422 on `assign_project_id`: "Only a standalone request can be assigned to a project."
- **Decision 99 versus D-M2-5 (open, in `docs/m2-decisions.md` Follow-ups):** implement D-M2-5 as written (Direct allocation, no organization-pool check) and copy the follow-up into `notes.followup`.

## Contract

No new route. `RequisitionAdapter.on_outcome(APPROVED, context)` (E09-S05's 409 placeholder is replaced) and two service functions:

- `projects.service.create_generated_project(conn, scope, *, settings, actor_id, parent_id: UUID | None, bu_id, name, description, department_code, ledger_account_code, currency, owner_id, planned_end, generated_by_requisition_id: UUID | None) -> ProjectRead`. It reuses `ProjectService.create`'s insert, numbering, balance row and `register_project_scope` path (extract the shared body; do not copy it), sets `status = 'active'` and the marker, and audits `project.create` with `source = "requisition"`. Authorized edit to `modules/projects/service.py`.
- `projects.service.generated_for(conn, scope, requisition_id) -> ProjectRef | None`, which E09-S09 uses.

## Behaviour (all inside the decision's transaction; any exception rolls the decision back, convention H)

1. **Project-linked** (and assigned-standalone after its reserve, step 3):
   1. Lock `project_balance` of the requisition's project `FOR UPDATE`, then the requisition row (the balance-first order).
   2. Re-validate what can have changed since submit: the parent is `active` (else 409 `project_not_active`: "Plant 4 Line Retrofit is deferred. Reactivate it, then approve."); the live reservation's remaining equals the estimate (else 409 `reservation_mismatch`, an invariant breach that is logged at error level).
   3. `create_generated_project(parent_id = project_id, …)`. Depth is guaranteed by submission (D-M2-12). If the depth CHECK still fires, answer 409 `project_depth_exceeded`, which only a direct data change can cause.
   4. Requisition: `status = 'sourcing_in_progress'`, `decided_at`, `version + 1`. Status event (with the decision id), audit `requisition.approve` (with the sub-project id).
2. **Standalone, new project** (`assign_project_id is None`):
   1. `context.permitted("budget.allocate", AuthorizationTarget(ScopeType.BU, bu_id))` else 403: "Approving a standalone request creates budget, which needs Budget Allocate on Manufacturing. Ask someone who holds it to approve, or assign an existing project."
   2. `create_generated_project(parent_id=None, generated_by_requisition_id=None, …)`.
   3. `budget.service.allocate(conn, scope, project_id, AllocationCreate(amount=estimate, currency, effective_date=today, reason="Standalone requisition REQ-00431 approved", evidence_ref="requisition:REQ-00431"), actor_id, idempotency_key=f"req:{id}:allocate:1")`.
   4. Increment `funding_round` to 1 and `reserve(…, idempotency_key=f"req:{id}:reserve:1")`. Set `project_id` and `reservation_entry_id`.
   5. Status `sourcing_in_progress`, `decided_at`, event, audit (with the new project id and both ledger entry ids).
3. **Standalone, assigned** (`assign_project_id` set): the assign permission check, then lock that project's `project_balance` `FOR UPDATE`, then the requisition, then validate (active, not generated, depth < 5, currency equal to the requisition's, and funds by the E09-S04 rule; a shortfall → 409 `INSUFFICIENT_BUDGET` with "Assigning this request to Plant 4 Line Retrofit needs 196,000.00 USD; 150,000.00 USD is available."). Then round 1 reserve, set `project_id`, then step 1.3–1.4.
4. Every ledger write goes through `budget.service`. No SQL on budget tables from requisitions.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Final approval of a project-linked requisition: one new project with `parent_id` = the requisition's project, status `active`, `generated_by_requisition_id` set, owner = requester. The requisition is `sourcing_in_progress`. Ledger: **no new row** (the reservation from submit stands). The parent's `reserved` is unchanged.
- [ ] A new requisition against that generated sub-project is refused (E09-S01's REQ-003 guard, end to end).
- [ ] Approval while the parent is `deferred` → 409 `project_not_active`. The decision, the status and the project table are all unchanged (WF-003).
- [ ] Standalone, approver with `budget.allocate`: a top-level `active` project, an allocation and a reservation of the estimate (round 1), and the requisition linked to the project. Without `budget.allocate` → 403, nothing written.
- [ ] Standalone assigned to a project with enough funds: reservation on that project, plus a generated sub-project under it. To a project with too little → 409 `INSUFFICIENT_BUDGET`, nothing written. Without `requisition.approve` on that project → 403.
- [ ] **ACC-001 on the assign path:** two standalone requisitions of 60, approved concurrently and both assigned to a project with 100 available: exactly one succeeds. 20 runs, real Postgres.
- [ ] A project-linked approval carrying `assign_project_id` → 422.
- [ ] Replaying the final decision (same Idempotency-Key, E10-S06) creates no second project, allocation or reservation.
- [ ] After every case, `project_balance` reconciles to the ledger (`budget.service` reconcile for the org reports no drift).
- [ ] Auto-approval at submit (a workflow with no approval step) runs the same effects inside the submit transaction.

## Plants

1. Catch and log the `project_not_active` error in `on_outcome` instead of raising it: the deferred-parent WF-003 test fails, because the decision commits without its effects.
2. Skip the `budget.allocate` check: the standalone-403 test fails.
3. Reserve on the assigned path before locking `project_balance`: the assign concurrency test fails (20 runs).
4. Mark the standalone new project as generated: the "may take further requisitions" test fails (add it: a second requisition against the standalone-created project is accepted).
