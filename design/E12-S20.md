## Requirements (inlined)

- **SRC-002** — An RFQ must contain items/services, quantities, specifications, delivery requirements, response deadline, terms, attachments, and invited vendors.
- **SRC-011** — Evaluators must be able to score qualitative and quantitative criteria with comments and conflicts-of-interest declarations where configured.

## Screens

Two cards appended to E12-S13's editor card list, after `Compliance Requirements`:

- **`Evaluation Criteria`** on `/sourcing/rfqs/:id/edit` (draft only; on `amend` the card is
  read-only, since criteria are edited only while the RFQ is `draft`, E12-S07). Rows of `name`
  (1..120) and `weight` (1..100), add, remove and reorder (with `Move Up`/`Move Down` buttons, no
  drag). Loaded from `GET …/evaluation`; when `source: "default"` the card says `From your
  organisation's default. Save to keep them for this RFQ.` Saved with `PUT …/evaluation/criteria`.
  The page shows **no** weight total: `CriteriaRead` carries none (`sourcing/evaluation.py:50`) and the
  page computes none. The server's signal is E12-S07's 422 `weights_total` at publish, shown through
  E12-S13's error summary. Record "add `weights_total` to `CriteriaRead`" in `notes.followup`.
- **`Evaluators`** on both `edit` and `amend` (so `draft` and `published`, the editor's states,
  `editor/Editor.tsx:27`; managing them while `closed`/`under_evaluation` is a `notes.followup`): the
  picker is ## Blocked B1; add with `POST …/evaluators`, remove with `DELETE`. Rows show
  `EvaluatorRead.display_name` as plain text (it has no email, so not `Person`; `notes.followup`). Shown only when
  `RfqRead.can_edit`. A declared conflict shows `Recused` with the declaration detail to the owner.

Loading, empty (`No criteria yet.` / `No evaluators yet.`), partial (a card that fails keeps the
rest of the editor usable) and error states, per sourcing.md.

## Blocked

- **B1 (scope and authorization):** the evaluator picker has no data source. `POST …/evaluators` takes
  a known `identity_id` and checks `rfq.view` only after submission (`api/sourcing.py:497`); the only
  people lookup is gated by `project.create` and returns every active identity
  (`api/projects.py:111`, `modules/projects/service.py:370`). Who may list eligible evaluators, and
  which endpoint serves it (a new sourcing route is an API change this packet puts out of scope)?

## Plants

2. Allow criteria edits on `amend`: the read-only assertion fails.
