## Requirements (inlined)

- **PO-008** — Approval or issue must convert the applicable requisition reservation into a commitment atomically. *(Issue, per claude-plan §14 #5.)*
- **PO-012** — Duplicate clicks, retries, worker retries, and integration retries must not issue or transmit a PO more than once unintentionally.
- **FIN-005 / invariant 3** — lock before you validate; ledger insert and balance update in one transaction.

## Issue (`POST /purchase-orders/{id}/issue`, Idempotency-Key, `purchase_order.issue` on the unit)

**One transaction, lock order:** every involved `project_balance` (the sub-projects of the lines) in
id order, then the requisitions in id order, then the PO `FOR UPDATE`.
1. The PO must be `approved` and its version current. If it is already `issued` at this version, return the **same** result (200 with the issued PO); never commit again (PO-012).
2. `assert_current_upstream` for each requisition (expired codes block, D-M2-25).
3. For each line, group by (requisition, sub-project, department, ledger account): `amount = Σ budget_amount_project` (E13-S02, at the PO's stored rate, decision 167).
4. **Commit** each group via `budget.postings.commit(conn, scope, project_id=sub_project, amount, currency=project currency, source_type="purchase_order", source_id=po.id, department_code, ledger_account_code, idempotency_key=f"po:{id}:v{version}:commit:{group_key}", from_reservation_entry_id=requisition.reservation_entry_id, …)`. That converts that much of the requisition's reservation into a commitment.
   - **Opus decision (recorded):** if a group's amount exceeds the reservation remaining (possible only by rounding or a tax/freight edit on the draft), the excess must fit the sub-project's **availability** under the same lock, or the issue fails 409 `over_reservation`. Raise a `ProblemError` shaped like `budget/ledger.py::InsufficientBudget`: `checks = {"problem": "over_reservation", "group_key": …, "requested": str(group amount), "reservation_remaining": str(…), "excess": str(requested − reservation_remaining), "available": str(sub-project availability under the lock)}`, all Decimal strings in the project currency. Nothing is ever committed beyond reservation plus availability.
5. `requisitions.service.issue_lines(conn, scope, [(line_id, qty)], actor)`: a new `requisition_line.issued_qty` column, `+= qty`, with a CHECK that `issued_qty ≤ ordered_qty`.
6. `lifecycle.transition(issue)` → `issued`; store `issued_at`, `issued_by`, and the fixed `rate`.
7. **Requisition `completed` (decision 206):** when every non-cancelled line of a requisition has `issued_qty = quantity − cancelled_qty`, `requisitions.service.mark_completed`, audited.
8. Enqueue the transmission (E13-S09's `transmission.enqueue(po_id, version)`; until E13-S09 merges, a no-op hook) **in this transaction**, so a failed send never strands a commitment and a rolled-back issue never sends.

Notify the requester internally (an in-app notification, not an email copy; decision 168). The notification engine is E17-S02 (M4, unmerged) and no notification table exists, so call a no-op hook `notify_requester_issued(conn, scope, po_id)` in `issue.py` **in this transaction**, like item 8; E17-S02 wires it. No new table in this story; record the hook in `notes.followup`.

## Decided

**28 Oct 2026, operator (final)**, pre-flight 1:

1. **Zero-value groups are skipped at issue.** A group whose `Σ budget_amount_project` is `0` (a bid line may carry unit price 0, `20261022_0071_bids.py`) gets **no** `budget.postings.commit` call (`postings.py:143` rejects amounts ≤ 0) and **no** ledger entry; it converts and releases nothing at issue (any leftover reservation follows the requisition's existing close, cancel or expiry release). Its lines still go through `issue_lines` and `lifecycle.transition(issue)` as usual, so the requisition accounting is unchanged. A zero line **never appears on the vendor version** of the PO: see E13-S08 (`vendor_copy` omits it). Tests: a PO with one zero-value group and one real group commits only the real group (one commitment entry, reservation drops by that amount only, drift empty) and issues; a PO whose every group is zero issues with no ledger entry.

## Acceptance

- [ ] Hand-computed example: a PO of 2 lines on one sub-project (different ledger accounts) → two commitment entries converting the reservation; the reservation remaining drops by the same total; **drift empty** (`budget/reconcile`).
- [ ] **Concurrency (real Postgres):** two simultaneous issues of one PO → exactly one commits; the other returns the same issued result; the ledger has one set of entries.
- [ ] Retry with the same Idempotency-Key and with a new key → no second commitment (PO-012).
- [ ] Over reservation by one cent with no availability → 409, nothing written; with availability → commits.
- [ ] Approval alone posts nothing (regression from E13-S04).
- [ ] Requisition `completed` after its last line is issued; not before.
- [ ] Stale requisition / expired codes → 409, nothing written.
- [ ] TEN-010 and authorization per role; `flo check` green.
