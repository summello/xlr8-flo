## Requirements (inlined)

- **PO-002** — A PO must include requestor, Bill To, Ship To, vendor, items/services, descriptions, quantities, units, unit prices, currency, taxes, freight, terms, dates, project, department, and ledger accounts.
- **PO-016 / FIN-008** — tax and freight separately visible; per-unit treatment (DECISIONS 211).
- **PO-017** — notes and files with visibility, internal by default.
- **VEN-012** — vendor eligibility with an override for the authorized.

## Screen `/purchase-orders/:id/edit` (draft)

The order header (Bill To read-only from the masters; Ship To chosen from the requisition's
locations, never typed, decision 165; terms; needed-by); the vendor with `Eligibility
variant="field"` and, for `rfq.approve` holders, `Override` with a reason; recipients (the vendor's
contacts, `Service and Delivery` by default). Lines show price, tax, freight and line total **apart**,
and the project, department and account beneath each description. The freight mode is per line or per
document. The totals bar shows the server sums. **Budget Preview** is the server sentence (`Issuing
will commit 175,665.58 USD on <sub-project>, from its 193,012.13 USD reservation`), and says when
tax or freight does not count against the budget (DECISIONS 211). `Notes and Files` show visibility
in words, `Internal` by default, and `Sent to Vendor` notes. Files say "Not sent until scanning is
available" (decision 171). `Save` and `Submit for Approval` are never disabled. With no workflow, the
409 reads "No purchase order workflow is published. Ask an administrator to set one up. Your draft is
kept."
