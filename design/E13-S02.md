# E13-S02 — Tax and freight: separate on every line and total, per-unit budget treatment, line rounding

**Epic** E13 · **M3** · **ledger/M** · after **E13-S01** · tags: `money` (Opus review)

> **Conventions:** `docs/m1-conventions.md`, `docs/m2-conventions.md`, `docs/author-traps.md`.
> **Decisions:** canvas DECISIONS **211** (`tax.consumes_budget` and `freight.consumes_budget` per
> BU/OU, default on), 164 (tax per line; freight per line or per document, shared pro rata by line
> value; rounding per line; document totals are sums of rounded lines; a PO line total equals the
> award's landed line), claude-plan §14 #6. `design-system/pages/purchase-orders.md` §2 "Price, tax
> and freight are always separate". Back end only.
> **Writes:** every `PATCH`/`PUT`/`DELETE` on an editable resource follows `docs/m2-conventions.md` §O (`If-Match` with the integer version, `ETag` on reads, 400 when missing, 409 `stale_version` on mismatch). `Idempotency-Key` protects **POST** commands only (the middleware ignores other methods); where a packet says "(Idempotency-Key)" on a non-POST route, read §O instead.
> **RLS:** every new tenant table gets literal `ALTER TABLE <t> ENABLE ROW LEVEL SECURITY; … FORCE …; CREATE POLICY tenant_isolation ON <t> …` statements in the migration text, one block per table, never generated by a helper or f-string (the static gate in `tests/isolation/test_ci_guards.py` reads the text; the light check runs it).

## Requirements (inlined)

- **PO-016** — Tax and freight must follow the applicable BU/OU expense policy and remain separately visible.
- **FIN-008** — Tax and freight treatment must be independently configurable at organization or BU/OU level.
- **FIN-009** — Rounding mode, currency precision, and line-versus-document rounding rules must be centrally defined and consistently used.

## Design

**Settings:** add `tax.consumes_budget` and `freight.consumes_budget` (bool, default `true`) to
`modules/org/settings.py`, with validators (`_is_bool`), resolved for the PO's unit. **E12-S08 added
both already** (`modules/org/settings.py` lines 17–18 and 60–61; read through
`OrgService.effective_setting(unit_id, ...)` as `sourcing/awards.py` `_flags` does): reuse them, add nothing.

**Columns on `po_line`:** `price` (= `unit_price × quantity`), `tax`, `freight` (a share or a direct
amount), `line_total`, and `budget_amount` (= price + tax if
`tax.consumes_budget` + freight if `freight.consumes_budget`). All `NUMERIC(18,4)` in the PO
currency, **quantized per line with freight absorbing the residual** (Decisions 1): `price = quantize(unit_price × quantity)`,
`tax = quantize(tax_exact)`, `line_total = quantize(price_exact + tax_exact + freight_exact)` (the award's rule,
`sourcing.awards.amounts`, one quantize of the exact sum), `freight = line_total − price − tax`. `tax`, `freight` and `document_freight` with `CHECK (... >= 0)` (as `bid_line.tax` and `bid.freight` in
`20261022_0071_bids.py`), and a negative or non-decimal edit refused 422 `validation-failed`
(`ErrorCode.VALIDATION_FAILED`) with the field, each **quantized per line** through `kernel/money.quantize` (FIN-009: one rule, line
rounding). Plus `budget_amount_project` (= `budget_amount × po.rate`, quantized in the project
currency). This is what E13-S05 commits.

**Freight mode** on `purchase_order`: `freight_mode text NOT NULL DEFAULT 'line'` CHECK in `line` and `document`, with
`document_freight NUMERIC(18,4)` (null in `line` mode). A new PO is `line` (Decisions 2). Switching on a draft (PATCH
`freight_mode`): `line → document` sets `document_freight = Σ line freight`, then re-spreads it; `document → line` keeps
each line's current share as its direct freight and nulls `document_freight`. In `document` mode, each line's freight share is pro rata by line
**price** (decision 116/164), quantized per line, and the **residual cent goes to the largest line**
(ties: the lowest `line_no`), so the shares sum exactly to the document freight.

**Defaults from the award:** `award_line` stores no tax or freight (`migrations/20261022_0077_award.py`);
`sourcing/awards.py` rebuilds them from the bid (`_quotes`, a private `Quote(quantity, unit_price, tax,
freight_share)` per bid line). Expose that through the module contract: `sourcing.service.award_line_quotes(conn,
scope, award_id) -> list[AwardLineQuote]` (new schema in `sourcing/schemas.py`: `rfq_line_id`, `vendor_id`,
`currency`, `quoted_quantity`, `unit_price`, `tax`, `freight_share`, decimal strings), a thin wrapper over the
existing `_quotes` so the award and the PO read one source; purchasing never reads `bid` tables. A new PO line
takes its tax and freight from that quote in proportion to the ordered quantity (`× qty / quoted qty`, as
`sourcing.awards.amounts` does), quantized, so **ordering the whole
awarded quantity gives `line_total` equal to the award's landed line** (decision 164; assert it).
Draft edits may change tax and freight: this story extends E13-S01's `PATCH /purchase-orders/{id}` (draft, `If-Match`) with
`freight_mode`, `document_freight` (`document` mode only, else 422), and per line `lines: [{line_no, quantity?, tax?, freight?}]`
(`freight` in `line` mode only, else 422); a line's `tax`/`freight` edit sets that line's amount directly and recomputes its
`line_total`. If the same PATCH changes a line's quantity and its tax, the rescale runs first and the explicit value wins. **A draft quantity edit** (E13-S01 PATCH) rescales that line's tax and freight
from the award quote (`× new qty / quoted qty`), discarding any manual edit on that line; in `document` mode the unchanged
`document_freight` re-spreads over all lines (Decisions 3). The values stay visible separately on every read.

**Budget amount on a draft** is recomputed from the current `tax.consumes_budget` / `freight.consumes_budget` of the PO's
unit on every read and every write, so a settings change shows at once; E13-S05 freezes it at issue, when it commits
(Decisions 4). The stored `budget_amount` / `budget_amount_project` columns are rewritten on each draft write.

**Totals** on read: `price_total`, `tax_total`, `freight_total`, `total` and `budget_total`, each
the **sum of the rounded lines**, never recomputed on the document.

## Acceptance

- [ ] Hand-computed example (3 lines, document freight of 100.00 split 33.34 / 33.33 / 33.33, a 3-decimal currency case): every line and total matches the test's `Decimal` literals; shares sum exactly.
- [ ] A whole-quantity PO's `line_total` equals the award's landed line, including a rounding-edge case where three separate quantizes would differ by one minor unit (freight takes it); a half-quantity PO's equals half, quantized.
- [ ] A new PO is `freight_mode = line`; `line → document` gives `document_freight = Σ` the line freights and re-spreads; `document → line` keeps each share.
- [ ] Quantity edit: a line with a manual tax edit, lowered from 10 to 4, gets tax and freight `× 4 / quoted qty` (manual edit gone); in `document` mode every line's share moves and they still sum to `document_freight`.
- [ ] Flip `tax.consumes_budget` after the draft exists: the next read returns the new `budget_amount` without a write.
- [ ] Negative tax, freight or document freight → 422 `validation-failed` with the field.
- [ ] With `tax.consumes_budget = false` on the unit, `budget_amount` excludes tax, and `tax` is still returned; likewise freight. A child OU inherits its BU's setting.
- [ ] Totals are sums of rounded lines (a case where document-level rounding would differ by a cent).
- [ ] TEN-010 and authorization; `flo check` green.

## Plants

1. Round at the document level: the one-cent case fails.
2. Ignore `freight.consumes_budget`: the setting test fails.
3. Quantize freight on its own instead of taking the residual: the rounding-edge equality test fails.
4. Keep a manual tax edit on a quantity change: the rescale test fails.
5. Read `budget_amount` from the stored column without recomputing: the settings-flip test fails.

## Decisions

**27 Oct 2026, operator (final)**, from the Codex draft pre-flight of 27 Oct:

1. **Rounding:** freight absorbs the residual. Quantize price and tax; `line_total = quantize(price + tax + exact freight)` as the award does; `freight = line_total − price − tax`, so a whole-quantity PO line equals the award's landed line.
2. **Freight mode** defaults to `line`. `line → document` sets `document_freight = Σ line freight`, then re-spreads; `document → line` keeps each line's current share.
3. **Quantity edit on a draft** rescales tax and freight from the award quote (`× qty / quoted qty`), discarding a manual edit on that line; in `document` mode the unchanged `document_freight` re-spreads.
4. **Budget amount:** a draft recomputes `budget_amount` on every read and write; E13-S05 freezes it at issue.
