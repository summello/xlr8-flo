## Requirements (inlined)

- **PO-010** — Issued POs must be immutable; changes must create a numbered, approved change order/version.
- **PO-011** — A change order must preserve the prior version, identify changed fields/lines, recalculate budget effects, and be reapproved when policy requires.
- **ACC-005** — Changing an issued PO creates a new version/change order and preserves the vendor-sent snapshot.

## Design

**Immutability:** an `issued` PO's header and lines refuse every write (409 `issued_is_immutable`).
A trigger enforces it too (a raw UPDATE of an issued row's money columns raises).

**Revisions (pre-flight 1).** `version` stays the §O optimistic-lock counter (it already moves on
submit, approval and issue, `lifecycle.py`) and is never read as a document revision. Each applied
change order writes a **new `purchase_order` row** (and its `po_line` rows) with the same `number`, a
new column `revision int NOT NULL DEFAULT 0` (0 = the original issue, n = change order n) and
`supersedes_id uuid` (FK `(org_id, supersedes_id)` to `purchase_order(org_id, id)`); the migration
replaces `UNIQUE (org_id, unit_id, number)` with `UNIQUE (org_id, unit_id, number, revision)`. The
prior row moves `issued → superseded` (existing transition) and keeps its snapshot, PDF and
transmissions. "Version" below means revision.

**Change order** `po_change` (RLS): `org_id uuid NOT NULL REFERENCES organization(id)`, `id`, `po_id`
(FK `(org_id, po_id)` to `purchase_order(org_id, id)`, the revision it changes), `UNIQUE (org_id, po_id, number)`, `number int` (1, 2, … per PO), `from_version`,
`draft jsonb` (the proposed header and lines), `diff jsonb` (field-level `was`/`now` per line, in
words for the UI), `budget_effect jsonb` (per sub-project and code group: the signed delta in the
project currency), `material bool` (computed by the server), `status` CHECK in `draft`,
`approval_pending`, `approved`, `rejected` and `withdrawn`, plus `created_by` and `reason`.

**Editable in a change order:** line quantity (down, or up within the award line's open-to-order),
unit price (only down; up → 422, since a price rise above the award needs a new award), tax and
freight, needed-by, terms, and lines removed. Adding a line comes only from another awarded line of
the same vendor, award **and requisition** (`purchase_order.requisition_id`; a line from another
requisition → 422 `invalid`, since the PO's budget scope is one requisition). **Material** = any change to an amount, a line count, an item, or a line
removal. Dates and terms alone are not.

- Non-material → applies at once on `POST …/changes/{n}/apply` (`purchase_order.amend`).
- Material → `require_workflow_for(conn, scope, "po", …)` (decision 208), then approval through a **new** `PoChangeAdapter` (`document_type = "po_change"`, `document_id` =
the change id, `snapshot` reads `po_change`), registered in `api/document_types.py::register_document_types()`
beside `PoAdapter`; the existing `"po"` adapter reads `purchase_order` by id and cannot take a change id
(`purchasing/approval.py:41`). Its permissions and the change's own routes: see ## Blocked (b). Applies on approval.

**Apply (one transaction, locks as E13-S05):** the old version → `superseded` (its snapshot, PDF and
transmissions stay readable); the new version → `issued` with `version + 1`. The budget effect is
then posted: **increase** → `commit` more from the reservation (with the E13-S05 rule for any excess),
key `po:{id}:co{n}:commit:{group}`; **decrease** → `release_commitment` of the difference once, key
`po:{id}:co{n}:release:{group}`, then **re-reserve it on the sub-project** (decision 209) through
`requisitions.service.rereserve(conn, scope, requisition_id, amount, …)` (defined by E12-S09 step 4; reuse it) and return the quantity
through `order_lines` / `issue_lines` (negative deltas). The new version is transmitted (E13-S09
hook) in the same transaction.

## Acceptance

- [ ] Issued PO refuses edits (service and trigger).
- [ ] Diff: a quantity change and a date change produce field-level `was`/`now`; the date-only change is not material and applies without approval; the quantity change is material and needs approval (409 without a workflow).
- [ ] Budget effect hand-computed: a decrease of 50.00 → one release of 50.00 and one re-reservation of 50.00 on the sub-project; an increase of 20.00 → one commit; drift empty; apply retried → nothing posted twice.
- [ ] Project, department or ledger account change → 422 `use_transfer`; a price increase → 422.
- [ ] v1 remains readable with its snapshot after v2.
- [ ] Concurrency: two change orders applied at once on one PO → one succeeds (version check under the lock).
- [ ] TEN-010 and authorization; `flo check` green.

## Plants

1. Release without re-reserving: the decision-209 assertion fails.
2. Mark a quantity change as non-material: the approval test fails.

## Decided

Operator, 29 Oct 2026, answering pre-flight 1:

1. **A decrease releases newest first (option a).** For a decrease of D on a code group, release the group's open commitment entries newest first (latest `co{n}` commit, then older ones, then the issue commit) until D is met, one `release_commitment` per entry touched, key `po:{id}:co{n}:release:{group}:{entry_id}`. A partial release of the last entry touched is allowed. No pro rata, so no rounding residue.
2. **Change-order authorization and transitions are confirmed as proposed.**
   - `POST /purchase-orders/{id}/changes` (create, `draft`), `PATCH …/changes/{n}` (edit draft, §O), `GET …/changes`, `GET …/changes/{n}`: `purchase_order.amend` to write, `purchase_order.view` to read.
   - `POST …/changes/{n}/submit` (`draft → approval_pending`, material only); `…/withdraw` (`approval_pending → withdrawn`, the change's creator only); approve and reject through the approvals engine with `PoChangeAdapter.approve_permission = "purchase_order.approve"` (reject → `rejected`, terminal). `…/apply` (non-material only: `draft → approved`, applied in the same transaction) needs `purchase_order.amend`; a material change applies inside its approval outcome.
   - Separation of duties: the change's creator may not approve it.
   - One open (`draft` or `approval_pending`) change per PO; a second → 409 `change_in_progress`.
