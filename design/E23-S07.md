## Why this story is blocked

The backlog says `fleet.py`'s `plan()` picks the first eligible account (`room[0]`) for a new story, where `opus_account()` ranks by remaining capacity, and that `test_fleet.py` holds a "first eligible account wins" fixture. **None of that code is in this repository.**

- The supervisor is `~/.claude/fleet/fleet.py`, "user-scoped and project-agnostic" (`agents/fleet.toml` header). Only its configuration, `agents/fleet.toml`, is tracked. There is no `fleet.py`, `plan()`, `opus_account()` or `test_fleet.py` anywhere in this tree (searched for `def plan`, `room[0]`, `def opus_account`, `test_fleet*`).
- The capacity numbers the ranking would use come from Mission Control's collector (`[limits] command` in `fleet.toml`: `python3 -I $HOME/.claude/mods/mission-control/hooks/collect.py ...`), also outside the repo.
- AGENTS.md §0.2 and §0.5: a story may not touch files outside its worktree or invent scope. I could not read the code the backlog describes, so I cannot verify its description, and I will not write a packet that tells an author to edit a file they cannot see.

What **is** in the repo: `flo account <STORY>` (`agents/scripts/flo`, ~line 248) picks which account runs a *Sonnet author* by worker load, then "not used last"; `FLO_ACCOUNT` pins one. The supervisor passes `FLO_ACCOUNT={account}` into `begin.sh` (`fleet.toml` `[steps] begin`). So the account choice for a new story is made by the supervisor, upstream of anything tracked here.
