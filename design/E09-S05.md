# E09-S05 — Requisition lifecycle state machine (submit reserves, withdraw and cancel release)

**Epic** E09 · **M2** · **engine/L** · after **E09-S04**, **E09-S03**, **E10-S05**, **E10-S06** · tags: `money`, `concurrency`, `migration`

> **Conventions:** `docs/m1-conventions.md`, `docs/m2-conventions.md` (G–O; **H adapter registry, I permissions, J statuses, L funding rounds**) and `docs/author-traps.md`. Decisions: `docs/m2-decisions.md` — **D-M2-15 reserve at submit**, D-M2-1 reject modes and return-for-changes, D-M2-13 material fields, canvas decision 78 (withdraw). Read `design/E10-S05.md` "Contract" for the frozen `Outcome`, `OutcomeContext` and `DocumentAdapter`, and `design/E10-S06.md` for `approvals.service.withdraw`.

## Requirements

- **REQ-005** — The base lifecycle must be Draft → Approval Pending → Sourcing in Progress → Sourced → Awarded → Open for Purchase → Completed.
- **REQ-016** — A requisition must not complete while an associated award or PO remains actionable unless an authorized close-out rule permits it. *(Guard and registry here; the states that reach `completed` are M3.)*
- **WF-002** — A status transition must validate actor, current state, required fields, approvals, and financial side effects.
- **WF-003** — The document transition and its financial entries must commit atomically.
- **ACC-001** — Two concurrent requisitions cannot reserve more than the same project's available budget.
- **FIN-006 / invariant 3** — `SELECT … FOR UPDATE` on the funding row before validating availability, ledger insert and balance update in one transaction.
- **FIN-011 / REQ-013** (the release paths this story owns: withdraw, cancel and reject before approval) — rejected, withdrawn, cancelled requisitions release unused reservation exactly once.
- **FIN-012** — Retries and duplicate submissions must not create duplicate financial effects.
- **PROJ-018** (hook) — A project with open requisitions must not close without resolution or authorized override.

## Design decisions (this packet)

- **D-M2-15:** submit = lock the funding row, validate (E09-S04 with `lock=True`), check routing, **reserve**, start the approval instance, in one transaction. A standalone requisition reserves nothing at submit.
- **Withdraw (canvas 78):** only **the requester** (`actor_id == requester_id`, plus `requisition.withdraw` at the record) may withdraw, from `draft`, `approval_pending`, or `sourcing_in_progress` while no RFQ exists. The last case is implemented by E09-S07, and the table marks it. Anyone else gets 403 with "Only the requester can withdraw. Ask an administrator to cancel it."
- **Cancel** is the administrator's stop: `requisition.cancel`, a `reason` of at least 10 characters, from `approval_pending` (here) or `sourcing_in_progress` (E09-S07).
- **Return for changes keeps the reservation** (D-M2-1). Reject-to-draft and reject-permanently release it.
- **Baseline grants:** `requisition.submit` and `requisition.withdraw` to `requestor`, all three to `organization-administrator`. **`requisition.cancel` is not given to `requestor`**: a requester stops their own request by withdrawing, and cancel exists for someone else's.
- **Drafts do not block project closure.** `open_documents` counts `approval_pending`, `sourcing_in_progress`, `sourced`, `awarded` and `open_for_purchase` (the statuses that can hold or lead to money). A draft against a completed project simply fails submission (E09-S04).
- **Status history** is an append-only `requisition_status_event` table (the E06-S03 `project_status_event` precedent). It feeds the History card in E09-S11.

## Transition table (the only legal moves; every other pair is 409 `invalid_transition`)

| from | to | how | actor and permission | money |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| draft | approval_pending | `POST …/transitions {to}` (submit) | `requisition.submit` | reserve (project-linked), new round |
| draft | withdrawn | `POST …/transitions` | requester + `requisition.withdraw` | release the live reservation if any (a returned draft holds one), reason `withdrawn` |
| approval_pending | withdrawn | `POST …/transitions` → `approvals.service.withdraw` → `on_outcome(WITHDRAWN)` | requester + `requisition.withdraw` | release, reason `withdrawn` |
| approval_pending | cancelled | `POST …/transitions` → `approvals.service.withdraw` → `on_outcome(WITHDRAWN)` | `requisition.cancel`, reason ≥ 10 | release, reason `cancelled` |
| approval_pending | sourcing_in_progress | `on_outcome(APPROVED)` | final approver (E10-S06) | **E09-S06** (until it merges, `on_outcome(APPROVED)` raises 409 `CONFLICT`, `checks.problem = "approval_effects_unavailable"`) |
| approval_pending | rejected | `on_outcome(REJECTED, reject_mode="permanent")` | approver | release, reason `rejected` |
| approval_pending | draft | `on_outcome(REJECTED, reject_mode="return_to_draft")` | approver | release, reason `returned_to_draft` |
| approval_pending | draft | `on_outcome(RETURNED)` | approver | **keep** the reservation |
| sourcing_in_progress | withdrawn | `POST …/transitions` | requester + `requisition.withdraw`, no RFQ linked | **E09-S07** |
| sourcing_in_progress | cancelled | `POST …/transitions` | `requisition.cancel`, reason ≥ 10 | **E09-S07** |
| sourcing_in_progress | expired | daily job | system | **E09-S07** |
| sourcing_in_progress → sourced → awarded → open_for_purchase → completed | — | M3 stories | — | M3 (commitment conversion) |

`completed`, `rejected`, `withdrawn`, `cancelled` and `expired` are terminal. Until E09-S07 merges, the rows marked E09-S07 return 409 `invalid_transition` with "Not available yet." The `→ completed` move is guarded by `assert_completable` (behaviour 7).

## Contract

- `POST /api/v1/requisitions/{id}/transitions` body `{to: "approval_pending"|"withdrawn"|"cancelled", reason?: str}`, **Idempotency-Key** and **If-Match** (convention O) → 200 `RequisitionRead` | 400 | 403 | 404 | 409 `invalid_transition` / `stale_version` / `no_workflow` / `no_eligible_approver` / `route_blocked` | 409 `INSUFFICIENT_BUDGET` (the ledger refused at reserve: a race the lock lost, never expected after a passing check, but mapped) | 422 `submission_invalid` (E09-S04's shape, plus `routing/workflow` problems).
  - The route is guarded by `require("requisition.view", requisition_target)` (401/404 concealment and the route gate). The router builds `permitted(permission) -> bool` from `kernel.authz.permits(request, permission, target)` and passes it to the service, which checks the permission the table names **after** locking and reading the state. That is the E06-S03 round-3 pattern; reuse `permits`, and add nothing to `kernel/authz.py`.
- `GET /api/v1/requisitions/{id}/events` — `requisition.view` (record) → 200 `[StatusEventRead]` newest first: `{at, from_status, to_status, actor_email | null, system: bool, reason, funding_round, ledger_entry_id | null, decision_id | null}`. A nil-UUID or null actor gives `system: true`, `actor_email: null` and the UI says "System". Emails come from `identity.service.emails_by_id`. This is the History card's data (E09-S11). A foreign-tenant id → 404, with a `COVERED` entry and a real foreign-tenant test.
- `GET /api/v1/requisitions/{id}/transitions/available` → 200 `[{to, allowed, blocked_reasons: [str]}]` for the caller. **`allowed` means "would succeed once the required input is supplied"**: a missing `reason` for cancel does not make it `false` (the E06-S07 amendment-3 lesson). A failing submission check makes submit `allowed: false` with the problem messages as `blocked_reasons`.
- `SubmissionCheck` (E09-S04) gains `route: RoutePreview | null` from `approvals.service.preview_route`, and routing problems under `routing/workflow`, for example "No approver is set for Manufacturing above 250,000.00 USD. Ask an administrator" (the page-spec wording; fill in the unit and the step name from the preview).

2. **Submit** (one `tenant_transaction`):
   1. Read the requisition unlocked to learn its project. Call `check_submission(..., lock=True)`, which locks `project_balance` then the requisition (E09-S04). Then check `If-Match` and `status = 'draft'` on the locked row.
   2. Add routing problems from `approvals.service.preview_route(conn, scope, document_type="requisition", document_id=…, resolver=…)`: a missing workflow, no eligible approver at a step, or a blocked condition each become a `routing/workflow` problem. Any problems → 422 `submission_invalid`, nothing written.
   3. **Reserve** (project-linked): let `remaining` = the live reservation's remaining, read through a new `budget.service.reservation_remaining(conn, scope, reservation_entry_id) -> Decimal` (a one-line read of the E07-S04 view, 404 for a foreign id; requisitions never query budget tables directly), or 0 if there is no live reservation. If `remaining == estimate`, keep it (a return-for-changes draft resubmitted unchanged). Otherwise release `remaining` with key `req:<id>:release:<round>:resubmit` (when > 0), increment `funding_round`, and `budget.service.reserve(conn, scope, project_id=…, amount=estimate, currency, source_type="requisition", source_id=id, effective_date=today, actor_id, department_code, ledger_account_code, idempotency_key=f"req:{id}:reserve:{round}")`. Store `reservation_entry_id`. The funds check at step 1 used `need = estimate − remaining`.
   4. Set `status = 'approval_pending'`, `submitted_at`, `version + 1`. Call `requisitions.service.record_requested` (E09-S03). Insert the status event (with the ledger entry id) and an audit row `requisition.submit`.
   5. `approvals.service.start(conn, scope, document_type="requisition", document_id=id, actor_id, resolver)`. An auto-approval at start calls `on_outcome(APPROVED)` in this transaction (E09-S06).
3. **Release helper** `requisitions.funding.release_live(conn, scope, requisition_row, reason)`: no-op when `reservation_entry_id` is null or its remaining is 0. Otherwise `budget.service.release_reservation(conn, scope, reservation_entry_id=…, amount=None, actor_id, reason=<words>, idempotency_key=f"req:{id}:release:{round}:{reason}")`. Lock order: `project_balance` first (taken by `release_reservation`), then the requisition row. E09-S07 and E09-S08 reuse it.
4. **Withdraw / cancel while pending (amendment 1 supersedes the lock order written here: the approval instance is locked first, so call `approvals.service.withdraw` before taking any balance or requisition lock; the requisition's `pending_terminal` is a `ContextVar`, not a column):** call `approvals.service.withdraw(conn, scope, document_type="requisition", document_id, actor_id, reason)`. Its `on_outcome(WITHDRAWN)` reads `pending_terminal` (null means `withdrawn`, the requester's path), releases, sets the terminal status and clears `pending_terminal`. A draft withdraw skips the approvals call.
5. **The adapter** `modules/requisitions/approval.py::RequisitionAdapter` implements the frozen E10-S05 protocol exactly:
   - `document_type = "requisition"`, `approve_permission = "requisition.approve"`, `read_permission = "requisition.view"`, `accepts_project_assignment = True`.
   - `fields()` returns `{"amount": "money"}` **only** (canvas 83, D-M2-4; the Phase 2 list is in E10-S02).
   - `snapshot()` returns `version`, `requester_id`, `target` (project target, or the BU/OU target for standalone), `attributes = {"amount": Money(estimate, currency)}`, and `material` built **exactly as `design/E10-S07.md` Contract item 2 specifies** (D-M2-13): `amount` (estimate, four places), `project_id`, `ledger_account_code`, `catalogue_items` (sorted, comma-joined catalogue ids of the lines, `-` for a line without one), `line_count`. When the requisition has a pending amendment (E09-S08), `snapshot` presents the amendment's values and version instead. E09-S08 adds that branch.
   - `read_permission = "requisition.view"` (convention P: it replaced `can_read()` in the frozen protocol; approvals checks it at `snapshot().target`).
   - `summaries(conn, scope, ids)`: one query returning `{number, title, amount: Money(estimate, currency), requester_id, href: f"/requisitions/{id}"}`.
   - `on_outcome()`: the outcome rows of the transition table. It locks `project_balance` (when a reservation exists), then the requisition, writes status, `decided_at`, `version + 1`, the status event and an audit row.
6. **Composition (convention H):** `apps/api/src/flo/api/document_types.py::register_document_types()` is created by whichever of E09-S05 and E10-S13 merges first; the second adds its adapters to it. ## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] **Parametrized state-machine test:** every row of the table succeeds for an authorized actor; every other (from, to) pair over the 11 statuses → 409 `invalid_transition` (AGENTS.md 3.5 `workflow`).
- [ ] **ACC-001:** two requisitions of 60 each, submitted concurrently against a project with 100 available: exactly one is `approval_pending` with a reservation, the other gets 422 with a `funds/estimate` problem and holds nothing. Run 20 times against real Postgres. `project_balance.reserved` equals the sum of `reservation_remaining.remaining`.
- [ ] Submitting the same requisition twice with the same Idempotency-Key gives one reservation and the first response. With a new key, the second gets 409 `invalid_transition`.
- [ ] A submit that fails routing (no published workflow) writes nothing: no ledger row, no status change, no catalogue event.
- [ ] Withdraw from `approval_pending` by the requester: status `withdrawn`, one release row equal to the reservation, the approval instance `withdrawn`. By another user holding `requisition.withdraw`: 403. Two concurrent withdraws: one release row in total.
- [ ] Cancel from `approval_pending` without a reason → 422; with one → `cancelled` and released once.
- [ ] `on_outcome`: REJECTED/permanent → `rejected` and released; REJECTED/return_to_draft → `draft` and released; RETURNED → `draft` with the reservation unchanged; then resubmitting unchanged keeps the same `reservation_entry_id` and round, and resubmitting with a larger estimate releases round N and reserves round N+1 under the lock.
- [ ] A standalone submit writes no ledger row.
- [ ] `transitions/available` agrees with what POST does for every status and actor (requester, approver, administrator, viewer); cancel without a reason is `allowed: true`.
- [ ] A project with an `approval_pending` requisition cannot be completed (E06-S03 `ClosingBlocked` names "1 open documents"); with only a draft, it can.
- [ ] `requisition_status_event` cannot be updated or deleted; every transition wrote one.
- [ ] Existing tenant: after the migration, the requestor system role holds `requisition.submit` and `requisition.withdraw`, and not `requisition.cancel`.
- [ ] Foreign tenant → 404 on both routes. No grant → 404. View-only grant → 403 on POST.
- [ ] Migration up/down/up preserves requisitions, events and role grants.

## Pre-flight amendments (11 Oct 2026, Opus; binding, they override anything above)

Each item below was found by reading the code this story touches. A block on any of them is a packet defect, not an author failure.

1. **Lock-order law (applies to every E09 and E10 story).** Locks are taken in this order only: **approval instance → `project_balance` rows (ascending project id) → requisition row.** E10-S06's decision path locks the instance first and then `on_outcome` takes balance → requisition, so any other path that takes balance or requisition *before* the instance forms a cycle and Postgres kills one side with 40P01 (a 500). Submit is safe because no running instance exists yet (balance → requisition → `start`). **Behaviour 4 (withdraw and cancel while pending) is therefore changed:** call `approvals.service.withdraw` **first**, with nothing pre-locked; authorize on an unlocked read; `on_outcome(WITHDRAWN)` takes balance → requisition and re-checks the status under those locks. Replace the `pending_terminal` column (drop it from the migration) with a `ContextVar` in `requisitions/lifecycle.py` that the withdraw/cancel caller sets around the `withdraw` call and `on_outcome(WITHDRAWN)` reads (unset means `withdrawn`); reset it in `finally`. Writing a column would lock the requisition before the instance. Acceptance adds: a concurrent approve decision and a requester withdraw, 20 runs against real Postgres, exactly one wins, the loser gets a 409 (`task_closed` or `invalid_transition`), and **no run raises 40P01 or answers 500**.
2. **`reservation_remaining` returns more than a number.** Replace `-> Decimal` with `ReservationState(project_id: UUID, amount: Decimal, remaining: Decimal, currency: str)` (frozen dataclass in `budget/schemas.py`, exported), read from `ledger_entry` joined to the `reservation_remaining` view in one query, 404 for a foreign or non-reservation id. The project id is needed by amendment 4, and `amount` by amendment 6. `requisition_status_event.actor_id` stays **without** a foreign key (E09-S07 writes the nil UUID there).
3. **Permissions and the catalogue tests** are settled by the body (`<next+1>_requisition_permissions.py`, `EXPECTED_PERMISSIONS`, `tests/authz/conftest.py`). The E10 lane edits the same set literal and migration list; the second lane to merge resolves the conflict by union.
4. **A returned draft can be moved to another project** (E09-S01 PATCH allows `project_id`) while it still holds a reservation on the old one. Submit step 2.3 as written would check funds against the new project with `need = estimate − remaining`, where `remaining` sits on the old project, and would take the two balance locks in arbitrary order. Replace with: read the live reservation unlocked (`ReservationState`); if its `project_id` differs from the requisition's, call `check_submission(..., also_lock=[held.project_id], need=<full estimate>)` (balances lock ascending), **release the held remainder first** (key `req:<id>:release:<round>:resubmit`, on the old project), then reserve the full estimate on the new one (round + 1). Same project: `need = estimate − remaining`, as written. After the locks, re-read the state; if it differs from the unlocked read, answer 409 `stale_version` ("The reservation changed. Retry."). Acceptance adds: a returned draft held on A, moved to B and resubmitted: A's `reserved` falls by the held amount, B's rises by the estimate, round + 1; two requisitions swapping A and B concurrently, 20 runs, no 40P01.
5. **Routing problems have two shapes.** `preview_route` problems found at submit become 422 `submission_invalid` entries under `routing/workflow`, nothing written. The 409 labels in the Contract (`no_workflow`, `no_eligible_approver`, `route_blocked`) are what `start` itself raises when the route changed between the preview and the start (a workflow unpublished, an approver removed): the transaction rolls back and the problem is answered as written. Both are tested. **Cross-lane gate:** `design/E10-S05.md` never defines the shape of `RoutePreview`, `RouteStep` or `TaskRead`. This story consumes, per route step, `{label, approver_emails: [str], state}` and, per problem, `{code, step_label, message}`. Read the merged E10-S05 code before starting; if a field is missing, write `notes.blocked` naming it. Do not compute emails (D-M2-16) or step labels in `requisitions`.
6. **`RequisitionRead` gains two single-record blocks** (list rows carry both as `null`, so the list issues no extra query): `reservation: {entry_id, reserved, released, remaining, currency, funding_round, project_available} | null` and `approval: {instance_id, status} | null`. `reserved` is `ReservationState.amount`, `released = reserved − remaining`, and `project_available` is non-null only when the caller holds `budget.view` on the project (`permits`), otherwise `null`: a requester must not learn a project's balance from this card. They are filled on `GET /requisitions/{id}`, the PATCH and the transitions response. This is what the confirmation card (`requisition-create.md` §6) and the Reservation card (`requisitions.md` §2) render, so E09-S11 never parses a ledger label or computes a figure. Acceptance: a viewer without `budget.view` sees reserved and released and `project_available: null`; after a withdraw `released == reserved` and `remaining == 0.0000`.
7. **The open-documents registry needs a connection and a scope.** `Lifecycle` holds a `ProjectRepository`, which already exposes `.connection` (public) and carries the scope via `ScopedRepo`. Call each registered counter with `(repo.connection, repo.scope, project_id)`; expose `scope` as a property if it is private (authorized edit in `projects/lifecycle.py`). The stub `open_documents(project_id)` in that file is deleted, and `Lifecycle.snapshot` still reads `reserved` and `committed` from the balance row, so **a returned draft that holds a reservation already blocks closure through `reserved > 0`**; the counter correctly leaves drafts out.
8. **`posting_not_allowed`.** `assert_posting_allowed` refuses a `reserved` posting unless the project is `active` (409 `posting_not_allowed`). E09-S04's project check reports it first as a `project/project_id` problem; do not let the raw ledger error reach the client.
