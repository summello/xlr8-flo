## Requirements (inlined)

- **PO-001** — POs must be creatable from approved and sourced requisitions or authorized project items. *(M3: from approved awards only, decision 163.)*
- **PO-002** — A PO must include requestor, Bill To, Ship To, vendor, items/services, descriptions, quantities, units, unit prices, currency, taxes, freight, terms, dates, project, department, and ledger accounts.
- **PO-006** — PO numbers must be unique within their configured organization/BU/OU sequence.
- **PO-009** — Partial POs must reduce only the corresponding open requisition quantity and value.
- **SRC-018** — Every expenditure line must complete RFQ sourcing before final award, PO submission, or financial effect.
- **VEN-012 / VEN-013** — eligibility at selection; the vendor snapshot is kept.

## Data

`purchase_order` (RLS): `id`, `org_id`, `number` (`org.numbering` doc type `"po"`, **scoped to the
PO's unit**: `allocate(scope_id=unit_id, "po", date)`, PO-006), `unit_id`, `vendor_id`,
`vendor_snapshot jsonb` (names, number, address; VEN-013), `award_id`, `currency` (the vendor's),
`rate jsonb` (the award's stored rate to the project currency: rate, source, effective date;
decision 167), `bill_to jsonb` (from the org or unit address), `ship_to_location_id` (one of the
requisition's delivery locations), `requester_id` (the requisition's requester), `terms text`,
`needed_by date`, `status` (E13-S03 owns the machine; this story writes `draft` only), `version int
DEFAULT 1`, `created_by`, `created_at`.

`po_line`: `po_id`, `line_no`, `award_line_id`, `requisition_id`, `requisition_line_id`,
`description`, `uom_code`, `quantity NUMERIC(18,4) > 0`, `unit_price` (from the award line, vendor
currency), `project_id` (**the requisition's sub-project**, D-M2-26), `department_code`,
`ledger_account_code` (the requisition line's codes), with tax and freight columns added by E13-S02.

## The ordered-quantity guard (PO-009)

`open to order = awarded_qty − ordered_qty` on `requisition_line`, but only for quantity awarded **to
this vendor on an approved award** (tracked per award line: `award_line.ordered_qty`, a new column
added here). Add `requisitions.service.order_lines(conn, scope, changes, actor)` on the
`source_lines` pattern: lock in id order, call `assert_current_upstream`, refuse 409
`over_open_quantity` with the figures, and apply `ordered_qty += delta`. **A draft PO holds its
quantity** (same reasoning as E12-S01). Deleting the draft or lowering a line returns it.

## Creation

`POST /api/v1/purchase-orders` (Idempotency-Key, `purchase_order.create` on the unit), body
`{award_id, lines: [{award_line_id, quantity}]}`. **All lines must belong to one vendor**: one PO per
vendor, or 422 `mixed_vendors`. The award must be `approved`. The vendor's eligibility (E11-S11)
for the unit is checked: not eligible → 409 `vendor_not_eligible`, unless `override_reason` and
`rfq.approve` on the unit (MASTER §7.14; audited). Bill To and Ship To come from the masters, never
typed (decision 165). The recipients default to `vendors.service.document_recipients` (stored on
the PO, E13-S09 sends).

Also `GET /purchase-orders/awarded-lines?award_id=&vendor_id=` (open to order per award line,
grouped by vendor), `GET /purchase-orders/{id}`, `PATCH /purchase-orders/{id}` (draft: terms,
needed-by, Ship To from the allowed list, line quantities through `order_lines`), and
`GET /purchase-orders?tab=&cursor=` (a paged list with counts per tab for E13-S12).

Register the `purchase_order` link-graph node (with `access`) and the `award` provider check that
E12-S09 uses to refuse supersede once a PO exists.

## Acceptance

- [ ] Migrations up, down and data preservation.
- [ ] Numbering: two units each get their own sequence; numbers are unique per unit and never reused.
- [ ] Guard: under, equal and above open → 409 with figures; partial PO reduces only that line's open quantity; deleting the draft returns it.
- [ ] **Concurrency (real Postgres):** two POs for the last 5 units of one award line at once → exactly one.
- [ ] Mixed vendors → 422; unapproved award → 409; stale requisition / pending amendment / expired codes → 409 from `assert_current_upstream`.
- [ ] Ineligible vendor refused; overridden by `rfq.approve` with a reason, audited.
- [ ] Line project = the sub-project (D-M2-26), and codes copied from the requisition line.
- [ ] TEN-010 and authorization per role (purchasing-user creates; sourcing-specialist cannot).
- [ ] `flo check` green.
