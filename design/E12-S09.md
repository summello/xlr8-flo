## Requirements (inlined)

- **SRC-017** — Cancelling or superseding an RFQ/award must preserve the complete history and reconcile its downstream budget effect.
- **WF-005** — a cancelled document keeps its history and stays readable.
- **FIN-011** — a release happens exactly once.

## Decided here

- **Cancel RFQ** (`rfq.approve` on the unit, reason required): allowed from `draft`, `published`, `closed` and `under_evaluation`; never once `awarded` (supersede the award instead). → `cancelled`. Invitees of a published RFQ are told through the outbox (one email per recipient, idempotent). Bids and scores stay readable. **Quantity:** each RFQ line returns its quantity through `source_lines` (negative delta). A requisition that was `sourced` and now has open quantity moves back to `sourcing_in_progress` through `requisitions.service.mark_unsourced`, audited. *(Quantity is not money; E12-S01 already assumes it.)*
- **Cancel an award** (`approval_pending` or `draft`, by `rfq.approve`): → `cancelled`; nothing was posted, so nothing reverses.
- **Supersede an approved award** (`rfq.approve`, reason required): the award → `superseded`, the RFQ → `under_evaluation`, and a new draft award may be created. It is refused (409 `po_exists`) when any PO has been created from the award (E13 registers that check through a link provider).
- History: every transition is audited, and the RFQ and award detail remain readable with their versions.
