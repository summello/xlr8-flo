**Epic** E12 · **M3** · **ledger/M** · after **E12-S08** · tags: `money` (Opus review) · decided 19 Oct (DECISIONS 212, 213)

> **Decisions:** canvas DECISIONS **212** (cancelling an RFQ leaves the reservation with the
> requisition) and **213** (superseding an award reverses its approval's release and return
> exactly), operator, 19 Oct 2026, `design/E12-S19.md` item 3.
> **Writes:** every `PATCH`/`PUT`/`DELETE` on an editable resource follows `docs/m2-conventions.md` §O (`If-Match` with the integer version, `ETag` on reads, 400 when missing, 409 `stale_version` on mismatch). `Idempotency-Key` protects **POST** commands only (the middleware ignores other methods); where a packet says "(Idempotency-Key)" on a non-POST route, read §O instead.
> **RLS:** every new tenant table gets literal `ALTER TABLE <t> ENABLE ROW LEVEL SECURITY; … FORCE …; CREATE POLICY tenant_isolation ON <t> …` statements in the migration text, one block per table, never generated by a helper or f-string (the static gate in `tests/isolation/test_ci_guards.py` reads the text; the light check runs it).

## Requirements (inlined)

- **SRC-017** — Cancelling or superseding an RFQ/award must preserve the complete history and reconcile its downstream budget effect.
- **WF-005** — a cancelled document keeps its history and stays readable.
- **FIN-011** — a release happens exactly once.

## Decided here

- **Cancel RFQ** (`rfq.approve` on the unit, reason required): allowed from `draft`, `published`, `closed` and `under_evaluation`; never once `awarded` (supersede the award instead). → `cancelled`. Invitees of a published RFQ are told through the outbox (one email per recipient, idempotent). Bids and scores stay readable. **Quantity:** each RFQ line returns its quantity through `source_lines` (negative delta). A requisition that was `sourced` and now has open quantity moves back to `sourcing_in_progress` through `requisitions.service.mark_unsourced`, **new in this story** (the inverse of `mark_sourced` in `requisitions/sourcing.py`: lock the row, move only from `sourced` when some non-cancelled line has `sourced_qty <> quantity - cancelled_qty`, `write_status` audited, return whether it moved; export it from `requisitions/service.py` beside `mark_sourced`). *(Quantity is not money; E12-S01 already assumes it.)* **Email:** template id `rfq-cancelled-v1` (constant beside `TEMPLATE = "rfq-version-v1"` in `sourcing/lifecycle.py`), enqueued the same way as the version notice, with a local fixture `apps/api/templates/email/rfq_cancelled.html` mirroring `rfq_version.html` (`rfq_number`, `title`, `reason`); one `outbox.add_email` per contact from `self._recipients(vendor_id, contact_ids)` for every `(vendor_id, contact_ids, _)` in `self._invitees(id)` (the helper's existing signature; do not change it), idempotency key `rfq:{rfq_id}:cancelled:{contact_id}` (outbox keys are unique per tenant; a vendor may have several contacts).
- **Cancel an award** (`draft` only, by `rfq.approve`, reason required): → `cancelled`; nothing was posted, so nothing reverses. An award at `approval_pending` is refused (409 `invalid_transition`): withdraw it first through the existing `POST /rfqs/{id}/award/withdraw` (RFQ Edit, ends the approval instance WITHDRAWN and returns the award to `draft`), then cancel. *(Planner, 27 Oct, from pre-flight: this composes the existing withdraw rule instead of giving `rfq.approve` a second way to end an approval; widening it is the operator's call.)*
- **Supersede an approved award** (`rfq.approve`, reason required): the award → `superseded`, the RFQ → `under_evaluation`, and a new draft award may be created. It is refused (409 `po_exists`) when any PO has been created from the award. E13 is not built yet, so this story adds the hook, not the PO query: `sourcing.service.register_supersede_guard(probe)` where `probe(conn, scope, award_id) -> bool` returns True when a PO exists for that award; a module-level list, empty by default, checked inside the supersede transaction before any ledger write (any True → 409 `po_exists`). E13 registers its probe from its own wiring (purchasing imports `sourcing.service`, never the reverse). The test registers a stub probe and removes it afterwards. (Requisition link providers are display nodes per requisition and cannot answer this per award.)
- History: every transition is audited, and the RFQ and award detail remain readable with their versions. `GET /rfqs/{id}/award` keeps returning the live award (else the latest); a new `GET /rfqs/{id}/awards` (`ViewContext`, as `GET /rfqs/{id}/award`) lists every award of the RFQ newest first, superseded and cancelled included, each as `AwardRead` built the way `AwardService.read` builds it, from the rows `award_by_id` reads, so a superseded award stays readable after its replacement draft exists.

## Routes (all `POST` commands: `Idempotency-Key`, CSRF and the global MFA gate as every sourcing POST, nothing new to wire; no `If-Match`)

| Route | Context | Body | Success | Refusals |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `POST /rfqs/{id}/cancel` | `ApproveContext` | `{reason}` (1..2000, required) | 200 `RfqRead` | 409 `invalid_transition` (awarded, cancelled), 400 missing reason, 404 foreign tenant |
| `POST /rfqs/{id}/award/cancel` | `ApproveContext` | `{reason}` | 200 `AwardRead` | 409 `invalid_transition` (not `draft`) |
| `POST /rfqs/{id}/award/supersede` | `ApproveContext` | `{reason}` | 200 `AwardRead` (the superseded award) | 409 `invalid_transition`, 409 `po_exists`, `InsufficientBudget` problem |
| `GET /rfqs/{id}/awards` | `ViewContext` | — | 200 `list[AwardRead]` | 404 foreign tenant |

## Budget effect (decided 19 Oct 2026)

| Event | Quantity | Reservation and ledger |
|---|---|---|
| Cancel RFQ | returned (above) | **None** (decision 212). The reservation stays with the requisition, which can be sourced again. No ledger entry. Cancelling the requisition itself (M2) is still what releases it. |
| Supersede approved award | each awarded line's `awarded_qty` is given back through `requisitions.service.award_lines` (negative delta); a requisition at `awarded`/`open_for_purchase` returns to `sourced` through a new `requisitions.service.mark_unawarded`, audited | **Exact reversal of E12-S08 step 3** (decision 213), below. |

**Supersede, in one transaction, per requisition on the award:**

1. `requisitions.funding.lock_funding` (main project and the project holding the live reservation, in id order), then the requisition row `FOR UPDATE`, **before** any amount is read.
2. `returned` = the amount E12-S08 moved sub → main for this award and requisition: the `:in` leg on the main project posted under idempotency key `award:{award_id}:return:{requisition_id}`. None or 0 → skip steps 3 and 4 (the award used the whole scope).
3. Transfer `returned` **main → sub** with `requisitions.funding.move_funds`, key `award:{award_id}:supersede:return:{requisition_id}`. When the main project no longer has it available, `post_legs` raises the existing `InsufficientBudget` problem; the whole supersede rolls back (award still `approved`, no ledger row, no quantity change).
4. `requisitions.service.rereserve(conn, scope, requisition_id, amount=returned, key=f"award:{award_id}:supersede", actor_id)`: **new in this story**, the shared helper E13-S06 and E13-S07 also call. It follows the resubmit pattern in `requisitions/lifecycle.py` (`release_live`, then `budget.reservations.reserve` of `remaining + amount` on the reservation's project with `funding_round + 1`, key `req:{id}:reserve:{round}`, and the requisition's `reservation_entry_id`/`funding_round` updated). Never a second live reservation.
5. Quantity and status as in the table, then the award → `superseded` and the RFQ → `under_evaluation`.

Exactly once (FIN-011): a second supersede of the same award is refused by the state machine (409 `invalid_transition`) and posts nothing; the idempotency keys above make a retried transaction post nothing new.

## Acceptance

- [ ] State machine for every valid and invalid transition.
- [ ] Quantities returned exactly; `sourced` → `sourcing_in_progress` when open quantity reappears.
- [ ] Invitees told once (outbox).
- [ ] Supersede refused once a PO exists (stub probe registered through `register_supersede_guard`; with the list empty, supersede proceeds).
- [ ] Cancel an `approval_pending` award → 409 `invalid_transition`; withdraw then cancel → `cancelled`.
- [ ] After supersede and a new draft award, `GET /rfqs/{id}/awards` returns both, the superseded one with its status and versions.
- [ ] **Cancel RFQ posts nothing:** `ledger_entry` row count and the requisition's reservation remaining are unchanged by a cancel from each allowed state.
- [ ] **Supersede reverses exactly (hand-computed):** scope 1,000, award 900, approved (E12-S08: one release of 100 and one transfer of 100 sub → main). Supersede → one transfer of 100 main → sub; the requisition's live reservation remaining is **1,000** on a new reservation entry (`funding_round` + 1); `awarded_qty` back to 0 on every line; requisition `sourced`; **drift is empty** (`budget/reconcile`).
- [ ] **Award that used the whole scope:** scope 1,000, award 1,000 → supersede posts no transfer and no new reservation; quantity and status still reverse.
- [ ] **Main project spent since:** after approval, spend the main project's available balance below 100; supersede → `InsufficientBudget`, award still `approved`, ledger row count unchanged, quantities unchanged.
- [ ] **Concurrency (real Postgres):** two supersedes of the same award at once → exactly one succeeds, one transfer and one new reservation in the ledger.
- [ ] Supersede twice → 409 `invalid_transition`, no ledger row.
- [ ] TEN-010 and authorization per role; `flo check` green.

## Blocked

Pre-flight 27 Oct 2026 (Codex, packet 74c9e5159b), state transition — operator decides, the author must not guess:
cancelling an `under_evaluation` RFQ while it has a `draft` or `approval_pending` award. Today `award.approve`
does not check the RFQ status and sets it to `awarded` (`sourcing/awards.py` ~810, ~840), so a cancelled RFQ
could be resurrected. Options: (a) refuse cancel with 409 while any non-terminal award exists (withdraw or reject
it first); (b) cancel also terminates the live award and its approval instance, releasing any award-held reservation
exactly once. Either way `award.approve` should refuse on a non-`under_evaluation` RFQ.
