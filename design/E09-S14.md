# E09-S14 — M2 exit criterion end to end: requisition through a multi-level conditional workflow

**Epic** E09 · **M2** · **test/S** · after **E09-S12** · tags: none

## Why
M2's exit criterion (docs/claude-plan.md §4): "A requisition moves Draft→Approved through a
multi-level conditional workflow, generates its sub-project, and reserves budget atomically."
Each part is tested on its own (conditional routing in `tests/approvals/test_engine.py`; approval
effects with a single-step workflow in `tests/requisitions/test_approval_effects.py`; reserve at
submit in `test_submit_concurrency.py`). No test runs the whole path in one go.

## Requirements
- **WF-002** — routing evaluates the workflow's conditions on the document's attributes.
- **WF-003** — the document transition and its financial entries commit atomically.
- **REQ-002** — approval of a project-linked requisition creates a requisition-generated sub-project; the reservation (made at submit, D-M2-15) stays on the parent.
- **ACC-001** — reservations never exceed available budget.

## The test (one new file: `apps/api/tests/requisitions/test_exit_criterion.py`)
Reuse the existing fixtures and helpers (`lifecycle_db`, `project`, `unit`, `linked`, the
approvals `publish`/graph helpers in `tests/approvals/`); add no new fixture module.

1. Publish a `requisition` workflow with a **condition** on the estimate: `estimate > 1000` goes to
   **two sequential approval steps** (approver A, then approver B); otherwise one step (approver A).
2. **High branch:** create a project-linked requisition with estimate 1500 against a project with
   2000 available; submit. Assert: status `approval_pending`; one reservation of 1500 on the
   project; the instance's route has two steps (A then B).
3. A approves: still `approval_pending`, no sub-project yet, reservation unchanged, task now with B.
4. B approves: status `sourcing_in_progress` (or the approved state the code uses); exactly one
   generated sub-project under the project (`generated_by_requisition_id` = the requisition, no
   allocation or reservation of its own); the parent still holds exactly one 1500 reservation;
   ledger drift is empty.
5. **Low branch:** a second requisition with estimate 300 routes to one step; A approves; approved
   with its sub-project.
6. **Atomicity:** make the sub-project insert fail on B's approval (monkeypatch the generate call
   to raise); assert the decision, the status change and every ledger/project row roll back, the
   task is still open with B, and the reservation is unchanged.
