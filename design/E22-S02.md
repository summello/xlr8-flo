# E22-S02 — Soft delete for every draft document

**Epic** E22 · **M3** · **crud/M** · after **E09-S15** · tags: `ui`, `migration`

> Decision: `design/E09-S13.md` decision 12 (D-M2-27). A Bin view is on M7.

## Design
- A `deleted` status (or `deleted_at` where a status machine does not fit) for drafts of: requisitions and
  Project Requests, projects, approval workflow drafts, budget change requests. Amendment drafts already delete.
- `DELETE /api/v1/<resource>/{id}` (Idempotency-Key) allowed only from draft, by whoever may edit the draft;
  409 `not_draft` otherwise; foreign tenant 404. Audited (`<resource>.delete`). Numbers are never reused.
- Every list and lookup excludes deleted rows; a direct GET returns 404 to everyone except holders of
  `record.read` with `include_deleted=true` (for the future Bin).
- UI: Delete draft action with a confirm dialog on each detail screen; the S11 "discard" path uses it.

## Decision

Operator, 24 Oct 2026 (arbitration after round 5): **a draft project deletes whatever its
`project_balance` shows.** Funds on a draft are not appropriated until the project is approved or
active, so deleting a draft strands nothing and needs no balance check. A finding that a draft
project's delete must refuse or move budget is out of scope for this story. That
`budget/transfers.py` lets a transfer fund a `draft` receiving node contradicts this rule; it is a
follow-up story, not this one.
