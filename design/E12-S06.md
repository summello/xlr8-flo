## Requirements (inlined)

## Rules (all `Decimal`, quantized only at the end through `kernel/money.quantize` in the comparison currency)

- **Comparison currency** = the RFQ's currency (the requisition lines' currency, E12-S01).
- **Rate:** `org.fx.rate_for(conn, bid.currency, rfq.currency, on)` where `on` is the date the
  comparison was **frozen** (below). Store `Conversion(rate, source, effective_date)` per bid.
  `stale = (today − effective_date) > 7 days`. `POST …/comparison/refresh-rate`, available to any
  holder of `rfq.edit` on the unit (decision 117, tightened 25 Oct 2026 — not `rfq.view`: that also
  sits on Auditor, and an Auditor never takes action, only records observations), re-freezes at
  today's rate (audited).
- **Freeze:** the first comparison read after the deadline freezes the inputs into
  `rfq_comparison(rfq_id, frozen_at, rates jsonb)`. Later reads use the frozen rates until refreshed,
  so the figures do not drift while people decide.
- **Per line, per vendor:** `price = unit_price × qty`; `tax` as keyed; freight is pro rated only
  across lines with a nonzero `price` — `freight share = bid.freight × (line price / Σ nonzero line
  prices of that bid)` (decision 116); a line priced at 0 gets `freight_share = 0` (operator, 25 Oct
  2026: freight doesn't apply to a 0-price item). If every line is priced at 0, no line can take a
  share: report the bid's whole freight as `freight_unallocated` on the vendor (not folded into any
  landed line) and include it once in that vendor's `landed total`. `landed line = (price + tax +
  freight share) × rate`. Unquoted → `not_quoted`, with no figure.
- **Per vendor:** `landed total = Σ landed lines` (+ `freight_unallocated` if any); `full = quoted
  every line`; `difference from lowest` among full bids; `met n of N` (waived requirements excluded
  from N); `lead_time_days` against the earliest line `needed_by`, counted **from the comparison
  date** (the date this read runs — operator, 25 Oct 2026: that's when the RFQ is ready for a
  purchase order, not the bid's `sent_at` or the RFQ deadline); and `score` from E12-S07 when
  available (`null` until then).
- **Markers:** `lowest` (among **full** bids only; a partial bid can still be lowest on a **line**),
  `fastest`, `highest_score`. Ties → `tied: true` and the earlier `sent_at` wins the marker (decision
  115); equal `sent_at` → the lower `bid.id` (UUID byte order) wins, so the result is deterministic. A **buyer override** of a tie is an E12-S08 concern.
- **Award bases** (decision 100), both always returned:
  - `lowest_cost_supplier`: per line, the vendor with the lowest landed line (ties as above). Total = Σ.
  - `best_average_cost`: the full bid with the lowest landed total. **If no bid is full** (operator,
    25 Oct 2026): drop the full-bid restriction and take the lowest landed total among all quoted
    (partial included) bids instead, with `partial: true` on that basis so the UI and the award
    (E12-S08) can flag it. Total = its landed total either way.
  - `insight`: the difference between the two totals and the vendor counts, plus `difference_pct`
    (operator, 25 Oct 2026: add it, server-side — **denominator and rounding still open**, see
    `## Blocked`).
- **Scope figure:** `approved_scope` = Σ over the RFQ's requisition lines of
  `requisition_line.amount × (rfq_line.quantity / requisition_line.quantity)` in the requisition
  currency. Return `within_scope` / `over_by` for each basis (SRC-016). The award re-checks it under
  the lock.
- **Late bids** are included only if accepted (E12-S05). **Sealed:** before the deadline the
  endpoint returns the sealed shape only (call `sealing.visible`).

## API

`GET /api/v1/sourcing/rfqs/{id}/comparison` (`rfq.view` on the unit, plus evaluators): verdict per
vendor, the matrix rows (with `identical: bool` for `Show Only Differences`), by-line, cost breakdown
(price, tax and duty, freight, total, plus `approved_scope`), conversion notes, markers, both bases,
and `stale_rate`. `POST …/comparison/refresh-rate` (`rfq.edit` on the unit).

## Acceptance

- [ ] **Engine unit tests against hand-computed values** (a fixture of 3 vendors, 4 lines, one partial, one foreign currency, freight on two): every landed line, total, difference, marker and both bases match `Decimal` literals in the test. Include a tie, decided by `sent_at`. Include a bid with one zero-price line (that line's `freight_share == 0`, the rest absorb its share) and a bid with every line at zero price (`freight_unallocated == bid.freight`, folded once into `landed total`).
- [ ] A fixture with no full bid: `best_average_cost` is the lowest landed total among the partial bids, `partial: true`, and `insight`/`within_scope` are computed against it.
- [ ] Freight pro rata sums back exactly to the bid's freight: use `kernel/money.allocate` as-is, residual to the earliest ratio (operator, 25 Oct 2026 — not the largest line). **Which currency the sum is checked in is still open**, see `## Blocked`.
- [ ] A partial bid is never `lowest` overall, but can be lowest on a line.
- [ ] Rate: the conversion note carries rate, source and effective date; a rate 8 days old → `stale_rate: true`; refresh re-freezes and is audited; the bid tables are unchanged (SRC-010).
- [ ] Sealed: before the deadline the endpoint returns no figure (reuse E12-S05's assertion).
- [ ] TEN-010 and authorization per role.
- [ ] `flo check` green.

## Plants

1. Mark `lowest` across partial bids: the partial test fails.
2. Convert before adding freight share at a different rate: the hand-computed totals fail.
3. Recompute rates on every read: the freeze test fails.

## Blocked

Codex pre-flight 24 Oct 2026 (`.scratch/E12-S06.preflight.log`) raised six money/authorization
questions. Operator ruled 25 Oct 2026 — resolved into the Rules/API/Acceptance sections above:
refresh-rate needs `rfq.edit`, not `rfq.view` (Auditors never take action, only record
observations); freight residual follows `kernel/money.allocate`'s earliest-ratio rule; a 0-price
line gets no freight share (`freight_unallocated` when every line is 0); `best_average_cost` drops
the full-bid restriction when no bid is full, flagged `partial: true`; `lead_time_days` counts from
the comparison date (when the RFQ is ready for a PO); `difference_pct` is added server-side.

**Still open — needs more explanation before a ruling, do not guess:**

1. **Which currency is the freight-sum check in** — the bid's own currency (pre-conversion) or the
   comparison currency (post-conversion, decision 116's pro-rata already runs there)? Both are
   defensible; this changes where rounding error can appear.
2. **`difference_pct`'s denominator and rounding.** Candidates: the higher total, the lower total,
   or the `best_average_cost` total — each tells a buyer a different story ("X% more expensive than
   the best bid" vs. "X% cheaper than the alternative"), and the rounding/quantization rule isn't
   decided either.

**Codex pre-flight 25 Oct 2026, re-run against the fixed packet (`.scratch/E12-S06.preflight.log`)
— 5 packet defects against the real code, for the planner to fix (not operator decisions):**

3. **`rfq_comparison` has no `org_id`.** The table is specified with only `rfq_id, frozen_at, rates`
   (line 32), but every tenant table needs `org_id` for its RLS policy (every existing tenant table
   has one, e.g. `migrations/20261022_0071_bids.py:47`). Add the column and its
   `CREATE POLICY tenant_isolation` block.
4. **The freeze doesn't freeze.** "Later reads use the frozen rates... so the figures do not drift"
   (line 32) is false as written: a bid can still be submitted/accepted while the RFQ is `closed` or
   `under_evaluation` (`apps/api/src/flo/modules/sourcing/bids.py:471`, proven by
   `apps/api/tests/sourcing/test_sealed.py:267`), which changes the comparison inputs with no
   refresh triggered. Decide and state what re-opens the freeze — a late/accepted bid writing a new
   bid row after `frozen_at`, or something narrower — and encode it in the Rules.
5. **No `duty` field exists.** The API promises a "tax and duty" breakdown (line 69), but bid
   schemas and the `bids` table (migration 0071) carry only `tax` — no `duty` anywhere. Either add
   the column/schema field or drop "and duty" from the API description.
6. **`Conversion(rate, source, effective_date)` is invented.** The real return type of
   `org.fx.rate_for` is `FxRateRead` (`apps/api/src/flo/modules/org/schemas.py:164`), and the
   module's own `Conversion` (`apps/api/src/flo/modules/org/fx.py:69`) also carries `amount,
   from_ccy, to_ccy`. Rewrite the Rate rule against the real type, not a shape made up for the
   packet.
7. **`kernel/money.allocate` as named doesn't exist and wouldn't take this input anyway.** It's
   `Money.allocate`, takes integer ratios, and rejects amounts not already quantized to currency
   minor units (`apps/api/src/flo/kernel/money.py:100`) — but bid freight is stored at 4 decimal
   places, unquantized (`apps/api/src/flo/modules/sourcing/bids.py:405`). Fix the call site/name and
   decide how freight gets quantized before allocation (and in which currency — see item 1 above,
   they're the same unresolved question).
