## Requirements (inlined)

## Rules (all `Decimal`; rounding through `kernel/money.quantize`, HALF_UP)

- **Rounding (operator, 25 Oct 2026): per converted component.** For each line, `price × rate`,
  `tax × rate` and `freight share × rate` are each quantized once in the RFQ currency; the landed
  line is their **sum**, and every vendor total, basis total and breakdown column is a sum of those
  quantized components, so the breakdown reconciles exactly. The only other rounding steps are freight's and `approved_scope`'s, each stated below; none exists
  (freight's bid-currency quantize below is the only pre-conversion one).

- **Comparison currency** = the RFQ's currency (the requisition lines' currency, E12-S01).
- **Rate:** `org.service.rate_for(conn, bid.currency, rfq.currency, on)` (re-exported at
  `modules/org/service.py:34`) where `on` is the comparison's `rate_date` (below: the RFQ deadline's date until a refresh). It
  returns `FxRateRead(base, quote, rate: str, source, effective_date)`
  (`modules/org/schemas.py:164`); use `Decimal(r.rate)`. Same currency returns `rate="1"`,
  `source="identity"`. Do **not** use `org.service.convert`: it quantizes each amount
  (`modules/org/fx.py:221`), and this engine quantizes each converted component once (Rounding, above). The conversion note per
  bid is the `FxRateRead` fields (rate, source, effective date, base, quote).
  `stale = (today − effective_date) > 7 days`. `POST …/comparison/refresh-rate`, available to any
  holder of `rfq.edit` on the unit (decision 117, tightened 25 Oct 2026 — not `rfq.view`: that also
  sits on Auditor, and an Auditor never takes action, only records observations), re-freezes at
  today's rate (audited).
- **Freeze — the rate date is the RFQ deadline's date** (operator, 25 Oct 2026): `rate_date =
  (rfq.deadline_at AT TIME ZONE rfq.deadline_tz)::date`, so no reader chooses it — a GET is
  available to `rfq.view` (Auditor included) and skips CSRF and idempotency, so it must never pick
  a date. The first read after the deadline only **caches** that deterministic result (an
  idempotent write: the figures would be identical without it, `INSERT … ON CONFLICT (org_id,
  rfq_id) DO NOTHING`) into
  `rfq_comparison(org_id uuid NOT NULL REFERENCES organization(id), rfq_id uuid NOT NULL,
  rate_date date NOT NULL, frozen_at timestamptz NOT NULL, rates jsonb NOT NULL, row_version int
  NOT NULL DEFAULT 1)`,
  `PRIMARY KEY (org_id, rfq_id)`, `FOREIGN KEY (org_id, rfq_id) REFERENCES rfq(org_id, id)`, with
  the literal ENABLE / FORCE / `CREATE POLICY tenant_isolation` block shaped like
  `migrations/20261022_0071_bids.py:47-51`. `rates` is keyed by **currency**, not by bid:
  `{"USD": {"rate": "…", "source": "…", "effective_date": "YYYY-MM-DD"}}`. Later reads use the
  frozen rates until refreshed, so existing figures do not drift while people decide.
  **The freeze pins rates only; bids are read live** (operator, 25 Oct 2026). A bid can still be
  submitted after `frozen_at` — late with an accepted override (`modules/sourcing/bids.py:525`)
  or sent on time and keyed later. It joins the comparison at the frozen date's rate: if its
  currency is already in `rates`, that rate; if not, take `SELECT … FOR UPDATE` on the
  `rfq_comparison` row, add `rate_for(conn, ccy, rfq.currency, rate_date)` to `rates`, and
  leave `rate_date` and `frozen_at` unchanged. The response carries `bids_since_freeze: int` (accepted bids with
  `submitted_at > frozen_at`) so the screen can say the set changed. Only `refresh-rate` (POST: CSRF, `Idempotency-Key`, `rfq.edit`, audited with the old and new
  `rate_date`) moves the date, to today, and re-takes every currency's rate.
- **Per line, per vendor:** `price = unit_price × bid_line.quantity` (operator, 25 Oct 2026: the
  quantity the vendor priced, kept per bid version, `modules/sourcing/bids.py:125`; never the
  amended `rfq_line.quantity`). Each vendor also carries `rfq_version` and `on_current_version:
  bool` so the screen can flag a bid answering an older version; `approved_scope` still uses
  `rfq_line.quantity`. Unquantized `price` here; it is quantized after conversion; `tax` as keyed; freight is pro rated only
  across lines with a nonzero `price` — `freight share = bid.freight × (line price / Σ nonzero line
  prices of that bid)` (decision 116); a line priced at 0 gets `freight_share = 0` (operator, 25 Oct
  2026: freight doesn't apply to a 0-price item). If every line is priced at 0, no line can take a
  share: report the bid's whole freight as `freight_unallocated` on the vendor (not folded into any
  landed line) and include it once in that vendor's `landed total` as `quantize(freight_unallocated × rate)`
  (the bid's frozen rate, quantized once in the RFQ currency like any component). `landed line = q(price × rate) + q(tax × rate) + q(freight share × rate)`, `q` =
  `quantize(·, rfq.currency)`. Unquoted → `not_quoted`, with
  no figure.
- **Freight allocation runs in the bid's own currency** (operator, 25 Oct 2026), before
  conversion: `fq = kernel.money.quantize(bid.freight, bid.currency)` (HALF_UP; bid freight is
  stored at 4 dp, `modules/sourcing/bids.py:405`, so at most half a minor unit is lost here, and
  the stored bid is never altered — SRC-010); ratios are the **exact unquantized** line prices as
  integers, `int(price.scaleb(8))` (`unit_price` and `quantity` are each `NUMERIC(18,4)`, so
  `price` has at most 8 decimals and this is exact); every positive price, even `0.0010`, gets a
  positive ratio, and only a price of exactly 0 gets ratio 0; shares = `Money(fq, bid.currency).allocate(ratios)` (`kernel/money.py:100`:
  integer ratios only, whole minor units only, residual to the earliest nonzero ratio in line
  order). Every ratio 0 → skip `allocate` (it rejects an all-zero list) and set
  `freight_unallocated = fq`.
- **Which bids:** only **current** bids — `status = 'submitted'` and no submitted bid replaces it
  (the rule `BidRead.current` already uses, `modules/sourcing/bids.py:202`, `:269`), so one vendor
  counts once. Drafts and superseded bids are never read. This set is also what
  `bids_since_freeze` counts.
- **Per vendor:** `landed total = Σ landed lines` (+ `quantize(freight_unallocated × rate)` if any); `full = quoted
  every line`; `difference from lowest` = landed total − the lowest landed total of the set `best_average_cost`
  draws from (full bids; all quoted bids when none is full) — `null` for a partial bid while any
  full bid exists; `met n of N` (waived requirements excluded
  from N); `lead_time_days` against the earliest line `needed_by`, counted **from the comparison
  date** (the date this read runs — operator, 25 Oct 2026: that's when the RFQ is ready for a
  purchase order, not the bid's `sent_at` or the RFQ deadline): `ready_by = comparison date +
  lead_time_days`, `meets_need = ready_by <= min(needed_by)`. `lead_time_days` null (nullable on
  `bid`) → `ready_by` and `meets_need` null and the bid is not a `fastest` candidate. No line has a
  `needed_by` → `meets_need` null for every bid, while `ready_by` is still reported; and `score` from E12-S07 when
  available (`null` until then).
- **Markers:** `lowest` (among **full** bids only; a partial bid can still be lowest on a **line**),
  `fastest` (lowest `lead_time_days` among bids that state one), `highest_score` (among scored
  bids). A marker with no candidate is `null`, not omitted. Ties → `tied: true` and the earlier `sent_at` wins the marker (decision
  115); equal `sent_at` → the lower `bid.id` (UUID byte order) wins, so the result is deterministic. A **buyer override** of a tie is an E12-S08 concern.
- **Empty comparison:** an RFQ can close with no current bid (`modules/sourcing/lifecycle.py:417`).
  Then `vendors: []`, every line `not_quoted` by everyone, every marker `null`, each basis returned
  as `{vendor_id: null, total: null, partial: false, within_scope: null, over_by: null}`, and
  `insight: null`. `lowest_cost_supplier` gives a line nobody quoted no winner; its total covers
  the quoted lines only, with `uncovered_lines: [rfq_line_id…]` listing the rest.
- **Award bases** (decision 100), both always returned:
  - `lowest_cost_supplier`: per line, the vendor with the lowest landed line (ties as above). Total = Σ.
  - `best_average_cost`: the full bid with the lowest landed total. **If no bid is full** (operator,
    25 Oct 2026): drop the full-bid restriction and take the lowest landed total among all quoted (a bid quoting **no** line is never a candidate for either basis or any marker — submission allows it, `modules/sourcing/bids.py:491` — and appears only as `not_quoted` throughout)
    (partial included) bids instead, with `partial: true` on that basis so the UI and the award
    (E12-S08) can flag it. Total = its landed total either way.
  - `insight`: the difference between the two totals and the vendor counts, plus `difference_pct`
    (operator, 25 Oct 2026): `(higher − lower) / lower × 100`, `quantize(Decimal("0.01"),
    ROUND_HALF_UP)`, returned as a string (`"12.50"`, read "the dearer basis costs 12.50% more");
    `null` when the lower total is 0. Same shape as `modules/budget/queries.py:30`.
- **Scope figure:** `approved_scope` = Σ over the RFQ's requisition lines of
  `quantize(requisition_line.amount × rfq_line.quantity / requisition_line.quantity, currency)` —
  each term quantized once (HALF_UP) in the requisition currency, which is the RFQ currency, then
  summed, the same per-component rule as above. `over_by = max(0, basis total − approved_scope)`,
  both already in minor units; `within_scope = over_by == 0`. Return `within_scope` / `over_by` for each basis (SRC-016). The award re-checks it under
  the lock.
- **Late bids** are included only if accepted (E12-S05). **Sealed:** before the deadline the
  endpoint returns the sealed shape only (call `sealing.visible`).

## API

`GET /api/v1/sourcing/rfqs/{id}/comparison` (`rfq.view` on the unit, plus evaluators): verdict per
vendor, the matrix rows (with `identical: bool` for `Show Only Differences`), by-line, cost breakdown
(price, tax, freight, total, plus `approved_scope`; no duty — bids carry no duty field), conversion
notes, markers, both bases, `stale_rate` and `bids_since_freeze`. `POST …/comparison/refresh-rate` (`rfq.edit` on the unit).

## Acceptance

- [ ] **Engine unit tests against hand-computed values** (a fixture of 3 vendors, 4 lines, one partial, one foreign currency, freight on two): every landed line, total, difference, marker and both bases match `Decimal` literals in the test. Include a tie, decided by `sent_at`. Include a bid with one zero-price line (that line's `freight_share == 0`, the rest absorb its share) and a bid with every line at zero price (`freight_unallocated == quantize(bid.freight, bid.currency)`, folded once into `landed total`).
- [ ] Breakdown reconciles: for every vendor, Σ price + Σ tax + Σ freight (+ unallocated) == landed total, to the minor unit.
- [ ] An amended RFQ (line quantity changed after a bid): that bid is priced at its `bid_line.quantity`, `on_current_version: false`.
- [ ] No full bid: every vendor's `difference from lowest` is against the lowest partial total.
- [ ] A foreign-currency bid with every line at zero price: its `landed total == quantize(fq × rate, rfq.currency)`.
- [ ] A vendor with a replaced bid is counted once, at the replacement's figures.
- [ ] A bid with no `lead_time_days` is never `fastest` and has `ready_by: null`; no current bids → the empty shape above, HTTP 200.
- [ ] A fixture with no full bid: `best_average_cost` is the lowest landed total among the partial bids, `partial: true`, and `insight`/`within_scope` are computed against it.
- [ ] Freight pro rata sums back exactly, in the **bid currency**: Σ shares == `quantize(bid.freight, bid.currency)`, via `Money.allocate`, residual to the earliest ratio (not the largest line). The fixture includes a sub-minor freight (`10.0050 USD` → shares sum to `10.01`).
- [ ] `difference_pct` matches a hand-computed literal, and is `null` when the lower total is 0.
- [ ] Rates are taken at the deadline's date in `deadline_tz`, whichever role reads first and whenever; an Auditor's first read writes the same rates a buyer's would. Refresh moves `rate_date` to today and is audited.
- [ ] A submitted bid quoting no line is in no basis and no marker; a `0.0010` line receives a nonzero ratio.
- [ ] `approved_scope` from a 1/3 quantity split matches a hand-computed literal.
- [ ] A bid submitted after the freeze appears in the comparison; the existing `rates` entries and `frozen_at` are unchanged; a new currency is added at the frozen date's rate; `bids_since_freeze == 1`.
- [ ] A partial bid is never `lowest` overall, but can be lowest on a line.
- [ ] Rate: the conversion note carries rate, source and effective date; a rate 8 days old → `stale_rate: true`; refresh re-freezes and is audited; the bid tables are unchanged (SRC-010).
- [ ] Sealed: before the deadline the endpoint returns no figure (reuse E12-S05's assertion).
- [ ] TEN-010 and authorization per role.
- [ ] `flo check` green.
