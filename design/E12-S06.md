## Rules (all `Decimal`, quantized only at the end through `kernel/money.quantize` in the comparison currency)

- **Comparison currency** = the RFQ's currency (the requisition lines' currency, E12-S01).
- **Rate:** `org.fx.rate_for(conn, bid.currency, rfq.currency, on)` where `on` is the date the
  comparison was **frozen** (below). Store `Conversion(rate, source, effective_date)` per bid.
  `stale = (today − effective_date) > 7 days`. `POST …/comparison/refresh-rate`, available to any
  holder of `rfq.view` on the unit (decision 117), re-freezes at today's rate (audited).
- **Freeze:** the first comparison read after the deadline freezes the inputs into
  `rfq_comparison(rfq_id, frozen_at, rates jsonb)`. Later reads use the frozen rates until refreshed,
  so the figures do not drift while people decide.
- **Per line, per vendor:** `price = unit_price × qty`; `tax` as keyed; `freight share = bid.freight
  × (line price / Σ quoted line prices of that bid)` (pro rata by line value, decision 116);
  `landed line = (price + tax + freight share) × rate`. Unquoted → `not_quoted`, with no figure.
- **Per vendor:** `landed total = Σ landed lines`; `full = quoted every line`; `difference from
  lowest` among full bids; `met n of N` (waived requirements excluded from N); `lead_time_days`
  against the earliest line `needed_by`; and `score` from E12-S07 when available (`null` until then).
- **Markers:** `lowest` (among **full** bids only; a partial bid can still be lowest on a **line**),
  `fastest`, `highest_score`. Ties → `tied: true` and the earlier `sent_at` wins the marker (decision
  115); equal `sent_at` → the lower `bid.id` (UUID byte order) wins, so the result is deterministic. A **buyer override** of a tie is an E12-S08 concern.
- **Award bases** (decision 100), both always returned:
  - `lowest_cost_supplier`: per line, the vendor with the lowest landed line (ties as above). Total = Σ.
  - `best_average_cost`: the full bid with the lowest landed total. Total = its landed total.
  - `insight`: the difference between the two totals and the vendor counts.
- **Scope figure:** `approved_scope` = Σ over the RFQ's requisition lines of
  `requisition_line.amount × (rfq_line.quantity / requisition_line.quantity)` in the requisition
  currency. Return `within_scope` / `over_by` for each basis (SRC-016). The award re-checks it under
  the lock.
- **Late bids** are included only if accepted (E12-S05). **Sealed:** before the deadline the
  endpoint returns the sealed shape only (call `sealing.visible`).

## API

`GET /api/v1/sourcing/rfqs/{id}/comparison` (`rfq.view` on the unit, plus evaluators): verdict per
vendor, the matrix rows (with `identical: bool` for `Show Only Differences`), by-line, cost breakdown
(price, tax and duty, freight, total, plus `approved_scope`), conversion notes, markers, both bases,
and `stale_rate`. `POST …/comparison/refresh-rate`.

## Acceptance

- [ ] **Engine unit tests against hand-computed values** (a fixture of 3 vendors, 4 lines, one partial, one foreign currency, freight on two): every landed line, total, difference, marker and both bases match `Decimal` literals in the test. Include a tie, decided by `sent_at`.
- [ ] Freight pro rata sums back exactly to the bid's freight (after quantization, the residual goes to the largest line; state this rule in a `ponytail:` if you choose another).
- [ ] A partial bid is never `lowest` overall, but can be lowest on a line.
- [ ] Rate: the conversion note carries rate, source and effective date; a rate 8 days old → `stale_rate: true`; refresh re-freezes and is audited; the bid tables are unchanged (SRC-010).
- [ ] Sealed: before the deadline the endpoint returns no figure (reuse E12-S05's assertion).
- [ ] TEN-010 and authorization per role.
- [ ] `flo check` green.

## Plants

1. Mark `lowest` across partial bids: the partial test fails.
2. Convert before adding freight share at a different rate: the hand-computed totals fail.
3. Recompute rates on every read: the freeze test fails.

## Blocked

Codex pre-flight 24 Oct 2026 (`.scratch/E12-S06.preflight.log`): six questions are money or
authorization rules, so the operator decides them before this story starts.

1. **Refresh-rate authorization.** Decision 117 gives `POST …/comparison/refresh-rate` to every
   `rfq.view` holder, but every `*.view` permission is read-only and granted to the Auditor role
   (`identity/models.py:222`, `:387`). May an Auditor re-freeze the rate, or does refresh need a
   sourcing write permission (which one)?
2. **Freight rounding.** Shares are quantized in the comparison currency after conversion, but the
   acceptance asks them to sum exactly to the bid-currency freight, residual to the largest line,
   while `kernel/money.allocate` gives the residual to the earliest ratio (`money.py:100`). Which
   currency is the sum checked in, and which residual rule (kernel's or largest line)?
3. **Freight with zero line value.** A bid may quote every line at unit price 0 with freight > 0
   (migration 0071 allows it), so `line price / Σ line prices` divides by zero. Allocate equally,
   by quantity, or show freight unallocated?
4. **No full bid.** If every bid is partial, what is `best_average_cost` (null with a reason, or
   something else), and what do `insight` and `within_scope` report?
5. **Lead-time anchor.** "`lead_time_days` against the earliest `needed_by`": from which date are
   the days counted (bid `sent_at`, RFQ deadline, or the comparison date)?
6. **Insight percentage.** `rfq-comparison.md` shows a percentage the UI may not compute. Add
   `difference_pct` to the server's `insight`? Denominator: the higher total, the lower total, or
   the `best_average_cost` total? Rounding?
