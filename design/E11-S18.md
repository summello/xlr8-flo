## Requirements (inlined)

- **VEN-007** — Vendor performance must be measurable across quality, pricing, delivery/time adherence, service, and configurable categories.
- **VEN-015** — Performance scores must retain source transaction, scorer, period, rationale, and any dispute/adjustment history.

## Design

- **Performance card** on `/vendors/:id` (in the `.v-w`/`.v-n` pair slot E11-S12 leaves): the overall score and the four categories plus any configured ones from `GET /vendors/{id}/performance`, each with the period window and row count, and `Open Scorecard`. Figures are the server's `Decimal` strings, tabular and right-aligned; an empty state when there are no scores.
- **Scorecard sheet** (wide side sheet, not a route; DECISIONS 134): rows of PO reference, category, score, rationale, and the scorer (email, on the line under the rationale), with `Dispute` per row for `vendor.edit`. An adjusted row shows the **struck original** and the adjustment beneath, linked. Disputes are appended rows with their resolution.
- `Add Score` (purchasing users, as the server permits): an inline form with category, score (1..5), period (month), source order and rationale, all required. Field errors and a summary on failure; input kept.
- `Resolve` (vendor managers): upheld requires the adjusted score and rationale; dismissed requires a note.
