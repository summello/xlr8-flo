## Answered by the operator, 15 Oct 2026 (planner session; record in DECISIONS as 6i-9 to 6i-12)

1. **Refuse.** An award with no published award workflow is refused with 409 `workflow_not_set_up` (D-M2-21 applies to awards). This supersedes canvas decision 147's "approved when you submit" notice.
2. **Release at award approval, back to the main project.** The unused reservation is released once when the award is approved, and the released amount is transferred from the requisition's sub-project back to its main project **in the same transaction** (`reduce_to`, then `post_legs` sub → main).
4. **As proposed:** `sourced` when every open line is on a published RFQ; `awarded` **and** `open_for_purchase` together when every line's quantity is awarded on approved awards; `completed` when every line is ordered (received, from M4).
5. **No approval for RFQ publish.** It is the buyer's act, audited.

**Answered 19 Oct 2026 (planner session; DECISIONS 212, 213):**

3. **Cancel RFQ: the reservation stays with the requisition** (no ledger entry; the quantity returns). **Supersede an approved award: exact reversal**: the awarded quantity is given back as open, and the amount the award's approval released and returned to the main project is transferred back main → sub-project and re-reserved, in one transaction; refused with `InsufficientBudget` when the main project no longer has it. E12-S09 and E12-S18 are unblocked.

Items 6 to 10 remain open and ship with their stated assumptions.
