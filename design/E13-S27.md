## Requirements (inlined)

- **PO-007** — The lifecycle should support Draft, Approval Pending, Approved, Issued, Partially Delivered, Delivered, Accepted, Closed, Cancelled, and Superseded.
- **ACC-008** — A complete project-to-PO workflow is operable without a mouse.

## Defect

E13-S03 shipped `POST /api/v1/purchase-orders/{id}/transitions` `{action: "close", reason}` (`If-Match`,
`Idempotency-Key`, `purchase_order.close` on the PO's unit), and the PO read lists `close` in `actions`
(`purchasing/service.py` ~701). The detail header (`apps/web/src/routes/purchase-orders/detail.tsx` ~72)
renders `issue`, cancel/reduce, `change_order` and `edit`, but nothing for `close`: an issued PO cannot be
closed from the product.

## Design

- When `p.actions` includes `close`, the header shows `Close Purchase Order` (secondary, not destructive).
  It opens a confirmation dialog (the pattern of `CancelOrReduce` in the same header): one required `Reason`
  (1..500), the line "Closing ends the order. It cannot be reopened." (closed is terminal in
  `purchasing/lifecycle.py` `TRANSITIONS`; say nothing about budget: closing moves no money, E13-S12 keeps a
  closed PO `committed`), buttons `Close Order`
  and `Keep Open` (safe button focused). Submit POSTs the transition with the PO's current version in
  `If-Match` and one `Idempotency-Key` held until a response arrives. On success reload the PO.
- Errors: a field error on `reason`; 409 `stale_version` says the order changed and keeps the reason; any
  other problem uses the shared failure text (UX-005).
- Regenerated client types only.

## Acceptance

- [ ] Playwright (stub): an issued PO with `close` in `actions` shows the control; closing with a reason sends `{action: "close", reason}` with `If-Match` and `Idempotency-Key`, and the page reloads showing Closed.
- [ ] Without `close` in `actions` the control is absent.
- [ ] Empty reason is refused in words before any request; a stale 409 keeps the reason.
- [ ] Keyboard only: reach the control, open, type, submit; Escape returns focus to the trigger. axe in both themes.
- [ ] MASTER §8 checklist; tokens only; `flo check --light` green.
