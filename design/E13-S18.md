## Requirements (inlined)

- **PO-010 / PO-011 / ACC-005** — numbered change orders; prior version kept; changed fields identified; budget effect recalculated; reapproved when policy requires.

## Screen `/purchase-orders/:id/changes/:n`

`Start Change Order` on an issued PO creates draft n. Quantities, prices (down only), tax, freight,
needed-by and terms are editable. **As you type** (debounced, server-computed): the diff as words
with a struck `was`; the **signed budget effect** per project (a decrease is parenthesised, signed
and coloured, and says it is re-reserved for the requisition, DECISIONS 209); and the reapproval
line (`Needs approval: the amount changes` / `No approval needed: date only`). A refused project,
account or department change explains `use_transfer` in words. `Apply` or `Submit for Approval` is
never disabled.
