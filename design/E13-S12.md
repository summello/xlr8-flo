- **PO-007** — The lifecycle should support Draft, Approval Pending, Approved, Issued, Partially Delivered, Delivered, Accepted, Closed, Cancelled, and Superseded.

## Screens

**`/purchase-orders`:** tabs `Drafts`, `Awaiting Approval`, `Ready to Issue`, `Issued`, `Needs
Attention` (bounced, failed or suppressed transmissions) and `All`, with server counts. Columns per
§4.1 (`Total` with the currency visible; `Sent` as a transmission `Signal`). `New PO` is the one
Primary (hidden until E13-S13 exists). Server paging.

**`/purchase-orders/:id`** (§4.4): the title is the PO number (display size, mono); the vendor
beneath it; the **version control** disclosure (current version only; it lists versions and change
orders on open and closes on Escape); the award link and project. The header bar holds the lifecycle
stepper (`aria-current="step"`, the future M4 states shown as future) over the `Order` and `Vendor`
swimlanes. The body is 70/30 (stacked below 1100px container width): left `Lines` (price, tax and
freight apart, totals), `Notes and Files`, `Transmissions`; right `Budget Summary` (per project of
its lines: `Committed`, `This PO` with the word reserved/committed/released, `Available After
Issue`, `Project Actual`, all **server figures with the DECISIONS 210 meanings**; then the sentence
naming what the PO holds now), `Linked Records` and `History`. Header actions by state and
permission; actions whose story has not merged stay hidden.

Money: tabular, right-aligned, currency code visible; negatives parenthesised, signed and coloured.
