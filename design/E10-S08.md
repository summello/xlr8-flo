# E10-S08 — Delegation, out-of-office, due dates, reminders and escalation

**Epic** E10 · **M2** · **engine/L** · after **E10-S06** · tags: `migration`

> **Conventions:** `docs/m1-conventions.md`, `docs/m2-conventions.md` (H, I, K) and `docs/author-traps.md`. Decisions: canvas `DECISIONS.md` 80, 81, 82; D-M2-14. Page spec (UI is E10-S10): `design-system/pages/approval-inbox.md` §5.

## Requirements

- **APR-017** — Delegation, out-of-office coverage, reminders, due dates, and escalations should be configurable, without changing historical attribution.
- **APR-014** — Every decision must record actor, acting authority/delegation, timestamp, outcome, comment/rationale, and document version.
- **APR-019** — A user must be prevented from approving their own request where separation-of-duty policy applies.

## Contract

| Route | Permission | Success |
|---|---|---|
| `POST /api/v1/approvals/delegations` (Idempotency-Key) | `approval.delegate` (organization) | 201 `DelegationRead` |
| `GET /api/v1/approvals/delegations?view=mine\|covering\|organization` | `require_member()` for `mine`/`covering` (self-scoped, convention P); `organization` additionally `permits(approval.admin)` | 200 `{rows, next_cursor}` |
| `POST /api/v1/approvals/delegations/{id}/revoke` (Idempotency-Key) | the delegator, or `approval.admin` | 200 |
| `GET /api/v1/approvals/delegations/eligible-delegates?document_type=` | `approval.delegate` | 200 `{rows: [{user_id, email}]}` (convention Q) |
| `POST /api/v1/approvals/tasks/{id}/reassign` (Idempotency-Key) | `approval.admin` | 200 `TaskRead`; body `{user_ids: [..], reason}` |
| `POST /internal/jobs/approval-reminders` | origin secret (the `budget-reconcile` precedent) | 200 report |

`DelegationCreate = {delegate_id, starts_at, ends_at, document_types: [..] | null, reason}`, where `null` means all document types (the page spec's `Covers: All my approvals | Purchase orders only`, generalised to any subset of the convention-H types). `starts_at < ends_at`, `ends_at` at most 366 days after `starts_at`.

## Behaviour

1. **Tables** (one migration): `approval_delegation(id, org_id, delegator_id, delegate_id, starts_at timestamptz, ends_at timestamptz, document_types text[] NULL, reason text NOT NULL, created_at, revoked_at timestamptz NULL, revoked_by uuid NULL)` with an exclusion constraint (or a checked trigger) refusing overlapping non-revoked periods for one delegator (page spec, "Server"); `approval_task` gains `escalated_at timestamptz NULL`, `escalation_rule jsonb NULL`, `reminded_on date NULL`; `approval_decision` already has `on_behalf_of` and `authority` (E10-S06). Permissions `approval.delegate` and `approval.admin` in a **separate permissions-only migration** `<next+1>_approval_permissions.py` (convention I's three places; baseline grants: `approval.delegate` to `approver` and `organization-administrator`, `approval.admin` to `organization-administrator`).
2. **Time (D-M2-14):** organizations have no time zone yet (user preferences arrive in E17-S06). `starts_at` and `ends_at` are instants. The UI converts the user's chosen dates to the start of the first day and the end of the last day in the browser's time zone. The page spec's "dates are inclusive" is honoured by that conversion.
3. **Who may be a delegate:** a member of the organization, not the delegator, holding the approval permission (`approve_permission`, E10-S04) for every covered document type at the organization or at a scope covering the delegator's approval scope. The picker lists only such people (APR-004), and the server re-checks at creation **and at decision time**: a delegate whose own access lapsed cannot decide.
4. **Deciding under a delegation:** while an active, unrevoked delegation covers the task's document type, the delegate is a current member of every task where the delegator is a current member (convention K extended), and the delegator stays a member too. The decision row records `actor_id = delegate`, `on_behalf_of = delegator`, `authority = 'delegate'` (APR-014). Separation of duty applies to the **delegate as well as the delegator**: a delegate cannot decide a document they requested (APR-019, page spec). `members_at_decision` lists members and active delegates.
5. **No re-delegation (canvas 81):** a delegation whose delegate currently covers for someone cannot pass that cover on. Creating a delegation never extends to tasks the delegator holds only *as a delegate*; the server refuses an attempt with 409 `CONFLICT`, `checks.problem = "no_redelegation"`.
6. **Revocation (canvas 80):** revoking sets `revoked_at`. Undecided tasks are no longer decidable by the delegate (they "come back to the owner" because membership is live: the owner never stopped being a member). Decisions already made keep their `on_behalf_of` attribution forever (append-only, E10-S06).
7. **Duplicate approvals (canvas 82)** are already enforced by E10-S05 rule 3 and `allow_repeat`. Delegation must not bypass them: a delegate who decided step 1 *as themselves* is not a member of step 2 as a delegate unless `allow_repeat`.
8. **Due dates:** E10-S05 sets `due_at` from the node's `due_days`. A task past `due_at` is **overdue** (E10-S10's Overdue tab). Delegation does not move a due date (page spec).
9. **Reminders and escalation:** one daily job.
   - The Worker calls `POST /internal/jobs/approval-reminders` at 07:30 UTC on its `:30` path (`apps/web/worker/api-proxy.ts` `runScheduledWork`, beside `fx-ingest` and `budget-reconcile`; extend `apps/web/src/api-proxy.test.ts`).
   - The endpoint enqueues one `approval.reminders` job per organization (the `reconcile_all` pattern). The handler is registered in `_run_jobs_tick`.
   - For each open task due within 24 hours or overdue, and not reminded today (`reminded_on`), it sends **one digest email per recipient per day**. A recipient is any current member, and the delegate instead of the delegator while a delegation is active. The email uses the outbox, template `approval-digest-v1`, idempotency key `approval-digest:<user_id>:<date>`, and holds only document types, numbers and due dates, no amounts.
   - Digests respect the Resend 100-per-day ceiling (project memory): one email per person per day, never one per task.
   - **Escalation:** a node's `escalate_after_days` and `escalate_to` (an approver rule, E10-S01's format), copied to the task at creation as `escalation_rule`. When `now > created_at + escalate_after_days` and the task is still open, the job adds the escalation rule's members as current members of the task (live, recomputed at decision time) and sets `escalated_at`. Audited as `approval.escalated`. Historical attribution is unchanged: escalation adds deciders, it never rewrites decisions.
10. **Admin reassign** (`approval.admin`): replaces the task's `approver_rule` with `{type: users, user_ids}` (each must be eligible, E10-S04), with a required reason, audited with the before and after rule. Decisions already made on the task stay. Reassigning a task that has decisions keeps them counting only if their authors are still members (convention K).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A delegate decides a covered task; the decision records `actor_id` = delegate, `on_behalf_of` = owner, `authority` = `delegate`.
- [ ] A delegation covering only `po` gives no power over a `requisition` task.
- [ ] Overlapping delegations from one person are refused; back-to-back ones are allowed.
- [ ] After revocation the delegate's decision is 403, the owner can decide, and earlier delegate decisions are unchanged.
- [ ] A delegate cannot decide a document they requested (APR-019).
- [ ] A delegate who is themselves covering for someone cannot delegate that cover (`no_redelegation`).
- [ ] The reminder job sends one digest per person per day (run it twice in one day: one email), names no amount, and goes to the delegate during an active delegation.
- [ ] Escalation after `escalate_after_days` lets the escalation member decide; attribution of earlier decisions is unchanged.
- [ ] Admin reassign requires a reason, rejects an ineligible user, and is audited.
- [ ] Every route: 401 unauthenticated, 403 or 404 per convention A, foreign tenant 404; the internal route requires the origin secret and an `Idempotency-Key`, and is not replayed (convention C).

## Pre-flight amendments (12 Oct 2026, from the runner's block; binding, they override anything above)

1. **A delegator can always revoke what they granted, even after losing `approval.delegate`.** Option (a). Revocation only reduces authority, so the route is guarded by `require_member()` (an authenticated session in a selected organization, convention P as extended below) and the service authorizes the call itself, in the same transaction, with one tenant-scoped query: load the delegation by `(org_id, id)`; **absent or foreign to the organization → 404** (no distinction between the two); the caller is `delegator_id` **or** holds `approval.admin` at the organization (`kernel.authz.permits`) → proceed; otherwise **403**. A delegate cannot revoke a delegation made to them unless they are also an administrator. Revoking an already-revoked delegation is idempotent: 200 with the stored record, no second audit row. The route keeps `Idempotency-Key`. `approval.delegate` stays required to **create** a delegation (`require`, unchanged); only revocation is relaxed. No other mutation may use `require_member()` on the strength of this amendment.
2. **Tests (real Postgres, per role, TEN-010):** (a) an owner who has lost `approval.delegate` revokes their own delegation: 200, `revoked_at` set, the delegate stops being a member of the owner's tasks; (b) a same-organization member who is neither owner nor admin: 403 and the row is unchanged; (c) an `approval.admin` who is not the owner: 200; (d) a delegation id from another organization and an unknown id: both 404 with identical bodies; (e) replay with the same `Idempotency-Key` returns the first response and writes one audit row. **Plant:** remove the owner-or-admin check from the service (leave only `require_member`): test (b) fails; **second plant:** make the lookup unscoped by `org_id`: test (d) fails.
3. **Convention P** gains one line (`docs/m2-conventions.md`): `require_member()` is also allowed on a route that only **reduces** the caller's own or another's authority (a revocation), provided the service performs an explicit ownership-or-permission check in the same transaction and the route is listed in the story packet and in `tests/isolation/test_route_coverage.py` with its per-role tests. E10-S08's revoke route is the first and only user in M2.
4. **Operator-visible:** this relaxes who may call one mutation. It cannot grant authority; the worst case is that a person who lost the permission can still end a delegation they created.
