# E12-S24 — Lines with no priced quote: one rule across documents, auto-cancel at award, a small notice

**Epic** E12 · **M3** · **engine/L** · after **E12-S23**, **E12-S08**, **E13-S07** · tags: `money` (Opus review)

> **Status: operator decisions Q1-Q3 recorded 31 Oct 2026 (see ## Decisions); ready for pre-flight 2.**
> E13-S07 has merged: `requisitions.service.cancel_lines` exists (`requisitions/sourcing.py:339`). The
> notification engine is still E17-S02 (M4); the notice uses the no-op hook until then.
> **Why (operator, 28 Oct 2026):** an RFQ line that no vendor priced (not quoted by anyone, or refused as
> a zero price by E12-S23) must not silently vanish. Anyone investigating why an item is not on a PO must
> see why, on the requisition and the RFQ. It is **derived, never stored**, so it cannot drift.

## Rules (decided by the operator)

1. **One condition, no new column.** An RFQ line has *no priced quote* when no submitted, current bid has a quoted line for it with a price above 0. Put the condition in one helper (`sourcing.service`, `no_priced_quote(conn, scope, rfq_id) -> set[rfq_line_id]`) and use it everywhere: the RFQ comparison and award screens (a line badge and reason, "No vendor priced this line"), the requisition's line sourcing status, the award engine (such a line is not awardable), PO creation (never builds a line from it), and the vendor copy (never shows it). The flag applies to lines **nobody quoted as well as lines refused as zero** (decision 3).
2. **Auto-cancel at award approval.** When an award is approved, every requisition line whose RFQ line has no priced quote in that award is cancelled by `requisitions.service.cancel_lines` (`cancel_lines` raises `cancelled_qty` and audits; the reservation share is released and returned to the main project by `release_unawarded`, see ## Decisions), so the requisition can still complete (decision 206). The award screen **warns before approval** ("2 lines have no priced quote and will be cancelled on approval") so nothing happens unseen.
3. **A small notice.** The requester is told which lines were cancelled and why, through the same no-op hook as E13-S05 (`notify_requester_*`, wired by E17-S02), and the audit entry names the lines.

## Acceptance (to be sharpened at design time)

- [ ] A line unquoted by every vendor, and a line refused as zero, show the same reason on the requisition and the RFQ.
- [ ] Approving an award cancels exactly those lines, once (idempotent), releasing their reservation share; the requisition completes without them.
- [ ] The warning appears before approval and names the lines.
- [ ] No stored flag exists: changing a bid changes the reason on the next read.
- [ ] TEN-010 and authorization per role; `flo check` green.

## Decisions (operator, 31 Oct 2026: "use the recommended defaults")

Technical facts verified against the merged code (pre-flight 1):
- `cancel_lines(conn, scope, requisition_id, [(line_id, qty)], ...)` only raises `cancelled_qty` and audits;
  it does **not** release or return funds. Release and the sub-project -> main return are the separate
  `release_unawarded(..., unused, award_id, ..., version=...)` (`requisitions/sourcing.py:554`), which the
  caller invokes under the `project_balance` locks (lock before validate, one transaction).
- `cancel_lines` refuses unless the requisition is `open_for_purchase` or `completed` (`sourcing.py:364`);
  at award approval it is still `sourced` until `mark_awarded` runs (`sourcing.py:606`,
  `sourcing/awards.py:964`).

Decisions:
- **Q1 (state transition): auto-cancel runs after `mark_awarded`**, in the same transaction, once the
  requisition is `open_for_purchase`. `cancel_lines` is **not** changed to accept `sourced`; no state
  transition is added or altered.
- **Q2 (money): only the unpriced RFQ slice is cancelled**, and only that slice's reservation share is
  released. An RFQ may source part of a requisition line (`sourcing/service.py:568`); the rest of the line
  stays open and is not touched. The quantity cancelled is the RFQ line's quantity, never the line's whole
  unordered remainder.
- **Q3 (state transition): a requester may re-source a cancelled line** with a new RFQ against the same
  requisition (the cancelled quantity is no longer sourced, so it is sourceable again). No new requisition
  is required. The packet must state the guard that keeps re-sourcing from exceeding the line's original
  quantity less what is already ordered or cancelled.

Still owed by the design pass, not the operator: final wording and placement of the badge, warning and
notice (Rules 1-3 give provisional text; `design-system/pages/rfq-comparison.md` and `requisitions.md`
decide placement), and the acceptance criteria below sharpened to cover Q1-Q3 (the cancel-after-
`mark_awarded` ordering, the partial-slice quantity and release, and the re-source guard).
