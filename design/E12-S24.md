# E12-S24 — Lines with no priced quote: one rule across documents, auto-cancel at award, a small notice

**Epic** E12 · **M3** · **engine/L** · after **E12-S23**, **E12-S08**, **E13-S07** · tags: `money` (Opus review)

> **Status: blocked on three operator decisions (see ## Blocked), 31 Oct 2026.** E13-S07 has merged:
> `requisitions.service.cancel_lines` exists (`requisitions/sourcing.py:339`), and pre-flight 1 (Codex,
> 31 Oct) failed on the questions below. The notification engine is still E17-S02 (M4); the notice uses the
> no-op hook until then.
> **Why (operator, 28 Oct 2026):** an RFQ line that no vendor priced (not quoted by anyone, or refused as
> a zero price by E12-S23) must not silently vanish. Anyone investigating why an item is not on a PO must
> see why, on the requisition and the RFQ. It is **derived, never stored**, so it cannot drift.

## Rules (decided by the operator)

1. **One condition, no new column.** An RFQ line has *no priced quote* when no submitted, current bid has a quoted line for it with a price above 0. Put the condition in one helper (`sourcing.service`, `no_priced_quote(conn, scope, rfq_id) -> set[rfq_line_id]`) and use it everywhere: the RFQ comparison and award screens (a line badge and reason, "No vendor priced this line"), the requisition's line sourcing status, the award engine (such a line is not awardable), PO creation (never builds a line from it), and the vendor copy (never shows it). The flag applies to lines **nobody quoted as well as lines refused as zero** (decision 3).
2. **Auto-cancel at award approval.** When an award is approved, every requisition line whose RFQ line has no priced quote in that award is cancelled by `requisitions.service.cancel_lines` (E13-S07's contract: release its reservation share and return it to the main project, `cancelled_qty` up, audited), so the requisition can still complete (decision 206). The award screen **warns before approval** ("2 lines have no priced quote and will be cancelled on approval") so nothing happens unseen.
3. **A small notice.** The requester is told which lines were cancelled and why, through the same no-op hook as E13-S05 (`notify_requester_*`, wired by E17-S02), and the audit entry names the lines.

## Open until E13-S07 is merged

The exact `cancel_lines` signature and its reservation-return keys; whether a requester may re-source a cancelled line (a new requisition or RFQ), or only recreate it; the wording of the warning and notice (design-system copy rules); the UI placement on the comparison and requisition screens (`design-system/pages/rfq-comparison.md`, `requisitions.md`).

## Acceptance (to be sharpened at design time)

- [ ] A line unquoted by every vendor, and a line refused as zero, show the same reason on the requisition and the RFQ.
- [ ] Approving an award cancels exactly those lines, once (idempotent), releasing their reservation share; the requisition completes without them.
- [ ] The warning appears before approval and names the lines.
- [ ] No stored flag exists: changing a bid changes the reason on the next read.
- [ ] TEN-010 and authorization per role; `flo check` green.

## Blocked

**31 Oct 2026, planner** (pre-flight 1 findings, verified against the merged code). Technical facts the
design pass must correct, and three decisions only the operator may make:

Facts (no decision needed):
- `cancel_lines(conn, scope, requisition_id, [(line_id, qty)], ...)` only raises `cancelled_qty` and
  audits; it does **not** release or return funds. Release and the sub-project -> main return are the
  separate `release_unawarded(..., unused, award_id, ..., version=...)` (`requisitions/sourcing.py:554`),
  which the caller must invoke under the project_balance locks. Rule 2's parenthetical is wrong.
- `cancel_lines` refuses unless the requisition is `open_for_purchase` or `completed`
  (`sourcing.py:364`); at award approval it is still `sourced` until `mark_awarded` runs
  (`sourcing.py:606`, `sourcing/awards.py:964`). The call must come after `mark_awarded` in the same
  transaction, or `cancel_lines` must accept `sourced` — the latter is a state-transition change (Q1).

Questions for the operator (money / state transition; the planner does not answer these):
- **Q1 (state transition):** at award approval, may `cancel_lines` run on a `sourced` requisition, or
  must auto-cancel run after `mark_awarded` moves it to `open_for_purchase`?
- **Q2 (money):** an RFQ may source only part of a requisition line (`sourcing/service.py:568`). When
  that RFQ line has no priced quote, is the cancelled quantity (and the reservation share released) the
  unpriced RFQ slice only, or the requisition line's whole unordered remainder?
- **Q3 (state transition):** may a requester re-source a cancelled line (new RFQ against the same
  requisition), or only recreate it on a new requisition?

Also owed by the design pass, not the operator: final wording and placement of the badge, warning and
notice (Rules 1-3 give provisional text; `design-system/pages/rfq-comparison.md` and `requisitions.md`
decide placement), and the "Open until E13-S07" section removed once Q1-Q3 are answered.
