## Requirements (inlined)

- **SRC-001** — Sourcing users must be able to create an RFQ from approved requisition lines.
- **SRC-003** — The system must prevent sourcing more than the approved open quantity or amount unless the requisition is amended.
- **REQ-014 / REQ-015** — split sourcing; requested, sourced, awarded, ordered, remaining and cancelled per line.

## Screen `/sourcing/rfqs/new` (optional `?requisition=<id>`)

A table grouped by requisition, from `GET /sourcing/requisition-lines`. Each line is a checkbox row
(no drag, decision 152) with `Requested`, `Already Sourced`, `Open` and an editable `Quantity to
Source` that starts at `Open`. Above open is refused **at the field** with the figure and the way out
(`40 are open on REQ-00418. Amend the requisition to source more`). This is advisory: the server
enforces it under a lock. A line from another unit or currency than the first chosen is disabled
with the reason (`Another business unit`, `Different currency`). A title field, then `Create RFQ
Draft` as the one Primary, **never disabled**. A 409 `over_open_quantity` from the server maps back
to the line fields with the server's figures (someone else sourced first), keeping the user's
choices. Success lands on the RFQ editor (E12-S13; until it exists, the detail page).
