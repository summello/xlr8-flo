## Requirements (inlined)

- **SRC-017** — Cancelling or superseding an RFQ/award must preserve the complete history and reconcile its downstream budget effect.
- **WF-005** — cancelled documents stay readable.

## Design (fixed except the budget rows)

the requisition's open quantities and **the budget (rows from E12-S09 once decided)**, a required
reason, the safe button focused, and a confirm named for the action. The cancelled RFQ stays
readable with a banner. Server errors are shown in words with the reason kept.
