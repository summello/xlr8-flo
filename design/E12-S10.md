## Requirements (inlined)

- **SRC-009** — The comparison dashboard must normalize and compare total cost, unit cost, tax, freight, lead time, compliance, vendor score, and configured criteria.
- **SRC-010** — conversion shows rate, source and effective date, and never alters the bid.
- **SRC-007 / SRC-008** — sealed until the deadline; a late bid is marked and included only through an audited override.
- **A11Y-009 / RPT-013** — every chart ships an accessible data table.
- **A11Y-003 / A11Y-004** — no drag-only paths; colour never alone.

## Scope

Route `/sourcing/rfqs/:id/comparison`, as specified in `pages/rfq-comparison.md` §1 to §7: the award
basis cards with insight, scope pill and justification notice; the verdict strip; the matrix with
groups, `Show Only Differences`, sticky header and narrow two-vendor mode; the tabs `Overview`,
`By Line`, `Cost Breakdown` (bar chart **and** its `Table` tab) and `Scoring`; the bid sheet; the
sticky award bar with **`Start Award`** (a hand-off to `/sourcing/rfqs/:id/award` with the basis;
until E12-S16 exists, the route registry hides the button and records a follow-up). The states are
sealed, no bids, scores not available, stale rate with `Refresh Rate`, over scope, and all five widths.

Markers are icon plus words inside their cell. The three colour jobs come from tokens only (decision
120), and there is a colour key. Money is tabular, right-aligned, with the currency code; negatives
are parenthesised and signed.

- [ ] Chart and Table tabs: the table holds the same values as the chart (A11Y-009).
- [ ] Narrow mode at 768 and 375: two vendors and a picker that cannot choose the same vendor twice.
- [ ] Bid sheet focus: opens on Close, traps, Escape returns focus to the header button.
