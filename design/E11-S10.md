# E11-S10 — Shared decision panel at phone width: footer reflow and clear close button

**Epic** E11 · **M3** · **ui/M** · after **E11-S09** (built on **E10-S10**) · tags: `ui`

> **Conventions:** `docs/m1-conventions.md`, `docs/m2-conventions.md`, `docs/author-traps.md`. **UI authority, in order:** `design-system/MASTER.md` and `apps/web/src/styles/tokens.css`, then `design-system/pages/approval-inbox.md` §2 (decision panel) and §6 items 6 and 7, then the canvas board `ApprSheet375` for layout only.

> **Why it exists.** The M3 design pass found, on the vendor, award and purchase-order variants of the decision panel, that at 375px the five footer buttons wrap onto two uneven rows and the shell's top bar can overlap the sheet's close button. It is the shared component built in **E10-S10** (`components/approvals/DecisionPanel.tsx`), so it affects every document kind, requisitions included. It is fixed once, here, **before** any M3 story adds a vendor-onboarding, award or PO variant to the panel.

## Requirements

- **A11Y-011** — Touch targets, zoom/reflow, orientation, and responsive states must meet WCAG 2.2 requirements.
- **A11Y-002** — All functions must be operable by keyboard with visible, unobscured focus and logical focus order.
- **UX-008** — Desktop web is the primary Phase 1 target, but essential workflows must remain usable on common tablet and mobile browser widths.
- **APR-020** — The system must provide inbox, pending, overdue, completed, and delegated approval views (the panel is where a task is decided).

## Contract

No API, no migration. CSS and markup in `apps/web/src/components/approvals/DecisionPanel.tsx` and `apps/web/src/styles/approvals.css`.

### Layout decision *(Opus, recorded; not the operator's)*

Below **640px** of the sheet's own width (container query on the sheet, not the viewport), `.appr-actions` is a two-column grid. **DOM order and focus order do not change** (`Close`, `Post Comment`, `Return for Changes`, `Reject`, `Approve`), so the visual order matches the reading order:

| Row | Left | Right |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `Close` | `Post Comment` |
| 2 | `Return for Changes` | `Reject` |
| 3 | `Approve` (spans both, the one primary) | |

Every button is at least `var(--touch-target)` tall and `Approve` is at least as wide as any other. At 640px and up the footer is unchanged from E10-S10. When the decision buttons are absent (a task the caller cannot decide, WF-006), only row 1 remains and `Close` and `Post Comment` keep their places. Tokens only: gaps are `--space-*`; no raw pixel.

### The close button is never covered

The sheet is `position: fixed` at `--z-modal` and the shell's top bar is `--z-sticky`, so in the app the close button must already clear it; the canvas defect may be canvas-only. **Prove it, do not assume:** at 375 with the panel open, the close button's centre point is hit-tested (`document.elementFromPoint`) and must be the close button. If it is covered in the app, fix the stacking or the sheet's top inset, not the top bar. Either way the canvas boards are a design-session follow-up (`notes.followup`).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] At 375px, the footer renders as the three rows above; no button is narrower than its label or clipped; no horizontal scroll in the sheet.
- [ ] Tab order is `Close`, `Post Comment`, `Return for Changes`, `Reject`, `Approve`, at every width.
- [ ] Every footer control is at least 44px tall below 1024px.
- [ ] The close button receives the hit-test at 375 and at 768 with the panel open on a requisition task.
- [ ] A task the caller cannot decide shows only `Close` and `Post Comment` and the existing notice, laid out without a gap.
- [ ] At 640px and up the footer is pixel-for-pixel the E10-S10 layout (the existing 1440 screenshot assertion, if any, still passes; otherwise a computed-style assertion on the flex row).
- [ ] Axe passes with the panel open at 375, light and dark.
- [ ] The MASTER §8 checklist is ticked in the PR body.

## Required tests

`apps/web/tests/e2e/approvals.spec.ts` (extend; API-stubbed): a 375 case for layout, tab order, target height and the close hit-test; a 1440 case that the footer is one row. `apps/web/tests/a11y` entry for the panel at 375.

## Plants

1. Reorder the buttons with CSS `order` so Approve is visually first: the tab-order-equals-visual-order assertion fails.
2. Give the footer a fixed `height`: the target-height or clipping assertion fails.
3. Raise the top bar above the sheet: the close-button hit-test fails.
