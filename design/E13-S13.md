## Requirements (inlined)

- **PO-001** — POs must be creatable from approved and sourced requisitions (M3: from approved awards).
- **PO-009** — Partial POs must reduce only the corresponding open requisition quantity and value.
- **SRC-018** — every line completes sourcing before a PO.

## Screen `/purchase-orders/new?award=<id>` (and `Create PO` on an approved award)

Grouped **by vendor** (one PO per vendor), from `GET /purchase-orders/awarded-lines`. Columns are
`Awarded`, `Already Ordered`, `Open` and an editable `Quantity to Order`, starting at `Open`. Above
open is refused at the field with the figure. An empty state explains why (no approved award, or
everything ordered). `Create Draft POs` creates one PO per vendor with any quantity. It is never
disabled. A server 409 maps back to the fields with the server's figures. On success, it links to
each draft.
