# E13-S24 — Vendor cancellation email for a cancelled issued PO, through the outbox

**Epic** E13 · **M3** · **engine/M** · after **E13-S07**, **E13-S09** · tags: `security` (Opus review)

> **Conventions:** `docs/m1-conventions.md`, `docs/m2-conventions.md`, `docs/author-traps.md`.
> **Decisions:** invariant 6 (every external effect through the outbox), canvas DECISIONS 170 (what
> the supplier receives; nothing internal), `design-system/pages/purchase-orders.md` §2 "Cancel or
> reduce … whether the vendor is told". This story closes E13-S09's OPEN 3.
> **Writes:** no new route, no new request field, no `PATCH`/`PUT`/`DELETE`. It changes the
> behaviour of two existing POSTs, both already behind CSRF, the session/MFA gates and the
> idempotency middleware (`kernel/idempotency/middleware.py:101`, `_default_required_key`):
> `POST /api/v1/purchase-orders/{id}/cancel` (`api/purchasing.py:238-247`, `AmendContext` =
> `purchase_order.amend`, `If-Match` per m2-conventions §O, unchanged) and
> `POST /api/v1/purchase-orders/{id}/transmissions/resend` (`api/purchasing.py:379-383`,
> `IssueContext` = `purchase_order.issue`, no `If-Match`, unchanged).
> **RLS:** no table and no migration. `po_transmission` and its RLS are E13-S09's
> (`migrations/20261031_0086_po_transmission.py:29-33`); every row this story writes carries the
> scope's `org_id` through `PurchaseOrderService(...).repo` and `OutboxStore(conn, scope)`.
> **Dependencies merged:** E13-S07 (`04464627`), E13-S09 (`71c8e69e`) on `milestone/M3-sellable`.
> E13-S12 (`519d5c7d`) moved `READ_STATE` and `_FROM` into `purchasing/models.py:24-30` and added
> the list/detail reads named under **Reads**; this story reuses them and changes none.

## Requirements (inlined)

- **PO-013** — Cancelling or reducing a PO must release the unused commitment exactly once and must not erase its history.
- **PO-015** — Failed email/API/cXML transmissions must be visible, retryable, and auditable without duplicating the PO.
- **ARCH-006** — External effects such as email/API delivery must use a transactional outbox or equivalent durable handoff.

## Design

### What exists (all lines as on `milestone/M3-sellable` at `d99036c6`)

- **E13-S07** (`modules/purchasing/cancel.py`): a no-op stub `notify_vendor_cancelled(conn, scope,
  po_id)` (`cancel.py:51-53`), called once at `cancel.py:117-118`, `if status == "issued" and
  body.notify_vendor`, **after** `transition(conn, scope, po_id, "cancel", actor_id, version,
  body.reason)` (`cancel.py:116`) and inside the cancel transaction (`cancel.py:67`), which by then
  holds award, balances, requisition and the PO row `FOR UPDATE` (`cancel.py:70-100`). `status` is
  the locked pre-transition status (`cancel.py:101`), so a cancel before issue never reaches it. A
  retry returns at `cancel.py:102-103`, before it, so it runs once per cancelled row.
  `PoCancelCreate.notify_vendor: bool = True` (`schemas.py:293`).
- `transition` (`lifecycle.py:55-101`) writes `status` and `version = version + 1` (`:84-88`) and
  writes the cancel's one audit row itself: `purchase_order.cancel`, `before={"status": status}`,
  `after={"status": target}` (`:89-100`). There is no other cancel audit row
  (`tests/purchasing/test_cancel.py:141-143` pins exactly one). `TRANSITIONS` has no action out of
  `cancelled` (`lifecycle.py:21-34`), so a cancelled row's `version` is fixed, one above the version
  its issue rows carry (`issue.py:177` passes `version + 1`; a change-order revision,
  `changes.py:688`, passes `2`).
- **E13-S09** (`modules/purchasing/transmission.py`):
  - `po_transmission` — one row per recipient per attempt, `UNIQUE (org_id, po_id, attempt,
    lower(recipient_email))`, **no `version` in it** (`0086:27-28`); `recipient_email NOT NULL`
    (`0086:16`); `result` CHECK has no `not_sent` (`0086:19-20`); `outbox_id IS NULL` iff
    `address_suppressed` (`0086:25`). `attempt` is numbered per `po_id` across versions.
  - `_dedupe(recipients)` (`transmission.py:43-50`): first entry per `lower(email)`.
  - `_send(conn, scope, po_id, version, attempt, recipients, created_by)` (`transmission.py:53-113`):
    dedupes, raises 409 `no_recipients` on an empty list (`:63-65`), builds the issue PDF, summary
    and context itself (`:66-77`), then per recipient either an `address_suppressed` row
    (`_suppressed`, `:38-40`, `:86-87`) or `OutboxStore.add_email(..., template=TEMPLATE,
    idempotency_key=f"po_transmission:{row_id}", attachments=[attachment])` (`:89-95`) and the row
    with `template_version = TEMPLATE` (`:96-113`). `TEMPLATE = "po-issued-v1"` (`:30`).
  - `_latest_recipients(repo, po_id)` (`:116-124`): the rows of the highest `attempt` on `po_id`.
  - `enqueue` (`:127-141`), `list_transmissions` (`:144-159`, ordered `created_at DESC, attempt
    DESC`), `attention_counts` (`:162-171`), `resend` (`:174-222`: locks only the PO row `:179`;
    `status != "issued"` → 409 `not_resendable` `:180-181`; a `queued` row on the `po_id` → 409
    `transmission_queued` `:183-188` — that query has **no `version` and no template filter**;
    `max(attempt)` `:189-196`, `None` → 409 `nothing_to_resend` `:197-198`; recipients default
    `_latest_recipients` `:200` or `contact_ids` `:201-210`; `_send(..., h["version"], last + 1,
    ...)` `:211`; audit `purchase_order.transmission.resend` with `after={"attempt", "recipients"}`
    `:212-221`).
- `OutboxStore.add_email(..., attachments=())` omits the `attachments` key when empty
  (`kernel/outbox/store.py:149-168`, `:159`); the dispatcher calls the sender without attachments
  when the key is absent (`kernel/outbox/dispatcher.py:111-112`).
- Precedent for a vendor cancellation email: RFQ cancel, `sourcing/lifecycle.py:461-474`
  (`CANCELLED_TEMPLATE = "rfq-cancelled-v1"`, `:49`; fixture `apps/api/templates/email/rfq_cancelled.html`).
  That one carries a `reason`; this one must not (Resolved 2).
- An issued PO always has a recipient through the API: issue refuses without one
  (`issue.py:74-81`) and stores the list on the row (`:82-87`). The no-recipient case below is
  reachable only by data, so its test patches the resolver.

### One send path (refactor of `_send`, behaviour on the issue path unchanged)

Add keyword-only parameters with defaults so E13-S09's two call sites (`:141`, `:211`) do not change:
`_send(conn, scope, po_id, version, attempt, recipients, created_by, *, template: str = TEMPLATE,
context: Mapping[str, object] | None = None, attachments: Sequence[EmailAttachment] | None = None)`.
After the dedupe and the empty check (unchanged, still first), `context is None` runs the existing
PDF/summary/context block (`:66-77`) and sets `attachments = [attachment]`; otherwise the passed
`context` and `attachments` (or `()`) are used and **no PDF is rendered**. `template` replaces the
hard-coded `TEMPLATE` in `add_email` (`:91`) and in the row insert (`:108`). E13-S09's tests stay
green unmodified.

`_latest_recipients(repo, po_id, template: str | None = None)`: with a template, both the outer
rows and the `max(attempt)` subquery add `AND template_version = %(template)s`; without, the SQL is
unchanged.

### `transmission.CANCEL_TEMPLATE` and two functions

`CANCEL_TEMPLATE = "po-cancelled-v1"` beside `TEMPLATE`. Keep the `po-` prefix: E13-S10's
production domain gate (not merged; `design/E13-S10.md:43-44`) recognises a PO email by a template
name starting `po-`, so it covers this one with no change.

`cancellation_recipients(conn, scope, po_id) -> list[dict[str, Any]]` — reads only, no lock (the
caller holds the PO row): `_latest_recipients(repo, po_id)` (the highest `attempt` on this `po_id`,
any template, so a recipient chosen at resend carries forward); none → the row's `recipients`
column (one scoped `SELECT recipients FROM purchase_order WHERE org_id = %(org_id)s AND id =
%(id)s`); return `_dedupe(...)` of the result (possibly empty).

`enqueue_cancellation(conn, scope, po_id, actor_id, recipients)` — runs in the caller's
transaction, takes no lock, writes only `po_transmission` and `outbox`. Called only with a non-empty
list.
1. One scoped `SELECT number, revision, version, vendor_snapshot FROM purchase_order` for `po_id`
   (read after the transition, so `version` is the cancelled row's).
2. One scoped `SELECT max(attempt) FROM po_transmission WHERE org_id = %(org_id)s AND po_id = %(po)s`
   (all templates, all versions).
3. `_send(conn, scope, po_id, version, (max or 0) + 1, recipients, actor_id,
   template=CANCEL_TEMPLATE, context={"po_number": number, "revision": str(revision),
   "vendor_name": vendor_snapshot["trading_name"]}, attachments=())`. The `vendor_snapshot` key is
   written at PO creation (`service.py:788`). Attempt 1 would collide with the issue rows on the
   unique index (no `version` in it) and roll the cancel back. Nothing else goes in the context
   (Resolved 2: no reason); no attachment (Resolved 1: no PDF).

### Cancel (`cancel.py`)

Delete the `notify_vendor_cancelled` stub (`:51-53`) and replace the call at `:116-118` with:

```python
notify = status == "issued" and body.notify_vendor
recipients = transmission.cancellation_recipients(conn, scope, po_id) if notify else []
transition(
    conn, scope, po_id, "cancel", actor_id, version, body.reason,
    after={"vendor_notified": False} if notify and not recipients else None,
)  # fmt: skip
if recipients:
    transmission.enqueue_cancellation(conn, scope, po_id, actor_id, recipients)
```

`transmission.cancellation_recipients`; `transmission` imports nothing from `cancel`, so no cycle.)

`lifecycle.transition` gains one keyword parameter `after: Mapping[str, object] | None = None`,
merged into the audit: `after={"status": target, **(after or {})}`. Every existing caller passes

### Resend on a cancelled PO (edit to E13-S09's `resend`)

Same route, permission, body, response, single PO lock and attempt numbering. Changes:

- Status: `issued` → every query exactly as today. `cancelled` → resend the cancellation notice.
  Any other status → 409 `not_resendable`, detail now "Only an issued or cancelled purchase order
  can be sent again."
- On a cancelled row, the `queued` check (`:183-188`) adds `AND t.template_version =
  %(template)s` with `CANCEL_TEMPLATE`, so a still-queued issue email does not block a cancellation
  resend; the "nothing to resend" check counts cancellation rows only (a cancelled PO with no
  cancellation row — cancelled with `notify_vendor=false`, or with no recipient — gives 409
  `nothing_to_resend`); default recipients are `_latest_recipients(repo, po_id, CANCEL_TEMPLATE)`;
  `contact_ids` work as today. The new attempt number stays `max(attempt)` over all rows of the
  `po_id` + 1 (`:189-196`, unchanged query). The send is `_send(..., template=CANCEL_TEMPLATE,
  context=<as in enqueue_cancellation step 3>, attachments=())` at the cancelled row's version.
- The audit `after` gains `"template": <the template sent>` on both branches.

### Locks and transactions

`cancellation_recipients` and `enqueue_cancellation` add no lock to cancel's order (award,
balances, requisition, PO). The `po_transmission` inserts take only `FOR KEY SHARE` on the
referenced `organization`, `purchase_order` (already held `FOR UPDATE`) and `identity` rows; the
`outbox` table has no foreign key (`migrations/20260825_0007_jobs_outbox.py:69-84`). Resend still
locks only the PO row: a cancel waiting on that row holds nothing resend wants, so no cycle. Any
error in the send (outbox insert, unique violation) rolls the whole cancel back, release included;
that is the ARCH-006 handoff.

### Reads (no change; what E13-S12 and E13-S09 already show)

- `GET …/transmissions` (`list_transmissions`) lists cancellation rows first (newest `created_at`)
  with `template_version = 'po-cancelled-v1'` (`PoTransmissionRead.template_version`,
  `schemas.py:363`).
- `attention_counts` counts their failures (OBS-004).
- E13-S12's PO list `sent` column (`WORST_SENT_SQL`, `models.py:42-48`, used at `service.py:500`)
  and the detail `TransmissionsCard` "Sent to Vendor" block
  (`apps/web/src/components/purchasing/PoCards.tsx:87-96`) read the **latest attempt**, which on a
  notified cancelled PO is the cancellation notice. The list's `issued`/`attention` tabs filter
  `p.status = 'issued'` (`service.py:466-467`), so a cancelled PO is in neither. Labelling a row by
  its template is E13-S16's; record in `notes.followup` that E13-S16 must label `po-cancelled-v1`
  rows on both surfaces.

## Acceptance

- [ ] Cancel of an issued PO with two recipients: in the cancel transaction, two `po_transmission` rows on that `po_id` with `version` = the cancelled row's version, `attempt` = the PO's prior `max(attempt)` + 1 (issue was attempt 1, so 2), `template_version = 'po-cancelled-v1'`, `created_by` = the canceller, and two outbox emails with that template; the issue rows are unchanged; the cancel audit `after` is exactly `{"status": "cancelled"}`.
- [ ] Two recipients with the same email in different case → one row and one outbox email; the cancel succeeds.
- [ ] Leak: cancel with reason `REASONLEAK4`; the outbox payload, read before dispatch, has `context` keys exactly `{po_number, revision, vendor_name}`, the payload JSON does not contain `REASONLEAK4`, and it has no `attachments` key; `pdf_for` patched to raise does not affect the cancel.
- [ ] `notify_vendor=false` → no new transmission row and no outbox row, audit `after` `{"status": "cancelled"}`. Cancel of a `draft`, `approved` and `approval_pending` PO with `notify_vendor=true` → none either.
- [ ] **No recipient (operator, 7 Nov 2026):** `transmission.cancellation_recipients` patched to return `[]`, notified cancel of an issued PO → 200, status `cancelled`, the `release` ledger entry posted once, no new `po_transmission` row, no new outbox row, and exactly one `purchase_order.cancel` audit row with `after == {"status": "cancelled", "vendor_notified": False}`. - [ ] Retry of the cancel (same `Idempotency-Key`; and a new key with the new `If-Match`) → no second set of rows.
- [ ] Recipients carry: after an E13-S09 resend with `contact_ids` = contact X, the cancellation goes to X only.
- [ ] `_suppressed` patched to `True` → `address_suppressed` rows, no outbox email, the cancel succeeds.
- [ ] Atomicity: `OutboxStore.add_email` patched to raise during the cancel → the PO stays `issued` at its version, no `release` ledger entry, no transmission row, no outbox row, no cancel audit row.
- [ ] No send from the handler: with the email adapters' `send` patched to record (as `test_transmission.py:347-356`), `POST …/cancel` through the real app returns with zero provider calls; one dispatcher run with a sender that records `template` then makes one `po-cancelled-v1` call per recipient with key `po_transmission:{row id}`, and those rows read `sent`.
- [ ] Resend on a cancelled PO whose cancellation send is dead: attempt = prior `max(attempt)` + 1 at the cancelled version, template `po-cancelled-v1`, no attachment, one outbox email per recipient; no ledger entry; status and version unchanged; audit `purchase_order.transmission.resend` with `"template": "po-cancelled-v1"`.

- [ ] `GET …/transmissions` on a cancelled PO lists the cancellation rows before the issue rows, each with its `template_version`.
- [ ] Resend on a cancelled PO without `purchase_order.issue` → 403; a foreign tenant's cancelled PO → 404 (added to `test_transmission_routes_conceal_foreign_tenants`, `test_transmission.py:403`). `flo check --light` green.

## Resolved

Operator, 30 Oct 2026: the vendor cancellation email is its own story, E13-S24, after E13-S07 and
E13-S09 (closes E13-S09 OPEN 3), and goes through E13-S09's transmission rows and outbox path. From
E13-S07: only a cancel of an `issued` PO notifies, and only when `notify_vendor` is true.

Operator, 31 Oct 2026 (were OPEN 1-4, all as recommended and as implemented above):
1. **No PDF attached** to the cancellation email: the vendor already holds it, nothing renders inside
   cancel's locks, and a snapshot fault cannot block a money release.
2. **No cancel `reason`** in the email: it stays the internal transition and audit reason (PO-017).
3. **Resend of a cancellation notice** is `purchase_order.issue` through E13-S09's resend route; no new route.
4. **A still-queued issue email is left alone** when the PO is cancelled; both emails name the PO
   number and revision. No new `po_transmission` result, no migration.

Operator, 7 Nov 2026 (was `## Blocked`, pre-flight 1 finding 2), option (a): a notified cancel on
a PO with no recipient at all proceeds; no transmission row is written; the cancel's audit `after`
records `"vendor_notified": false`. No migration. Specified under **Cancel** and in Acceptance.
