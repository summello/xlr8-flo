# E13-S09 — Email transmission through the outbox: recorded, retryable, never duplicated

**Epic** E13 · **M3** · **engine/L** · after **E13-S08**, **E03-S06** · tags: `money`, `security` (Opus review)

> **Conventions:** `docs/m1-conventions.md`, `docs/m2-conventions.md`, `docs/author-traps.md`.
> **Decisions:** invariant 6 (every external effect through the outbox), canvas DECISIONS 168 (the
> email goes to the vendor's Service and Delivery contacts, i.e. `vendors.service.document_recipients`;
> the requester gets an internal notification, not a copy), 170 (one email and one PDF; no HTML
> attachment), project memory (Resend 100/day; transactional PO sends go immediately, never digested).
> `design-system/pages/purchase-orders.md` §2 "Sending is not arriving", §3 (transmission results).
> **Writes:** every `PATCH`/`PUT`/`DELETE` on an editable resource follows `docs/m2-conventions.md` §O (`If-Match` with the integer version, `ETag` on reads, 400 when missing, 409 `stale_version` on mismatch). `Idempotency-Key` protects **POST** commands only (the middleware ignores other methods); where a packet says "(Idempotency-Key)" on a non-POST route, read §O instead.

## Requirements (inlined)

- **PO-004** — Phase 1 must send approved POs by email and record recipient, time, delivery result, and the exact document version.
- **PO-012** — no duplicate transmission on retries.
- **PO-015** — Failed email/API/cXML transmissions must be visible, retryable, and auditable without duplicating the PO.
- **EML-002 / EML-006** — delivery results recorded; failures visible *(as cited by the page spec)*.

## Design

`po_transmission` (RLS): `id`, `po_id`, `version`, `contact_id`, `recipient_email`,
`template_version`, `outbox_id`, `provider_message_id`, `result` CHECK in `not_sent`, `queued`,
`sent`, `delivered`, `bounced`, `complained`, `failed` and `address_suppressed`, `result_at`,
`attempt int`, `created_by`, `created_at`. **One row per (PO, version, recipient, attempt).**

- **`transmission.enqueue(conn, scope, po_id, version)`** (the hook E13-S05 and E13-S06 call in their transaction): for each recipient stored on the PO, insert a row and an outbox email `add_email(..., idempotency_key=f"po:{id}:v{version}:{contact_id}:{attempt}")` with the email summary text and the version's PDF (E13-S08) as the **only** attachment. A suppressed address (E13-S10's list) → row `address_suppressed`, no outbox email, never retried silently.
- The **outbox dispatcher** sends it (no send from a handler). The provider result updates `result`, `provider_message_id` and `result_at`. Webhook results (`delivered`, `bounced`, `complained`) arrive through E13-S10.
- **Resend** (`POST /purchase-orders/{id}/transmissions/resend`, Idempotency-Key, `purchase_order.issue`): sends **the same version** to the current recipients as `attempt + 1`. It commits nothing (assert no ledger entry) and never changes the PO status. Refused (409) while a send of that version to that contact is `queued`.
- **Change recipient** (`PUT /purchase-orders/{id}/recipients`, `purchase_order.issue`): choose other contacts of the vendor (E11-S02). Audited.
- `GET /purchase-orders/{id}/transmissions`: rows newest first, with the recipient, time, version, template, provider id and result.
- **Business monitor (OBS-004):** expose the count of `failed`, `bounced` and `address_suppressed` transmissions in the last 7 days per org, the way the reconcile job exposes drift.

## Acceptance

- [ ] Issue enqueues one outbox email per recipient in the issue transaction (rolled-back issue → no outbox row).
- [ ] Dispatcher retry after a transient failure sends once (provider called once per idempotency key; fake provider).
- [ ] A transmission test per result: queued → sent → delivered; bounced; complained; suppressed (no send).
- [ ] Resend creates attempt 2 for the same version; no ledger entry; no status change; resend while queued → 409.
- [ ] The email has exactly one attachment (`application/pdf`) and no HTML attachment (assert on the outbox payload).
- [ ] No send from a request handler (the outbox assertion).
- [ ] TEN-010 and authorization; `flo check` green.

## Plants

1. Send directly in `enqueue`: the rolled-back-issue test sends an email and fails.
2. Drop the attempt from the idempotency key: resend sends nothing and the test fails.
