## Requirements (inlined)

- **SRC-011** — Evaluators must be able to score qualitative and quantitative criteria with comments and conflicts-of-interest declarations where configured.
- **SRC-013** — An award must record … evaluator results … *(this story stores them; E12-S08 attaches them).*

## Design

Every new table carries `org_id` and composite tenant FKs `(org_id, rfq_id) → rfq (org_id, id)`, as
`migrations/20261022_0074_rfq_comparison.py` does, with the RLS block above keyed on `org_id`.

**Criteria** (`rfq_criterion`): `id uuid` PK, `org_id`, `rfq_id`, `position`, `name` (1..120), `weight int` (1..100);
**Organisation default (decision 222):** a new setting `sourcing.default_criteria` in
`modules/org/settings.py` (org level, `unit_id` NULL; set through the existing org settings API,
`set_setting`, audited as `org_setting.set`). **Org only:** `modules/org/service.py::set_setting`
refuses this key with a non-NULL `unit_id` (422 `validation_failed`), since a value validator never
sees the scope. Value: a list of `{name, weight}`, 1..20 items, names
1..120 and unique, weights 1..100 totalling 100; the validator rejects anything else with 422.
No default set means no prefill. `GET /evaluation` on a **draft** RFQ with no saved criteria returns
the default as `criteria` with `"source": "default"` (nothing written); once the owner saves
(`PUT /criteria`) the RFQ has its own rows and `"source": "rfq"`. A later change to the default never
alters an RFQ's saved criteria. No copy at RFQ creation, so E12-S03 is untouched by this.

The weights on one RFQ total 100, checked at publish (E12-S03 gains the check: 422 `weights_total`
when criteria exist and do not total 100). Edited only while the RFQ is `draft`.

**Evaluators** (`rfq_evaluator`): `org_id`, `rfq_id`, `identity_id`, `added_by`; unique `(org_id, rfq_id, identity_id)`. Added by the RFQ owner
(`rfq.edit`) at any status before `awarded`. An evaluator needs `rfq.view` on the unit.

**Declaration first** (`rfq_evaluator_declaration`): `org_id`, `rfq_id`, `identity_id`, `conflict bool`,
`detail text` (required when conflict), `declared_at`. Append-only. **No score can be written and no
bid content is returned to an evaluator until they declare.** A declared conflict **recuses** them:
their evaluation endpoints return 409 `recused`, and the RFQ owner sees the declaration.

**Scores** (`rfq_score`): `org_id`, `rfq_id`, `identity_id` (FK to `rfq_evaluator (org_id, rfq_id, identity_id)`),
`vendor_id`, `criterion_id` (FK to `rfq_criterion.id`), `score int` (0..10),
`comment text`, `status` CHECK in `draft` and `submitted`. Submit is all or nothing per evaluator
(every criterion × every vendor in the comparison's set: the current submitted bid per vendor,
`comparison.py::_current_bids`, drafts and superseded bids excluded), and immutable after submit.

**Sealed:** before the deadline, scoring is closed (409 `scoring_not_open`) and nothing is returned
(E12-S05's `visible`). After the deadline, an evaluator sees **only their own** scores until every
non-recused evaluator has submitted; then every evaluator and `rfq.approve` holders see all of them
(sourcing.md §2 "Scores are sealed from everyone but their author until the deadline", read with
decision 118). The weighted score per vendor = Σ(mean score per criterion × weight / 100), as
`Decimal` quantized to `0.01` with `ROUND_HALF_UP` (as `comparison.py` and `vendors/scoring.py` do).
It is fed into E12-S06's `score` field (`comparison.py`, today `score=None  # E12-S07`) via
`evaluation.weighted(rfq_id)` in the same module, `None` until every non-recused evaluator has submitted.

## API (`/api/v1/sourcing/rfqs/{id}/evaluation`)

`PUT /criteria` (owner, draft), `POST/DELETE /evaluators`, `POST /declaration`, `GET /` (the
caller's view by the rules above), `PUT /scores` (draft save), and `POST /scores/submit`. All writes
take Idempotency-Key and are audited.

## Acceptance

- [ ] Default prefill: with `sourcing.default_criteria` set, a draft RFQ with no criteria returns them with `source: default` and writes no row; after `PUT /criteria` it returns its own rows with `source: rfq`; changing the default afterwards leaves those rows unchanged. A default whose weights do not total 100 → 422 from the settings API.
- [ ] An evaluator who has not declared → 409 on score routes and receives no bid content.
- [ ] Conflict → recused (409 `recused`), and the owner sees the declaration.
- [ ] Before the deadline → `scoring_not_open`; no score data in any response (reuse the sealed assertion).
- [ ] Visibility: an evaluator sees only their own scores until all have submitted, then everyone's.
- [ ] The weighted score matches a hand-computed `Decimal`, and the comparison shows it once available.
- [ ] Submitted scores are immutable (a trigger or service guard; a raw UPDATE raises if a trigger).
- [ ] TEN-010 and authorization per role (a non-evaluator with `rfq.view` cannot score).
- [ ] `flo check` green.

## Plants

1. Allow scoring before the declaration: the declaration test fails.
2. Show other evaluators' scores early: the visibility test fails.
3. Accept a default whose weights total 90: the settings 422 test fails.

## Blocked

Pre-flight 25 Oct 2026 (codex, fail 9). Two decisions are the operator's; authoring waits on them.

1. **Authorization — declaration first vs the comparison route.** "No bid content is returned to an
   evaluator until they declare" conflicts with `GET /rfqs/{id}/comparison` (`api/sourcing.py:424`),
   which returns bid content to any `rfq.view` holder after the deadline. Does an undeclared (or
   recused) evaluator lose comparison access, while other `rfq.view` holders keep it? Or does the
   rule cover only the evaluation endpoints?
2. **State — removing an evaluator who has scores.** Evaluators may be removed until `awarded`;
   submitted scores are immutable and completion counts every non-recused evaluator. Removing one
   with draft or submitted scores: refused (409), allowed with scores kept in results, or allowed
   with scores dropped from completion and results?
