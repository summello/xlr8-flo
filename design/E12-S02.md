## Requirements (inlined)

- **SRC-002** — An RFQ must contain items/services, quantities, specifications, delivery requirements, response deadline, terms, attachments, and invited vendors.
- **VEN-012** — A vendor that is inactive, suspended, expired, or not approved must not be invited or selected unless policy explicitly permits an authorized override.
- **VEN-013** — Vendor changes must not rewrite the vendor snapshot stored on historical RFQs, awards, or POs.

## Design (all edits only while the RFQ is `draft`; otherwise 409 `not_draft`)

**Content** on `rfq`: `specifications text` (≤ 20,000), `delivery_location text`,
`delivery_terms text` (Incoterm or free text), `terms text`, `deadline_at timestamptz NOT NULL`
(future, at least 1 hour ahead at publish), `deadline_tz text` (IANA; default the org time zone,
E12-S19 #9). Per line: `specification text NULL`.

**Compliance requirements** (decision 119): `rfq_requirement`: `id`, `rfq_id`, `position`, `text`
(1..500), `waived bool`, `waived_by`, `waived_reason`. Seed them from a template: a master kind
`rfq_requirement_template` whose attribute `requirements` is a list of strings
(`modules/org/master_kinds.py`). **Waiving** needs `rfq.approve` on the RFQ's unit and a reason, and
is audited.

**Attachments:** register `rfq` with collaboration's `register_document_access` (read `rfq.view`,
write `rfq.edit`). A per-attachment `vendor_visible bool` (default **false**, "Internal") lives in
`rfq_attachment_visibility(rfq_id, attachment_id, vendor_visible)`. Only `vendor_visible` files
with scan status clean are sent at publish (E12-S03).

**Invitations:** `rfq_invitee`: `rfq_id`, `vendor_id`, `contact_ids uuid[]` (the vendor's document
recipients from E11-S02 `document_recipients` at the time of invitation; editable to other contacts
of that vendor), a `vendor_snapshot jsonb` (trading name, legal name, number, status and the
eligibility result at invitation, VEN-013), `override_reason text NULL`, `overridden_by`, and
`invited_by` / `invited_at`. `UNIQUE (rfq_id, vendor_id)`.

- **Cap:** at most **5** invitees per RFQ. The sixth → 409 `invitee_limit` ("Up to 5 vendors per RFQ. Remove one to invite <vendor>."), **even with an override**. Enforce the count under `SELECT … FOR UPDATE` on the `rfq` row.
- **Eligibility** comes from `vendors.service.eligibility([vendor_id], rfq.unit_id, today)` (E11-S11) and nowhere else. Not eligible → 409 `vendor_not_eligible` with the reasons, unless the body carries `override_reason` **and** the caller holds `rfq.approve` on the RFQ's unit (decision 146). The override is stored and audited (`rfq.invitee.override`). Separation of duty on the override is allowed (E12-S19 #7).
- A vendor with no document recipients → 409 `no_recipient`, with "Add a Service and Delivery contact who receives RFQs".

## API (`/api/v1/sourcing/rfqs/{id}`)

`PATCH` (content), `PUT /requirements` (replace the list), `POST /requirements/from-template`,
`POST /requirements/{rid}/waive`, `PUT /attachments/{aid}/visibility`,
`POST /invitees` / `DELETE /invitees/{vendor_id}` / `PATCH /invitees/{vendor_id}` (contacts).
All take Idempotency-Key and are audited (`rfq.update`, `rfq.requirement.*`, `rfq.invitee.*`).
`GET /sourcing/rfqs/{id}` grows the content, requirements, attachments with visibility, and invitees
with snapshot and live eligibility.

## Acceptance

- [ ] Migration up, down and data preservation.
- [ ] Cap: five accepted; sixth → 409, also with an override; **concurrency** — two invitations for the fifth slot at once → exactly one succeeds.
- [ ] Ineligible vendor: refused without override; refused with override but without `rfq.approve` on the unit; accepted with both, audited.
- [ ] The snapshot does not change when the vendor is renamed afterwards (VEN-013).
- [ ] Waive needs `rfq.approve` and a reason.
- [ ] Attachment visibility defaults to internal.
- [ ] Non-draft edits → 409.
- [ ] TEN-010 on every route (foreign vendor id → 404/422, never invited). An authorization test per role, including that a hidden `Override` is refused by the server.
- [ ] `flo check` green.

## Plants

1. Count invitees before taking the RFQ lock: the concurrency test sees six.
2. Call the eligibility rule with the vendor's own unit instead of the RFQ's: the restricted-vendor test fails.
