## Requirements (inlined)

- **PO-014** — PDFs reproducible from the snapshot and template version.
- **PO-017 / EML-005** — internal-only content never included; covered by an automated test.

## Design

`/purchase-orders/:id/vendor-copy` (and a sheet on the detail): the email summary as the supplier sees
it, the PDF (an embedded viewer of `GET …/versions/{v}/pdf`), and a list **"Never sent to the
vendor"** (chartfields, budget, internal notes, uploaded files), built from the server's vendor-copy
response, not hardcoded.
