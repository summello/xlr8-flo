Links, Folds and Required Fields

Added after the approval screens. A hyperlink is coloured with the focus colour, underlined, and on hover the underline thickens and lifts and the arrow moves 3px (120ms, transform only). A fold hides audit detail that most people do not need; its chevron turns a quarter. A required field carries a Required tag in its label, a rail on the start edge of the empty control, and turns to Done with a tick the moment it has a value. All of this reads the control's own required state, so a new field needs the attribute and a tag and nothing else; the action bar says whether any required control is still empty. The submit button stays enabled: an incomplete form is refused with the error summary (DECISIONS 45).

Comparison Matrix, Award Basis and Cost Bars

Added with the Bid Comparison page. Decide in layers: a verdict strip, then a side-by-side matrix, then a sheet per vendor. The matrix is rows of measures and one column per vendor (up to 5), with Show Only Differences on by default. Colour has three jobs only, each with an icon and words: a win (green tint, Lowest, Fastest, Highest Score), worth a look (amber: partial, not comparable, not quoted) and a failure (red: misses the needed-by date, three or more unmet requirements). One or two unmet requirements are words and an icon, no tint. A tinted cell holds its own label, so a label can never sit on top of another box. Detail groups (price, tax and freight; terms) start closed, with a row count, so the first screen stays short. Below 1000px of content width two vendors are shown and a picker changes them. An award basis is a pair of radio cards that each show their own result. A cost bar is price, tax and freight in a fixed order with the total beside it and a dashed scope line; its accessible table is the Table tab.
